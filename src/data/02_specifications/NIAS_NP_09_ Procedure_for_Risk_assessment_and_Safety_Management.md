# 3. NP09-01 Risk Assessment Procedure.docx

### 1. PURPOSE (Tujuan)
The purpose of this procedure is to provide a systematic approach to risk assessment, ensuring that risks associated with tasks are reduced to an acceptable level, personnel are competent in evaluating risks, assessments are reviewed periodically or when changes occur, and compliance with applicable legislative requirements is maintained.

*(Tujuan dari prosedur ini adalah untuk menetapkan pendekatan sistematis dalam penilaian risiko, memastikan bahwa risiko yang terkait dengan tugas dikurangi hingga tingkat yang dapat diterima, personel kompeten dalam mengevaluasi risiko, penilaian ditinjau secara berkala atau ketika terjadi perubahan, serta kepatuhan terhadap persyaratan peraturan yang berlaku tetap terjaga).*

### 2. SCOPE (Ruang Lingkup)
This procedure applies to the assessment, documentation, and review of occupational health and safety risks arising from work activities at production facilities, both onshore and offshore, including brown-field and green-field projects. It excludes major hazards or process safety issues such as loss of containment, fire, and explosion, which are addressed in separate procedures. The standards outlined herein may also serve as minimum performance requirements for contractors, including those providing services activities, and must be integrated with the Permit to Work (PTW) and Job Safety Analysis (JSA) systems to ensure hazard identification and effective controls.

*(Prosedur ini berlaku untuk penilaian, pendokumentasian, dan peninjauan risiko kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja yang timbul dari aktivitas di fasilitas produksi, baik di darat maupun lepas pantai, termasuk proyek brown-field dan green-field. Prosedur ini tidak mencakup bahaya besar atau isu keselamatan proses seperti kehilangan containment, kebakaran, dan ledakan, karena hal tersebut diatur dalam prosedur terpisah. Standar yang dijabarkan di sini juga dapat digunakan sebagai persyaratan kinerja minimum bagi kontraktor, termasuk penyedia jasa, serta wajib diintegrasikan dengan sistem Permit to Work (PTW) dan Job Safety Analysis (JSA) untuk memastikan identifikasi bahaya dan pengendalian yang efektif).*

### 5. HAZARD IDENTIFICATION (Identifikasi Bahaya)
When a Job is to be undertaken, the Originator / Responsible Person or the worker who will perform the work will identify if a Risk Assessment is required. The Risk Assessment Process Flowchart shows the various stages.

Using the Preliminary Risk Assessment Checklist (PRAC) as a guide he will identify any hazards present in the work activity and answer either Yes or No on the checklist, in Column 1. This is Stage 1.
- If ‘Yes’ is entered in Column 1, details of how the risk will be minimized must be entered in Column 2. These may include existing Risk Assessments, Chemicals/Materials Assessment, Job Step Plan, etc.
- In Column 3 the question ‘Is the residual risk ALARP (As Low As Reasonably Practicable)’ is answered. If there is any doubt that the action or procedures written in Column 2 do not satisfactorily reduce the potential risk to ALARP, then a No is entered into Column 3.
- Any ‘No’ Answers in Column 3 require that further Risk Assessment be carried out. Also the inclusion of multiple ‘generic’ Risk Assessments may indicate the need for a job specific risk assessment.
- The Responsible Person then reviews the completed PRAC with the Work Leader.

*(Apabila suatu pekerjaan akan dilaksanakan, Originator / Penanggung Jawab atau pekerja yang akan melakukan pekerjaan tersebut harus mengidentifikasi apakah diperlukan Risk Assessment. Alur Proses Risk Assessment menunjukkan tahapan-tahapan yang harus dilakukan. Dengan menggunakan Preliminary Risk Assessment Checklist (PRAC) sebagai panduan, individu tersebut akan mengidentifikasi potensi bahaya dalam aktivitas pekerjaan dan mengisi jawaban Ya atau Tidak pada Kolom 1. Tahapan ini disebut Stage 1. Apabila jawaban Ya dicantumkan pada Kolom 1, maka rincian mengenai cara risiko akan diminimalkan harus diisi pada Kolom 2. Hal ini dapat mencakup Risk Assessment yang sudah ada, Chemicals/Materials Assessment, Job Step Plan, dan lain-lain. Pada Kolom 3, pertanyaan “Apakah risiko residual sudah ALARP (As Low As Reasonably Practicable)?” harus dijawab. Jika terdapat keraguan bahwa tindakan atau prosedur yang tertulis pada Kolom 2 belum secara memadai menurunkan potensi risiko hingga ALARP, maka jawaban Tidak harus diisi pada Kolom 3. Setiap jawaban Tidak pada Kolom 3 mengharuskan dilakukannya Risk Assessment lebih lanjut. Selain itu, penggunaan banyak generic Risk Assessments dapat mengindikasikan perlunya Risk Assessment yang lebih spesifik terhadap pekerjaan tersebut. Setelah itu, Penanggung Jawab meninjau PRAC yang telah dilengkapi bersama Work Leader).*

### 6. RISK ASSESSMENT (Penilaian Risiko)
The Risk Assessment process for Job Safety Analysis (JSA) is a method to systematically examine a specific work assignment in order to identify the hazards, evaluate the risks and specify necessary mitigation measures.

#### 6.1 Further Risk Assessment Required (Diperlukan Penilaian Risiko Lebih Lanjut)
- For a job specific risk assessment that has a clear job step, a Job Safety Analysis methodology and guidance to fill out JSA Form shall be conducted at minimum.
- For a non-job specific risk assessment (i.e. to determine a high-level identification of hazard for an overall project scope), Hazid methodology (inductive), or other type of Hazid methodology (deductive), can be used.
- The Responsible Person will select a team comprising a minimum of two persons. This will include a Team Leader for the Risk Assessment, who must be Risk Assessment trained. The team must have the correct skills, information and knowledge to undertake an assessment.
- Risk Assessment is completed using the standard LNG NIAS GASIFIKASI system Job Safety Analysis Form. Where Risk Assessment identifies that the overall residual risk is low, then the PRAC and Risk Assessment sheet are reviewed by the Responsible Person; if they are satisfied then they will sign both the PRAC and the Risk Assessment.
- The Work Leader then takes them forward to the next stage, Work Permit application approval and follow LNG NIAS GASIFIKASI HSE Standard Permit to Work unless already approved as a non-permitted task.
- If the risk cannot be reduced to an acceptably low level despite reassessing the work and introducing alternative methods, then the Responsible Person must bring this to the attention of the Facility Responsible Person (e.g. OIMs) who will refer the issue to HSE Jakarta for guidance.

*(Untuk penilaian risiko spesifik terhadap suatu pekerjaan yang memiliki langkah kerja yang jelas, metodologi Job Safety Analysis (JSA) serta panduan pengisian Formulir JSA wajib dilakukan sebagai standar minimum. Untuk penilaian risiko yang tidak spesifik pada pekerjaan tertentu (misalnya untuk mengidentifikasi bahaya secara umum dalam lingkup proyek secara keseluruhan), metodologi Hazid (induktif), atau jenis metodologi Hazid lainnya (deduktif), dapat digunakan. Penanggung Jawab akan memilih tim yang terdiri dari minimal dua orang. Tim ini harus mencakup seorang Team Leader untuk Risk Assessment, yang wajib memiliki pelatihan Risk Assessment. Tim tersebut harus memiliki keterampilan, informasi, dan pengetahuan yang memadai untuk melaksanakan penilaian. Risk Assessment diselesaikan dengan menggunakan Formulir Job Safety Analysis standar system LNG NIAS GASIFIKASI. Apabila hasil Risk Assessment menunjukkan bahwa risiko residual secara keseluruhan rendah, maka PRAC dan lembar Risk Assessment akan ditinjau oleh Penanggung Jawab. Jika dinyatakan memadai, Penanggung Jawab akan menandatangani PRAC dan Risk Assessment tersebut. Selanjutnya, Work Leader membawa dokumen tersebut ke tahap berikutnya, yaitu persetujuan aplikasi Work Permit, dan mengikuti LNG NIAS GASIFIKASI HSE Standard Permit to Work, kecuali bila pekerjaan tersebut telah disetujui sebagai tugas non-permit. Apabila risiko tidak dapat diturunkan hingga tingkat yang dapat diterima meskipun telah dilakukan penilaian ulang dan penerapan metode alternatif, maka Penanggung Jawab wajib melaporkannya kepada Facility Responsible Person (misalnya OIM), yang selanjutnya akan meneruskan isu tersebut kepada HSE Jakarta untuk mendapatkan arahan lebih lanjut).*

#### 6.2 Risk Assessment Not Required (Tidak Diperlukan Penilaian Risiko)
If the completed PRAC indicates that a full Risk Assessment is not required the Responsible Person can sign the PRAC, this takes them forward to the next stage which is to determine whether a PTW is required, if ‘Yes’, he proceeds as under Work Permit Application as per HSE PTW Procedure.
If a PTW is not required, then the Responsible Person completes a PJSM with the Work Leader to ensure they understand their duties and responsibilities at the work site. The Work Leader then continues to the worksite to carry out a PJSM with their team. The Work Leader is responsible to ensure all personnel involved in the work understand the result of risk assessment.

*(Apabila PRAC yang telah diselesaikan menunjukkan bahwa Risk Assessment lengkap tidak diperlukan, maka Penanggung Jawab dapat menandatangani PRAC tersebut. Selanjutnya proses dilanjutkan ke tahap berikutnya, yaitu menentukan apakah Permit to Work (PTW) diperlukan. Jika Ya, maka proses dilanjutkan sesuai dengan Work Permit Application sebagaimana diatur dalam Prosedur HSE PTW. Apabila PTW tidak diperlukan, maka Penanggung Jawab melaksanakan Pre-Job Safety Meeting (PJSM) bersama Work Leader untuk memastikan bahwa mereka memahami tugas dan tanggung jawabnya di lokasi kerja. Setelah itu, Work Leader melanjutkan ke lokasi kerja untuk melaksanakan PJSM bersama timnya. Work Leader bertanggung jawab memastikan seluruh personel yang terlibat dalam pekerjaan memahami hasil dari Risk Assessment).*

#### 6.3 Work Permit Application (Aplikasi Izin Kerja)
After completing the PRAC and, if required, a Risk Assessment the Originator will raise a Work Permit, following the LNG NIAS GASIFIKASI Regas Permit to Work Procedure, and will attach the completed PRAC, Risk Assessment and any supporting documentation (see also HSE Standards Manual).
A pre-job safety meeting (PJSM) is held between the Responsible Person and the Work Leader to ensure proper briefing on these points, and to ensure full awareness of all aspects of the work and the requirements for completing all tasks in a safe and effective manner (Stage 6). The Work Permit and attachments will be submitted to the Permit Authoriser for approval (Stage 7).
Where the Work Permit Authoriser cannot accept the Risk Assessment measures or considers that all the hazards have not been fully covered, the Authoriser will discuss with the Originator / Responsible Person any amendments to the assessment. If necessary, the assessment process will be repeated from Stage 2.
Approved Work Permit applications will be signed by the Permit Authoriser and issued by the Permit Issuer to the Work Leader, where he assures himself that the Work Leader fully understands his responsibilities under the Permit to Work Procedure (Stage 8).

*(Setelah menyelesaikan PRAC dan, apabila diperlukan, Risk Assessment, Originator akan mengajukan Work Permit sesuai dengan Prosedur LNG NIAS GASIFIKASI Regas Permit to Work, dengan melampirkan PRAC yang telah diselesaikan, Risk Assessment, serta dokumen pendukung lainnya (lihat juga HSE Standards Manual). Sebelum pekerjaan dimulai, dilakukan Pre-Job Safety Meeting (PJSM) antara Penanggung Jawab dan Work Leader untuk memastikan penyampaian pengarahan yang tepat terkait poin-poin tersebut, serta memastikan pemahaman menyeluruh mengenai seluruh aspek pekerjaan dan persyaratan penyelesaian tugas secara aman dan efektif (Tahap 6). Work Permit beserta lampirannya kemudian diserahkan kepada Permit Authoriser untuk persetujuan (Tahap 7). Apabila Permit Authoriser tidak dapat menerima langkah-langkah Risk Assessment atau menilai bahwa seluruh potensi bahaya belum sepenuhnya dicakup, maka Authoriser akan mendiskusikan dengan Originator / Penanggung Jawab mengenai perubahan yang diperlukan pada assessment. Jika perlu, proses assessment akan diulang mulai dari Tahap 2. Aplikasi Work Permit yang telah disetujui akan ditandatangani oleh Permit Authoriser dan diterbitkan oleh Permit Issuer kepada Work Leader, dengan memastikan bahwa Work Leader sepenuhnya memahami tanggung jawabnya sesuai dengan Prosedur Permit to Work (Tahap 8)).*

#### 6.4 Workforce Briefing & Confirmation (Pengarahan & Konfirmasi Kerja)
A Pre-Job Safety Meeting (PJSM) will be conducted for all tasks. This may be formal, with topics discussed fully documented, or informal, where only the signature of attendees is recorded. The PJSM will take place at the worksite following the issue of the Work Permit.
The Work Leader will ensure that the work team members are fully conversant with the work scope, any Work Permit requirements, procedures, assessment issues and risk reduction measures. The basis of the discussion will be the work scope, how it is to be achieved safely, the workplace, the hazards and protective / control measures in place to protect the team (Stage 10). Every member of the team must understand all requirements; any new member joining must be briefed in the same way (Stage 11).
Following the PJSM discussion, the Work Leader enters his name, signature and activity start date on the PJSM sheet. Each member of the work team enters their name, signs, and dates the sheet to confirm they have been briefed and understand the hazards, risks, and controls.

*(Pre-Job Safety Meeting (PJSM) wajib dilaksanakan untuk setiap pekerjaan. PJSM dapat dilakukan secara formal, dengan seluruh topik diskusi didokumentasikan secara lengkap, atau secara informal, dimana hanya tanda tangan peserta yang dicatat. PJSM dilaksanakan di lokasi kerja setelah Work Permit diterbitkan. Work Leader bertanggung jawab untuk memastikan bahwa seluruh anggota tim kerja benar-benar memahami lingkup pekerjaan, persyaratan Work Permit, prosedur, hasil assessment, serta langkah-langkah pengendalian risiko. Dasar diskusi mencakup lingkup pekerjaan, cara pelaksanaannya secara aman, kondisi lokasi kerja, potensi bahaya, serta langkah-langkah perlindungan/pengendalian yang tersedia untuk melindungi tim (Tahap 10). Setiap anggota baru yang bergabung wajib diberikan pengarahan dengan cara yang sama (Tahap 11). Setelah diskusi PJSM selesai, Work Leader mencantumkan nama, tanda tangan, dan tanggal mulai aktivitas pada lembar PJSM. Setiap anggota tim kerja mencantumkan nama, tanda tangan, dan tanggal untuk mengonfirmasi bahwa mereka telah menerima pengarahan).*

#### 6.5 Risk Assessment Revalidation (Revalidasi Penilaian Risiko)
The PRAC/Risk Assessment must be revalidated whenever:
- There has been a break in continuity of the work for more than 24 hours,
- The Work Leader or Responsible Person has changed (e.g. at night shift handover),
- There has been a significant change to the method of work.
If the change in method is significant enough, it may require a new PRAC/Risk Assessment and Work Permit.

*(PRAC/Risk Assessment wajib divalidasi ulang apabila: terjadi jeda dalam kesinambungan pekerjaan lebih dari 24 jam; terjadi pergantian Work Leader atau Responsible Person (misalnya saat serah terima shift malam); atau terdapat perubahan signifikan pada metode kerja).*

#### 6.6 Non-Work Permit TED Tasks (Tugas TED Tanpa Izin Kerja)
Routine and non-Work Permit tasks must follow the same PRAC/Risk Assessment steps. Certain routine tasks (especially Operations duties) are risk assessed and reviewed every 12 months or when the task changes. For low-risk routine activity, a PRAC/Risk Assessment may not be required upon advice from HSE Advisor Jakarta.

*(Untuk memastikan pekerjaan rutin maupun pekerjaan lain yang tidak memerlukan Work Permit tetap dinilai dengan standar yang sama, Penanggung Jawab wajib memastikan bahwa pekerjaan tersebut mengikuti tahapan yang sama. Pekerjaan rutin tertentu, khususnya tugas Operasi, wajib dilakukan Risk Assessment dan ditinjau setiap 12 bulan atau apabila terjadi perubahan. Untuk pekerjaan rutin dengan tingkat risiko rendah, PRAC/Risk Assessment dapat tidak diperlukan atas arahan HSE Advisor Jakarta).*

#### 6.7 Training, Review, Post Job Review & Record Retention
- **Training (Pelatihan):** Training will be carried out for all personnel likely to form part of a Risk Assessment team. Refer to HSE Training Matrix Procedure.
- **Review (Tinjauan):** All PRAC/Risk Assessments of routine non-Work Permitted tasks are treated as ‘live’ documents and reviewed every 12 months or sooner if significant changes occur. For Risk Assessments attached to Work Permits, the Review Team verifies contents before issuance.
- **Post Job Review (Tinjauan Pasca Pekerjaan):** Lessons learned must be captured and fed back into task Risk Assessments. Work Leader determines feedback level (de-briefing or notes).
- **Record Retention (Penyimpanan Catatan):** PRAC copy kept for at least 1 year and archived with PTW for 5 years. Risk Assessments stored electronically in discreet asset/function folders.

*(Pelatihan diberikan kepada seluruh personel. Dokumen PRAC/Risk Assessment rutin dianggap 'aktif' dan ditinjau setiap 12 bulan. Pelajaran dari pelaksanaan pekerjaan wajib didokumentasikan. Salinan PRAC disimpan 1 tahun dan diarsipkan bersama PTW selama 5 tahun).*

### 7. OTHER RISK ASSESSMENT GUIDANCE (Penilaian Risiko Lainnya)
- **Hazardous Chemical or Material:** Chemical Task Assessments require a qualified person and use Appendix E form. Reviewed every 12 months.
- **Manual Handling:** Significant, repetitive manual handling (e.g. moving helicopter baggage) requires a separate assessment.
- **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** Assessed in PRAC/JSA. Complex PPE requires specialist involvement. Respiratory Protective Equipment (RPE) specified based on chemical risk.
- **Noise:** Comprehensive Noise Assessment required when noise levels reach 85 dB(A) for 8-hour exposure or 83 dB(A) for 12-hour exposure. Must be conducted by trained personnel with a written report.
- **Computer Workstations:** Self-assessment required if continuous display screen work exceeds 1 hour per day or workstation changes occur. Guided by IS department.
- **Provision and Use of Work Equipment:** Assessed using Appendix G form for equipment posing significant risk. Large equipment (lathes, drills) requires individual forms. Reviewed yearly. Personal hand tools inspected per HSE Standards Manual.
- **Lead:** Lead exposure is NOT covered by this procedure; specialist advice from HSE Jakarta is mandatory.
- **Confined Space Entry:** Evaluated using the 'Confined Space Risk Model' (Appendix F).
- **Lifting Operations:** Requires PRAC/Risk Assessment Form for all lifting tasks and equipment.

### 8. COMPLIANCE (Kepatuhan)
This procedure complies with Indonesian K3/Migas regulations and international standards (ISO 45001). All jobs must go through JSA and Permit to Work.
- Any actual or suspected breach shall be reported to the Compliance Committee, who will report to the President Director.
- Non-compliance leads to disciplinary action, sanctions under Code of Conduct (CoC) and Company Regulation.
- Any exception requires approval from the President Director, Operation Director, and Technical Director.

# 4. NP09-02 Risk Management Procedure.docx

### 1. PURPOSE
To establish a systematic process for identifying, assessing, controlling, and monitoring health, safety, environmental, and operational risks associated with all activities at the mini-LNG regasification facility. The goal is to eliminate or reduce risks to a level that is **As Low As Reasonably Practicable (ALARP)**.

### 2. RISK MANAGEMENT CYCLE
The procedure follows a continuous cycle: **Identify → Assess → Control → Monitor & Review**

### 3. PHASE 1: RISK ASSESSMENT

#### Step 1: Hazard Identification Methods
- **Process Hazard Analysis (PHA):** Formal HAZOP (Hazard and Operability Study) for the entire process.
- **Job Safety Analysis (JSA):** For routine tasks (e.g., truck unloading, filter maintenance).
- **Management of Change (MOC):** For all modifications to equipment, processes, or high-level personnel.
- **Workplace Inspections & Audits:** Daily walks, formal weekly inspections.
- **Incident & Near-Miss Investigation:** To identify underlying causes.
- **Hazard Reporting:** Open culture reporting.

#### Common LNG Hazards
1. **Cryogenic Leaks:** LNG release causing frostbite, material embrittlement, pool formation.
2. **Flammable Gas Releases:** Explosive vapor clouds (with potential Rapid Phase Transition - RPT over water).
3. **Fire & Explosion:** Jet fires, pool fires, flash fires, vapor cloud explosions (VCE).
4. **Overpressure/Underpressure:** Storage tanks and process piping.
5. **Asphyxiation:** Nitrogen purging or oxygen displacement by natural gas.
6. **Physical Hazards:** High-pressure systems, noise, rotating equipment.

#### Step 2: Risk Analysis & Evaluation (The Risk Matrix)

##### Severity Rating Scale (Consequence)
| Score | Personnel Safety & Health | Environmental Impact | Asset/Financial Impact | Reputation Impact |
| ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| **5 - Catastrophic** | Fatality or permanent total disability. | Major release with severe, long-term environmental damage. Regulatory shutdown. | > $1,000,000; Total loss of facility. | National media attention, major legal charges, loss of license to operate. |
| **4 - Major** | Severe injury, permanent partial disability. | Significant release requiring external cleanup and major regulatory reporting. | $100,000 - $1,000,000; Major equipment loss. | Regional media attention, significant regulatory fines. |
| **3 - Moderate** | Lost Time Injury (LTI). | Localized containment failure, reportable release to authorities. | $10,000 - $100,000; Significant repair needed. | Local media attention, complaints from local community. |
| **2 - Minor** | Medical Treatment Case (MTC) or First Aid. | Minor spill contained on-site, minor cleanup. | < $10,000; Minor equipment damage. | Internal investigation, no external attention. |
| **1 - Negligible** | No injury or minor first aid. | No impact or negligible spill. | Negligible repair cost. | No impact. |

##### Likelihood Rating Scale (Probability)
| Score | Descriptor | Frequency Definition | Example for Mini-LNG |
| ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| **5 - Almost Certain** | Expected to occur | Once per year or more | Minor LNG spill during routine truck unloading. |
| **4 - Likely** | Will probably occur | Once every 1-3 years | Failure of a pump seal causing a localized leak. |
| **3 - Possible** | Might occur at some time | Once every 3-10 years | Overpressure event contained by a safety valve. |
| **2 - Unlikely** | Could happen but rare | Once in 10-30 years | Major leak from a primary process line. |
| **1 - Rare** | May occur only in exceptional circumstances | Less than once in 30 years | Vapor Cloud Explosion (VCE) with blast effects. |

##### Risk Matrix & Priority Scoring (Risk Score = Severity x Likelihood)
| Likelihood / Severity | 1 (Negligible) | 2 (Minor) | 3 (Moderate) | 4 (Major) | 5 (Catastrophic) |
| ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| **5 (Almost Certain)** | 5 (Medium) | **10 (High)** | **15 (Extreme)** | **20 (Extreme)** | **25 (Extreme)** |
| **4 (Likely)** | 4 (Low) | 8 (Medium) | **12 (High)** | **16 (Extreme)** | **20 (Extreme)** |
| **3 (Possible)** | 3 (Low) | 6 (Medium) | **9 (High)** | **12 (High)** | **15 (Extreme)** |
| **2 (Unlikely)** | 2 (Low) | 4 (Low) | 6 (Medium) | **8 (High)** | **10 (High)** |
| **1 (Rare)** | 1 (Low) | 2 (Low) | 3 (Low) | 4 (Low) | 5 (Medium) |

##### Risk Acceptance Criteria & Required Actions
- **EXTREME (15 - 25): UNACCEPTABLE.** Immediate action required to mitigate risk. Operations MUST NOT commence or must cease until adequate controls are implemented. Requires senior management intervention and a detailed risk reduction plan.
- **HIGH (8 - 12): UNDESIRABLE.** Senior management attention needed. Specific mitigation plans with defined responsibilities and timelines must be established. Requires temporary controls until permanent solutions are in place.
- **MEDIUM (4 - 6): TOLERABLE.** Must be managed by specific procedures and controls. Responsibility for monitoring must be assigned. Efforts to reduce risk further should be evaluated based on cost/benefit (ALARP principle).
- **LOW (1 - 3): ACCEPTABLE.** Managed by routine procedures. No additional controls are typically required, but monitoring is necessary to ensure the risk level does not increase.

### 4. PHASE 2: RISK CONTROL & MITIGATION
Hierarchy of Controls (Most to Least Effective):
1. **Elimination:** Physically remove the hazard (e.g. design modular skids instead of complex piping).
2. **Substitution:** Replace hazard with less hazardous alternative (e.g. less hazardous cleaning chemicals).
3. **Engineering Controls:** Isolate people from hazard (e.g. PSVs, gas detection, automatic ESD, firewalls, blast walls, double-walled pipes, dikes).
4. **Administrative Controls:** Change the way people work (e.g. PTW, LOTO, SOPs, emergency drills, training).
5. **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** Protect worker with personal equipment (e.g. FR clothing, cryogenic gloves, safety glasses, SCBA). *Note: Last line of defense.*

- **Risk Treatment Plan:** Formally created for High and Extreme risks in Risk Log and Action Plan.

### 5. PHASE 3: MONITORING, REVIEW, AND CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT
- **Performance Monitoring:**
  - *Leading Indicators:* Gas detector alarms responded to, PTW audits completed, training compliance %, safety observations reported.
  - *Lagging Indicators:* TRIR, Lost Time Injuries, releases of product.
- **Formal Review:** Risk Register reviewed **annually** by HSE & Operations Manager. Re-done after major incident or significant MOC.
- **Audit & Verification:** Internal and external audits.

### 6. EXAMPLE APPLICATION: LNG TRUCK UNLOADING
- **Hazard:** Rupture of unloading arm -> major LNG spill and vapor cloud.
- **Initial Risk:** Severity 5 (Catastrophic) x Likelihood 2 (Unlikely) = **10 (HIGH)**.
- **Controls & Mitigation:** ESD single button, double-walled hose, impoundment basin, strict PTW, JSA.
- **Residual Risk:** Severity 5 x Likelihood 1 (Rare) = **5 (MEDIUM / ALARP)**.

### 7. IDENTIFYING NEW RISK AND NEAR-MISS
- **On-site Reporting:** Personnel report new risks/near-misses orally or in writing to Safety & Environmental Officer. SSHQE officer records and submits to Site Manager. Site Manager reviews and draws **Form Incident Accident Investigation Report (NP11-01)**.
- **SYSMAN Addition:** SYSMAN team leader reviews NP11-01 and identifies items as objects for risk assessment.

### PPE Definitions & Notes
- **FR (Flame Resistant) Clothing / Pakaian FR (Tahan Api):** Must cover torso, arms, and legs. Cotton or inherent FR material. **NO synthetic materials (nylon, polyester) in process areas.**
- **Cryogenic Gloves / Sarung Tangan Kriogenik:** Loose-fitting, designed to protect against temperatures down to -162°C. They are **NOT** liquid-proof. Designed to shed cryogenic liquid quickly without absorbing it.
- **Face Shield / Pelindung Wajah:** Must be used **IN ADDITION TO** safety glasses, not as a replacement. Provides full-face splash protection.
- **Hearing Protection / Pelindung Pendengaran:** Required where 8-hour time-weighted average (TWA) sound level exceeds 85 dBA. Disposable earplugs or muff-style protectors acceptable.

# 6. App 03 Flow Chart Risk Assessment.docx

### Risk Assessment Flowchart Stages & Logical Flow
- **Stage 1 (TASK):** Originator/Responsible Person assesses hazard using the PRAC Process. *Is a Full Risk Assessment required?*
  - If **No**: Determine if Permit to Work (PTW) is required.
    - If PTW required (**Yes**): Proceed to Work Permit Application.
    - If PTW not required (**No**): **Stage 9** - Responsible Person Briefs Work Leader on Main Points written on PJSM form.
  - If **Yes**: Proceed to Stage 2.
- **Stage 2:** Responsible Person Selects a Suitable Assessment Team.
- **Stage 3:** Team Carries Out Risk Assessment of The Work Activities. *Can the activities be Undertaken with Acceptable Risk?*
  - If **Yes**: Proceed to Stage 6.
  - If **No**: Proceed to Stage 4.
- **Stage 4:** Responsible Person Reassesses the Activities, Procedure and Risks. *Can Activities be Modified to Reduce Risk to Acceptable Level?*
  - If **Yes**: Return to Stage 3.
  - If **No**: Proceed to Stage 5.
- **Stage 5:** Responsible Person Refers Issue to Facility Manager -> Refer to HSE Jakarta.
- **Stage 6:** Responsible Person Reviews Permit, Attaches PRAC/Risk Assessment and Briefs Work Leader on Main Points on PJSM.
- **Stage 7:** Authorizer Reviews Permit and Risk Assessment. *Authoriser Agrees Activities Can Proceed?*
  - If **Yes**: Proceed to Stage 8.
  - If **No**: Reassess from Stage 2/3.
- **Stage 8:** Permit and Risk Assessment Issued to Work Leader for Activity Implementation.
- **Stage 10:** Work Leader Briefs Workforce of Hazards and Risk Reduction Measures at PJSM. *Are Risks and Measures Understood by Workforce?*
  - If **Yes**: All Workforce Sign PJSM Sheet to Confirm the Risks and Appropriate Measures Have Been Explained and are Understood.
- **Stage 11:** **Work Activities can Proceed.** Post Job Review conducted.

| Activity | Hazard / Threat | Risk Description (Consequence) | Initial Risk (LxS=Rx) | Preventative Controls (Barriers) | Mitigating Controls (Recovery) | Residual Risk (LxS=Rx) | Responsible Party |
| ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| **Yard Layout & Design** | Poor layout -> congestion. | Vehicle collisions, blocked emergency routes, domino escalation. | 4x4=16 (High) | 1. **Inherently Safe Design:** One-way traffic, dedicated bays (min 15m spacing), separate pedestrian routes.<br>2. **Fire Engineering:** NFPA 59A / EN 1473 compliance.<br>3. **Simulation Review:** Vehicle movement simulation. | 1. **Emergency Access Routes:** Unobstructed fire/ambulance lanes.<br>2. **Remote Shutdown:** ESD activation from safe distance. | 2x3=6 (Medium) | Project Engineering / Facility Manager |
| **Drainage & Impoundment** | Inadequate spill containment. | LNG spill spreads -> vapor cloud travels to ignition sources -> explosion/fire. | 3x5=15 (High) | 1. **Sloped Surfaces & Trenches:** Direct spills to impoundment.<br>2. **Impoundment Sizing:** Basin holds 110% of largest tank capacity.<br>3. **Non-Combustible Materials:** Concrete / cryogenic compatible. | 1. **Vapor Dispersion Modeling:** Plume path modeling.<br>2. **Water Curtains:** Installed to disperse vapor clouds. | 2x3=6 (Medium) | Civil Engineer / HSE |
| **System & Procedure Development** | Lack of clear yard procedures. | Inconsistent practices, SIMOPS conflicts, incidents. | 4x4=16 (High) | 1. **Yard Management Plan:** Parking assignments, 10 km/h speed limit, max capacity.<br>2. **PTW System:** Mandatory PTW for hot work / confined space in yard.<br>3. **SIMOPS Review:** Matrix for overlapping tasks. | 1. **CCTV Monitoring:** Central control room oversight.<br>2. **Audits:** Scheduled compliance audits. | 2x2=4 (Low) | Operations Manager |

### Phase 2: During Yard Operations (Daily Management & Monitoring)
| Activity | Hazard / Threat | Risk Description (Consequence) | Initial Risk (LxS=Rx) | Preventative Controls (Barriers) | Mitigating Controls (Recovery) | Residual Risk (LxS=Rx) | Responsible Party |
| ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| **Tank Positioning & Parking** | Improper spacing / positioning. | Fire on one tank escalates to adjacent tanks; blocked valves. | 3x5=15 (High) | 1. **Bay Marking & Yard Master:** Ground markings; Yard Master assigns bays.<br>2. **Spacing Verification:** Ensure min 15m distance.<br>3. **Wheel Chocking:** Dual chocks for all parked tanks. | 1. **Fixed Deluge Systems:** Deluge / monitors to cool adjacent tanks.<br>2. **Emergency Towing Gear:** Ready to move tanks if safe. | 2x3=6 (Medium) | Yard Master / Shift Lead |
| **Continuous Monitoring** | **Pressure Build-Up (Boil-Off Gas).** | Tank overpressurization -> PRV release or tank rupture. | 4x5=20 (Extreme) | 1. **SCADA Alarm System:** Real-time tank pressure/temp monitoring with high-high alarms.<br>2. **Operator Rounds:** Physical checks every 2-4 hours for frost/leaks.<br>3. **Vapor Management:** Vapor recovery system or controlled venting. | 1. **Automatic ESD:** Auto isolate tank on high pressure.<br>2. **PRV & Flare:** PRVs vent to flare stack. | 3x3=9 (Medium) | Control Room Operator / Field Operator |
| **Simultaneous Operations (SIMOPS)** | Interaction of multiple hazards. | Hot work sparks ignite vapor from adjacent tank loading. | 3x5=15 (High) | 1. **SIMOPS Permit & Matrix:** Risk assessment and restricted combinations matrix.<br>2. **Separation:** Physical barriers or temporal separation.<br>3. **Gas Buffer Zones:** Extended gas monitoring around hot work. | 1. **Centralized Control:** Yard master coordinates all tasks.<br>2. **Area Classification:** Zone 1 / Zone 2 enforcement. | 2x3=6 (Medium) | Shift Lead / Permit Coordinator |
| **Vehicle & Pedestrian Movement** | Collision with tank or infrastructure. | Tank damage -> leak, injury, fatality. | 3x4=12 (High) | 1. **Traffic Management Plan:** 10 km/h speed limit, speed bumps, stop signs.<br>2. **Banksmen / Spotters:** Mandatory for vehicle reversing.<br>3. **Segregated Walkways:** Marked pedestrian pathways. | 1. **Bollards & Arresting Systems:** Protect skids/tanks.<br>2. **Vehicle PPE:** Spark-proof fittings & intrinsically safe radios. | 2x2=4 (Low) | All Personnel |

### Yard Management Core Standards
- **15-Meter Tank Spacing:** Minimum 15-meter clearance between parked ISO tanks.
- **110% Impoundment Basin:** Impoundment basin sized to hold 110% of the largest single ISO tank volume.
- **2 to 4 Hour Operator Rounds:** Physical inspection rounds conducted every 2 to 4 hours in addition to SCADA monitoring.
- **10 km/h Speed Limit:** Strictly enforced yard speed limit with mandatory reversing spotters/banksmen.
- **Sign-off:** TBM executed date, HSE Officer, TBM participant Position & Signature, Approved by Site Manager.
