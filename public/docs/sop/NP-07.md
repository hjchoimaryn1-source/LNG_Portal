## 1. The General {#np-07-1-1-the-general}

### 1.1 Purpose {#np-07-1-1-purpose}
The purpose of this procedure is to operate management system effectively, and to define Document control jobs identified by the Company (Nias Site).

### 1.2 Scope {#np-07-1-2-scope}
This procedure to be applied for identification, drawing, amendment, registration, distribution, familiarization, custody, abolition of the Document to be controlled by the Company (Nias Site).

### 1.3 Responsibility and Authority {#np-07-1-3-responsibility-authority}
* **Top Management**: Approval of Policy and Objective; Approval of Main Manual.
* **Management Representative (Head Office)**: Control for establishment, change of Policy and Objective; Control for creation and amendment of Main Manual; Approval of Process Manual; Approval of Procedure; Approval for regulations and guideline of the Company (Nias Site); Approval of Company (Nias Site)’s form; Approval of Company Circular; Approval of other system Document of the Company (Nias Site); Approval of the Document dispatch to outside.
* **SYSMAN Team Leader**: Working for identification, drawing, amendment, registration, distribution, familiarization, custody, abolition of Company (Nias Site)’s system Document; Assisting for the job relating to Document of Site Manager.
* **Team Leader**: Creation and amendment duty of system Document by order of Site Manager; Creation and amendment duty of system Document that SYSMAN leader request; Assisting for the job relating to Document of Site Manager.

### 1.4 Definition and Kind of Document {#np-07-1-4-definition-kind-of-document}
Document means diagrammed information, information supporting medium or meaningful data and there is various type of Document like as Paper, Magnetic, Computer disc, Photograph, Master sample, etc.

Kinds of Document are as followings:
1. **System Document**: Document describing the Company (Nias Site)’s regulations, job procedures, etc., in relation to management system of the Company (Nias Site) or recording the evidence or results of system operation.
   * Manual
   * Procedure
   * Regulation, Guide of the Company (Nias Site)
   * Company Circular
   * Forms
   * Poster, Extraneous matter
   * Record comes from the operation of management system
   * Internal communication like as job guidance comes from the operation of management system
2. **Out-sourced Document**: Document controlled by the Company (Nias Site), compiled by outside Company (Nias Site) for management system Company (Nias Site) operation.
   * Document relating to Agreement
   * Government regulations
   * ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 45001 coordination under control Head Office
   * Maker Instruction, Maker’s Information and Drawings
3. **Certificates**: An official Document issued by the Head Office or outside Company, proving particular facts, status, and qualification.
4. **Official Document dispatched to outside from the Company (Nias Site)**: Letter, information, report or data sending officially to outside Company (Nias Site) or other Company (Nias Site) by the name of the Company (Nias Site).
5. **Internal Official Document**: Letter, information, report or data to draw up for internal communication of Company (Nias Site), to communicate officially.

## 2. Dispatch & Receipt of Document {#np-07-2-1-dispatch-receipt-of-document}

### 2.1 General Regulation Relating to Document Dispatch & Receipt {#np-07-2-1-general-regulation-dispatch-receipt}
* Site Manager can assign person in charge for Document control and dispatch & receipt of Document.
* This Procedure shall apply to dispatch & receipt of all Document through post, fax, or electronic mail.
* The Document dispatching from the Company (Nias Site) shall be approved and dispatched by Site Manager. In case of authorized job, authorized person can dispatch it without approval in the name of authorized person.
* All Document dispatching from the On-Site shall be dispatched in the name of Site Manager after Site Manager’s approval.

### 2.2 Document Number System {#np-07-2-2-document-number-system}
* **System Documents requiring Document Numbers**: Manual, Procedure, Regulations, Guide, Extraneous matter, Poster, Certificates, Company Circular, Training materials, Records reported/submitted between Site and Head Office.
* **System Documents not requiring Document Numbers**: Records not necessary to report/submit to Head Office, Out-sourced Documents (Agreements, Government Regulations, Maker Instructions/Drawings).
* **Document Number Format**: `LNG-YYNNN` (e.g. `LNG-25001` where LNG = Abbreviation, 25 = Year 2025, 001 = Consecutive Number).
* **Company Circular Format**:
  * Accident Information: `CC01-YYNN` (e.g. `CC01-2501`)
  * Domestic/International Information: `CC02-YYNN` (e.g. `CC02-2501`)
  * Company Instruction: `CC03-YYNN` (e.g. `CC03-2501`)
  * Major Inspection Information: `CC04-YYNN` (e.g. `CC04-2501`)

### 2.3 Dispatch and Receipt Procedures {#np-07-2-3-dispatch-receipt-procedures}
* **Dispatch to External Party**: Principal to use "Business Letter Form". State Document number, receiver, sender, and attached descriptions. Registered in `Form NP07-19 Document Dispatch & Receipt Record Book`.
* **Dispatch List (NP07-18)**: Used when dispatching documents attached without descriptive text body.
* **Receipt Handling**: Classified by jobs and distributed to relevant teams. Documents received via Dispatch List returned with receiver's signature and date. Registered in `Form NP07-19`.

## 3. Control of System Document {#np-07-3-1-control-of-system-document}

### 3.1 Establishment, Amendment, Review, and Approval {#np-07-3-1-establishment-amendment-approval}
* **Triggers for Establishment/Amendment**: Policy/objective changes, new projects, domestic/international regulation updates, operational inefficiencies, or other identified needs.
* **Drawing & Reviewing**: Drafted in understandable language, reviewed by authorized personnel using `Form NP07-20 Confirmation for Registration/Distribution/Receipt of System Document`.
* **Approval**: Approved by designated authority (Management Representative / Site Manager). Signed on "I Contents & Approval" page and `Form NP07-20`.
* **Distribution**: Distributed with enforcement date and training instructions via `Dispatch List (NP07-18)`. Head Office SYSMAN provides 1 registered set per team and 2 CD copies onsite.
* **Effectuation**: Effective upon signature, fixed enforcement date, or receipt date.

### 3.2 Form Control Format {#np-07-3-2-form-control-format}
Form Numbering Structure: `Form-NP-AA-BB-C(D/E)/R/YYYY.MM.DD`
* `Form-NP`: Prefix
* `AA`: Document Number (e.g. `05`)
* `BB`: Serial Number (e.g. `01`)
* `C`: Distribution (`S` = Shore, `C` = Common, `N` = Nias) and Page Number
* `R`: Revision Number (e.g. `0`)
* `YYYY.MM.DD`: Enforcement Date (e.g. `2025.08.08`)

### 3.3 Record Control & Retention {#np-07-3-3-record-control-retention}
* All records filed according to `App 02 File Control List`.
* Storage and retention period reckoned from creation date (minimum 1 year).
* Destruction executed in January after prior report and confirmation by Site Manager.

## 8. Nonconformity, Correction and Corrective Action {#np-07-8-1-nonconformity-capa-procedure}

### 8.1 CAPA Workflow {#np-07-8-1-capa-workflow}
1. **Identification**: Nonconformities identified via inspections, audits, customer complaints, incident investigations, or monitoring data discrepancies.
2. **Corrective Action Request (CAR) Form**: Initiator submits CAR to HSE Officer detailing the finding.
3. **Action Tracking System (ATS)**: Action items approved by designated leader prior to posting in ATS. Every item requires a stated finding, target closure date, assigned responsible person, and clear statement of desired outcome.
4. **Field Verification**: Appointed auditor/verifier inspects field execution of corrective actions and records notes on the CAR form.
5. **Closure**: Status updated to "Closed/Completed" in Nonconformity Log once approved and signed by Site Manager.

## 9. SSHQE Management System (10 Elements & 53 Expectations) {#np-07-9-1-sshqe-management-system-elements}

### 9.1 Element Overview {#np-07-9-1-element-overview}
* **Element 1: Leadership & Culture** - Management sets vision, tracks SSHQE KPIs, conducts management walk-thrus, fosters open safety dialogue.
* **Element 2: Personnel & Training** - Competency assessment, training matrix, PTW training, health surveillance, alcohol & drugs policy.
* **Element 3: Contractors** - CSMS implementation, vendor pre-qualification, contractor SSHQE performance reviews.
* **Element 4: Risk Management** - Hazard identification, ALARP risk reduction, Risk Register, HAZOP/HAZID, QRA, hierarchy of controls (Elimination, Substitution, Engineering, Administrative, PPE).
* **Element 5: New Projects & Modifications** - Facility design/construction compliance, MOC, HAZOP, Pre-Start Up Review (PSUR).
* **Element 6: Operations** - Operating procedures, PTW system, critical shutdown/ESD/PSV testing, shift handover, SIMOPs procedures.
* **Element 7: Management of Change (MOC)** - System for managing temporary and permanent changes to operations, facilities, personnel, or legal requirements.
* **Element 8: Incident Investigation** - Reporting, investigating, root cause analysis, action item closure, lessons learned communication.
* **Element 9: Crisis & Continuity Management** - Emergency Response Plans (ERP), ERT drills, On-scene Commander assessment, Oil Spill Response Plan (OSRP), Business Continuity Plan (BCP).
* **Element 10: Assessment & Compliance** - Internal/external audits, management reviews, ISO certifications, compliance committee oversight.

## 11. Security Procedure {#np-07-11-1-security-procedure}

### 11.1 Security Zones {#np-07-11-1-security-zones}
* **Zone 1: General Area (Outside Perimeter)** - Anti-climb fencing (2.4m+ height), clear line-of-sight zones, warning signs.
* **Zone 2: Operations Area (Inside Main Gate)** - Single controlled access gate for vehicles/personnel. Facility ID required.
* **Zone 3: Restricted Area (Process Area, ISO Tank Area)** - Internal fencing, specific job authorization required.
* **Zone 4: Critical/Core Area (Control Room, Server Room)** - Highest access control (electronic cards, biometrics, PIN).
* **Zone 5: Administration & Control Building** - External doors/windows locked, visitor parking control.
* **Zone 6: Emergency & Safety Equipment Area** - Access to eyewash, fire extinguishers, mooring area.

### 11.2 Alert Escalation Matrix {#np-07-11-2-alert-escalation-matrix}

| Level | Classification | Description | Required Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | Warning (Low) | Unclassified unusual movement, perimeter sensor trip, suspicious vehicle. | CCTV validation; Supervisor alert; Increased local patrol frequency. |
| **Level 2** | Intrusion (Medium) | Confirmed unauthorized personnel inside perimeter; theft or vandalism in non-critical zones. | Internal response team deployment; Zone isolation; Mandatory POLRI notification. |
| **Level 3** | Threat to Life / Sabotage (Critical) | Confirmed armed intrusion, hostage situation, deliberate process sabotage, major civil disturbance. | Full facility lockdown; Emergency controlled shutdown (First Cut-Out); Activate Obvitnas protocol (POLRI/TNI engagement). |
