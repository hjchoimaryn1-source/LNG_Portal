## Chapter 1: ISO Tank Yard Management (Loaded & Empty) Procedure {#np-08-ch01-iso-tank-yard-management}

### 1. Purpose {#np-08-1-1-purpose}
The purpose of this procedure is to establish a standardized guideline for the safe, efficient, and compliant management of ISO tanks (full and empty) within the yard, including storage, handling, transportation, inspection, and emergency response.

### 2. Scope {#np-08-1-2-scope}
This procedure applies to all personnel, equipment, and activities involved in ISO tank management within the yard and during transport to/from the jetty, including receiving, storage, stacking, movement, loading/unloading, documentation, and emergency handling. This procedure applies to all personnel involved in the mobilization of ISO Tanks from Port Pelindo Gunung Sitoli Nias to/from the PLN PLTMG Nias 25 MW - Regasification Station, and vice versa.

### 3. Definitions {#np-08-1-3-definitions}
| Definition | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **LNG (Liquefied Natural Gas)** | Natural gas liquefied at a temperature of approximately –162 °C, with a flash point of 32 °C (90 °F), to facilitate safe transportation and storage. |
| **LNG ISO Tank Container** | An internationally standardized container designed to transport LNG in liquid form under cryogenic conditions. |
| **ISO Tank Yard** | Designated area for storage and management of ISO tanks. |
| **Stacking** | Arrangement of ISO tanks in one or more tiers within the yard. |

### 4. References {#np-08-1-4-references}
| Document Type | Document Number | Document Title |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| NFPA | NFPA 59A | Standard for the Production, Storage, and Handling of Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) |
| ISO | ISO 3691-2 | Safety requirements for transport equipment |
| ANSI | ANSI/ITSDF B56.1 | Safety standard for industrial trucks |
| IMDG Code | ISO 1496-3 / ADR | Agreement concerning the International Carriage of Dangerous Goods by Road |
| IMDG Code | Chapter 6.7 | Design, construction, inspection, and testing of portable tanks |

### 5. Role and Responsibilities {#np-08-1-5-role-and-responsibilities}
| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Site Manager (SM)** | Ensure all LNG ISO Tank mobilization activities comply with SOP, regulations, and HSE standards; approve Journey Management Plan and coordinate with relevant parties. |
| **HSE Officer (SO)** | Conduct hazard identification, risk assessment, verify safety controls, lead toolbox meetings prior to mobilization, monitor cryogenic PPE usage, and enforce safety procedures. |
| **Logistics Officer (LO)** | Arrange permits, ISO Tank certificates, and shipping requirements; coordinate with transporter, port authority, and receiving facility; submit ISO Tank movement reports. |
| **Cargo Logistic (CO)** | Manage planning, scheduling, and documentation for LNG ISO Tank cargo; ensure smooth transfer from yard to port and vice versa; coordinate with Stevedoring Team and Transporter. |
| **Stevedoring Team (ST)** | Execute LNG ISO Tank loading and unloading operations at the port; operate port handling equipment safely; coordinate with Cargo Logistics and Lifting Equipment Operator. |
| **Lifting Equipment Operator (LEO)** | Operate reach stacker/crane in compliance with standards; conduct daily pre-use inspections; ensure safe lifting, moving, and positioning of LNG ISO Tanks. |
| **Transporter / Driver** | Ensure vehicle roadworthiness via pre-trip inspection; drive per route and speed in Journey Management Plan (JMP); report abnormal conditions. |
| **Emergency Response Team (ERT)** | Standby for potential emergencies during mobilization; execute actions per Emergency Response Plan (ERP); report incidents and coordinate with local fire brigade and regulators. |

### 6. Procedure {#np-08-1-6-procedure}

#### 6.1. ISO Tank Incoming {#np-08-1-6-1-iso-tank-incoming}
Logistics conducts visual inspection with driver. Check Tank Condition Report and compare with physical condition.
Inspection includes:
- **General Inspection**: Ensure reach stacker and trailer are in good condition and checked per regulatory standards. Ensure reach stacker has sufficient capacity to lift LNG ISO tank. Verify twist locks function properly.
- **Pre-Use Inspection**: Performed by qualified Reach Stacker/Trailer Operator or Qualified Inspector (check tire pressure, fluid levels, brakes, lights, hydraulic system, spreader beam twist locks, LMI, audible alarms).
- **Monthly / Annual Inspection**: Performed by Qualified Inspector / Maintenance Teams for critical components.
- Document any discrepancies with photographs and report to Logistics Manager. Received ISO tanks are placed in yard zone according to status (FULL / EMPTY).

#### 6.2. Storage and Stacking in the Yard {#np-08-1-6-2-storage-and-stacking}
Safe Distances:
- Between full LNG ISO tanks: **minimum 3 meters (10 feet)**.
- From LNG ISO tank to non-hazardous ISO tank: **minimum 5 meters (16 feet)**.
- From fences or area boundaries: **minimum 7.5 meters (25 feet)**.
- From buildings, critical infrastructure, or electrical equipment: **minimum 15 meters (50 feet)**.

Stacking Rules:
- **Maximum 2 tiers**.
- Full LNG ISO tanks: **Bottom tier only (Tier 1)**.
- Empty ISO tanks: **Top tier allowed (Tier 2)**.
- Damaged, leaking, or abnormally pressurized ISO tanks must NOT be stacked.

Area Accessibility:
- Trailer lanes: minimum **6 meters** (two-way) or **4 meters** (one-way).
- Emergency lanes must remain completely free from obstructions.

#### 6.3. Transporting ISO Tank LNG Using Trailer {#np-08-1-6-3-transporting-iso-tank}
- Drivers must hold valid B3 hazardous transport certification.
- Verify chassis, brakes, lights, twist locks, and tire integrity.
- Verify documentation (permits, shipping papers, SDS, route clearance).
- Maintain steady speed, avoid sudden stops/sharp turns, stop periodically to inspect twist lock securing mechanisms.

#### 6.4. Lifting LNG ISO Tank Using Reach Stacker {#np-08-1-6-4-lifting-using-reach-stacker}
- Ensure yard surface is level, strong, and free of puddles or holes.
- Conduct pre-use inspection of reach stacker (brakes, hydraulics, reverse alarm, strobe light, horn, LMI).
- Conduct toolbox meeting (operator, spotter, marshal, yard supervisor).
- Lower spreader beam to hook corner casting; confirm twist lock is fully engaged before lifting.
- Maintain safe travel speed (**≤ 5 km/h**) and safe distance from personnel (**≥ 3 m**).

#### 6.5. ISO Tank Switching Preparation (Pre-Unloading) {#np-08-1-6-5-iso-tank-switching-prep}
- Apply FIFO (First In, First Out) for full ISO tanks unless technical/HSSE priority exists.
- Go/No-Go Criteria: Identity/documentation OK, physical frame/corner casting intact, no leaks/abnormal frost, pressure/temperature within design limits, product level sufficient.

#### 6.6. Backhauling Empty ISO Tanks {#np-08-1-6-6-backhauling-empty-iso-tanks}
- Verify shipping order/request for empty ISO tank delivery to jetty.
- Inspect physical frame, valves closed, protective caps installed, seals intact.
- Verify internal pressure is at safe near-atmospheric level. Hand over TCR, Delivery Note, Shipping Instruction.

#### 6.7. Inventory System {#np-08-1-6-7-inventory-system}
- Yard Inventory Board (manual/electronic) tracks real-time status.
- Minimum recorded fields: Tank Number, Status (FULL/EMPTY), Position Code (Block/Zone/Slot), Internal Pressure & Level, Physical Condition (OK/Not OK), Entry/Exit Dates, Movement Destination, Trailer & Driver Number.
- Daily Yard Walkdown conducted every morning by Yard Supervisor. Weekly reconciliation between manual, system, and physical stock.

## Chapter 2: Lifting LNG ISO Tank Procedure {#np-08-ch02-lifting-lng-iso-tank}

### 1. Purpose {#np-08-2-1-purpose}
To outline requirements ensuring all lifting activities of ISO Tanks from shore to ship and vice versa using ship crane and reach stacker are carried out safely, efficiently, and in line with international standards.

### 2. Scope {#np-08-2-2-scope}
Covers inspection, maintenance, and operation of ship cranes, reach stackers, and associated lifting gear at cargo dock facilities.

### 3. Definitions {#np-08-2-3-definitions}
| Term | Definition |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lifting Equipment** | Mechanical devices (cranes, reach stackers) used to lift, move, and position LNG ISO tanks. |
| **Lifting Gear** | Supporting accessories (slings, shackles, spreader beams) used with lifting equipment. |
| **Spreader Beam** | Device used to stabilize load during overhead lifting and keep slings perpendicular (90° angle). |
| **SWL (Safe Working Load)** | The maximum load the equipment can safely lift. |

### 4. Regulations, Roles, and Responsibilities {#np-08-2-4-regulations-roles-responsibilities}
- **Certifications**: Ship crane certified by ESDM Migas, KEMENAKER, or BKI Class.
- **Personnel Qualifications**: Crane Operator minimum CLASS II (PERMENAKER No. 8/2020), Rigger certified per PERMENAKER No. 8/2020.
- **Inspection Frequency**: Routine inspection every 6 months for slings (wire rope, chain, webbing) and annually for cranes, shackles, and spreader beams.

### 5. Lifting Execution & Safety Limits {#np-08-2-5-lifting-execution-limits}
- **Spreader Beam SWL**: Must be **≥ total load of filled ISO Tank**.
- **Sling Angle**: Angle between wire rope sling and spreader beam must **not exceed minimum 45°**. Number of wire rope slings = 4.
- **Crane Safety Limit**: Total lifted load (ISO tank + spreader beam + shackles + slings) must be **maximum 85% of SWL Crane** at the working radius.
- **Pre-lift Test**: Perform a **20–30 cm pre-lift test** to verify load balance and brake holding power before completing full vertical lift.
- **Personnel Rules**: No walking under suspended loads, no side loading on boom, Tag lines mandatory for load control.

### 1. Purpose & Objectives {#np-08-3-1-purpose-and-objectives}
Systematic process for converting LNG (−162°C) back into gaseous natural gas, expanding volume by **approximately 600 times** for pipeline distribution.

### 2. Operating Parameters {#np-08-3-2-operating-parameters}

| **PBU Supply Pressure** | 11.7 barg | PRV setting 0.75 MPa |

## Chapter 4: LNG ISO Tank Unloading Skid Operation Procedure {#np-08-ch04-unloading-skid-operation}

### 1. Purpose & Setup {#np-08-4-1-purpose-and-setup}
Unloading LNG from CIMC T75 ISO Tanks to the Regasification Unit via unloading skids using external PBUs and BOG system.
- **Skid Configuration**: 4 slots connected simultaneously.
  - **T-201 & T-202**: Dedicated LNG Liquid Supply to Regas Unit.
  - **T-203 & T-204**: Dedicated Pressure Build-Up (PBU) System.

### 2. Unloading & Switching Execution {#np-08-4-2-unloading-switching-execution}
- **Pre-Cooling**: Open liquid/vapor valves crack open to cool lines gradually until flexible hoses are completely covered with uniform frost icing.
- **PBU Gaseous Pressure**: PRV setpoint at **0.75 MPa (11.7 barg)**.
- **PBU Switching Threshold (T-203 -> T-204)**: Switch when scale weight drops to **15.151 ton** or level gauge reaches **220 mmH2O**.
- **Liquid Supply Switching Threshold (T-201 -> T-202)**: Switch when scale weight drops to **13.222 ton** (or 12.33 ton) or level gauge reaches **120 mmH2O** (or 50 mmH2O).
- **Safe Depressurization**: Depressurize empty ISO tank to Vent Stack (VT-101) until pressure reaches **0.4 MPa** (or **< 0.05 MPa** before hose disconnection).
- **Disconnection Sequence**: Bleed remaining NG, ensure 0.0 MPa on hose gauges, verify icing has completely melted, disconnect QCC couplings, and **remove grounding cable LAST**.

## Chapter 5: ISO Tank Management Procedure {#np-08-ch05-iso-tank-management}

### 1. Pre-Unloading Safety Controls {#np-08-5-1-pre-unloading-safety-controls}
- Toolbox Talk (TBT) mandatory before shift.
- Multi-point Gas Testing: LEL < 10%, O₂ 19.5%–23.5%.
- Safety radius established with cones and barricades.
- Grounding clamp attached and tested (**< 5 Ohm**).

### 2. Emergency Case Handling {#np-08-5-2-emergency-case-handling}
- **Minor LNG Leak**: Isolate connection, restrict area (25 m radius), cover leak with wet cloth to absorb cold and control evaporation, switch skid. Never spray water on liquid LNG.
- **Major LNG Leak / Rupture**: Trigger ESD button, isolate leaking tank, evacuate personnel (50–100 m radius), declare supply interruption if switching is impossible.
- **ISO Tank Overpressure**: Open pressure transfer line to another low-pressure tank to equalize. If PSV is popping, isolate popping PSV line via valve A-14.
- **LNG Spill on Ground**: Stop unloading on affected skid, cover spill with **dry sand** for natural evaporation, close drains to prevent run-off. **Do NOT spray water directly on liquid LNG**.

## Chapter 6: Flexible Hose & Quick Close Coupler Maintenance and Inspection Procedure {#np-08-ch06-flexible-hose-qcc-maintenance}

### 1. Inspection & Testing {#np-08-6-1-inspection-and-testing}
- Daily visual check before, during, and after unloading (check outer braid, ferrule, QCC body, latches, gasket).
- **Nitrogen Static Leak Test**: Conducted at **1.1 × MAWP** for 10–15 minutes.
- **Periodic Pressure Test**: Conducted at **1.5 × Working Pressure** per manufacturer spec.
- Acceptance criteria: Zero pressure drop, zero bubbles during leak test.

### 2. Storage Requirements {#np-08-6-2-storage-requirements}
- Dry hose and coupler thoroughly after use. Cap both ends with dust caps.
- Coil hose with bending diameter **≥ 10 × hose outer diameter (OD)**.
- Store on ventilated racks away from heat, UV, and chemicals.

## Chapter 7: Ambient Air Vaporizer Operating Procedure {#np-08-ch07-aav-operating-procedure}

### 1. System Setup {#np-08-7-1-aav-system-setup}
- **6 AAV Units**: VAP-101 through VAP-106.
- **Operating Configuration**: **4 Units Duty (Simultaneous Operation) / 2 Units Standby**.
- **Routing**: Train 1 (VAP-101/102 via XV-01), Train 2 (VAP-103/104 via XV-02), Train 3 (VAP-105/106).

### 2. Defrosting & Rotation Rules {#np-08-7-2-defrosting-rotation-rules}
- Monitor AAV parameters every 30 minutes.
- **Defrosting Threshold**: When aluminum fin frost/ice build-up **exceeds 70% of fin surface area**, switch to standby AAV unit.
- **Switching Procedure**: Open standby XV valve on DCS/SCADA, equalize flow, then close XV valve of frosted unit. Allow frosted unit to thaw naturally in ambient air. Check for structural cracks or deformation after thawing.

## Chapter 8: Environmental and Safety Precautions Ambient Air Vaporizer Procedure {#np-08-ch08-environmental-safety-precautions-aav}

### 1. Hazard Precautions {#np-08-8-1-hazard-precautions}
- **Cryogenic Hazard**: Wear face shield, cryogenic gloves, insulated coverall, safety boots.
- **Asphyxiation Risk**: Methane displaces oxygen. Maintain O₂ > 19.5%. **Never enter area with O₂ < 19.5% without Self-Contained Breathing Apparatus (SCBA)**.
- **GWP**: Methane GWP is ~25× CO₂. Venting must be directed to Vent Stack (VT-101) or flare.

### 2. Emergency Evacuation Radius {#np-08-8-2-emergency-evacuation-radius}
- Minor leak: Barricade **≥ 25 meters**.
- Major leak / rupture: Evacuate radius **50–100 meters**.

## Chapter 9: Gas Sales Metering Operating Procedure {#np-08-ch09-gas-sales-metering}

### 1. Configuration & Standards {#np-08-9-1-metering-configuration-standards}
- **Metering Skid M-101**: Dual Train configuration (**Run A - Duty / Run B - Standby**).
- **GC Analyzer**: Emerson 470XA Gas Chromatograph (measures GHV, Density, C1–C5 mol%, N₂, H₂S, Total Sulphur).
- **Standards**: AGA Report No. 3, 7, 8, 9; ISO 5167; OIML R140.

### 2. Line Switching & Calibration Sequence {#np-08-9-2-line-switching-calibration-sequence}
1. **Preparation**: Approved PTW, JSA, LOTO, Gas Test (<10% LEL), notification to Control Room and Offtaker (PLN).
2. **Line Switching**: Fully open Run B valves (MV-1B-IN, MV-1B-OUT). Gradually throttle Run A inlet (MV-1A-IN) until flow smoothly transfers to Run B. Close Run A outlet (MV-1A-OUT).
3. **Isolation & Depressurization**: Open drain BV-1A-DRN and vent BV-1A-VENT to depressurize Run A to **0 barg**. Apply LOTO on MV-1A-IN and MV-1A-OUT.
4. **Calibration**: Connect Prover/Master Meter to MV-PROVER. Perform calibration runs at low, medium, and high flow rates. Verify Meter Factor (MF) within contractual tolerance.
5. **Reinstatement**: Disconnect Prover, close vent/drain valves, pressurize Run A slowly via MV-1A-IN, perform soap leak test, return Run A to service, and archive report.

## Chapter 10: Transfer ISOTank Ship to Ship Procedure {#np-08-ch10-transfer-isotank-ship-to-ship}

### 1. Operational Framework {#np-08-10-1-sts-operational-framework}
- Transfer of 40ft LNG ISO Tanks from mother vessel (MV Savior) to receiving barge (TNB) 400m offshore.
- Governed by IGC Code, SIGTTO STS Transfer Guide, SOLAS Chapter IX (ISM Code), and MARPOL.

### 2. Weather Limits {#np-08-10-2-sts-weather-limits}
- **Wind Speed**: < 20 knots
- **Wave Height**: < 1.0 meter
- **Visibility**: > 1 nautical mile
- **Current Speed**: < 1.5 knots

### 3. Execution Protocols {#np-08-10-3-sts-execution-protocols}
- Syahbandar STS Operation Permit valid.
- Perform 20–30 cm pre-lift test to verify crane brake stability.
- Control lifted tank using tag lines. Barge transit speed < 3 knots. Gas test must confirm Methane LEL = 0%.
