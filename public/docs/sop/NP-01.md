## Chapter 1. General {#np-01-1-general}

### 1.1 Definition of terms {#np-01-1-1-definition-of-terms}

- **Senior Management**: CEO, SM (Management Representative), other executives
- **Management review**: It is the act that the management including CEO review the suitability adequacy and effectiveness of the “Safety, Health, Quality and Environmental Management System (NP-SSHQE)”
- **Management review meeting (We call it as the Meeting in this procedure)**: As a part of Management Review, it is the Meeting that managements and relevant team leaders analyze and review identified subjects to drive matters for a decision.

### 1.2 Responsibility and Authority {#np-01-1-2-responsibility-and-authority}

#### 1.2.1 President (CEO) {#np-01-1-2-1-president-ceo}
- Chairman of the Meeting
- To review and approve the decision of the Meeting
- To review and approve important communications in the organization

#### 1.2.2 Site Manager (SM) {#np-01-1-2-2-site-manager-sm}
- To identify the necessity of the Meeting
- To conduct the Meeting
- To supervise the implementation of items decided through the Meeting
- To analyze the effectiveness of NP-SSHQE
- Responsible Person for the Management Review Process.
- To confirm the implementation of organization instructions.
- Ensuring that the resources needed to establish, implement, maintain and improve the OH&S management system are available.
- To prepare the “Form NP01-03 Management Review Data” and submit it to the Headquarters (HQ)
- To implement organization instruction including decisions through the Management Review and report.

#### 1.2.3 HSE OFFICER {#np-01-1-2-3-hse-officer}
- To establish “form NP01-02 Management Review Meeting Plan”
- To collect, analyze and summarize each data for Management Review and make out the Meeting materials and distribute them to the Meeting members.
- To control the communication through the approval by CEO & SM
- To assist SM for the control of the NP-SSHQE


## Chapter 2. Management Review {#np-01-2-management-review}

### 2.1 Identification of Management Review Meeting {#np-01-2-1-identification-of-management-review-meeting}
- **Periodical Meeting**: the Meeting held at a fixed time annually.
- **Occasional Meeting**: the meeting held in following cases.
  - The time when Company (Nias Site) needs to change policy and objectives
  - The time when the senior management of the Company (Nias Site) is changed or big changes occur for the Company (Nias Site) and assignment in the Company (Nias Site).
  - The time when the Company (Nias Site) is faced with a critical situation.
  - The time when senior management identifies the necessary of the meeting.

### 2.2 Management Review Meeting Plan {#np-01-2-2-management-review-meeting-plan}
- **Periodical meeting**: Until Dec. 05th in every year, the leader of HSE OFFICER shall establish the Management Review Meeting Plan (form NP01-02) and addressed it to every team & site after winning an approval from SM.
- **Occasional Meeting**: The leader of HSE OFFICER shall establish the Management Review Meeting Plan (form NP01-02) 7 days before the prearranged date of the Meeting and addressed to every team & site after winning an approval from SM.

### 2.3 Submitting the data for Management Review {#np-01-2-3-submitting-the-data-for-management-review}
After receiving the Management Review Meeting Plan (form NP01-02), the team leader and the Site Manager shall submit the data for management review to the Company(Nias Site) until Dec. 15th (in case of Periodical meeting) or 3 days before meeting (in case of Occasional meeting) according to clause 2.4.

### 2.4 Holding the Management Review Meeting {#np-01-2-4-holding-the-management-review-meeting}

#### Meeting members {#np-01-2-4-1-meeting-members}
- **Chairman**: President
- **Other members**: Site Manager on vacation, others appointed by chairman.

#### Meeting date {#np-01-2-4-2-meeting-date}
- **Periodical meeting**: on third Wednesday Jan. in every year
- **Occasional meeting**: the date decided by chairman

#### Progress of the Management Review Meeting {#np-01-2-4-3-progress-of-meeting}

##### Establishment of the agenda {#np-01-2-4-3-1-establishment-of-agenda}
The chairman shall choose meeting agenda by reflecting his management commitment & the analysis matrix for management review.
Other members are able to propose to the meeting agenda by reflecting his opinion and the analysis matrix for management review.
The chairman is able to select the meeting agenda proposed by meeting members.

##### A discussion for the agenda {#np-01-2-4-3-2-discussion-for-agenda}
Under the chairman, meeting members shall do their best to draw an ideal matter for the decision by giving their own opinions.

##### Decision of the discussion {#np-01-2-4-3-3-decision-of-discussion}
Drawing the matters for decision including followings:
- Plans to improve the effectiveness and efficiency of management system
- Plans to improve the effectiveness of the management system process
- Plans to improve to improve the service that is suitable for customer requirement
- Necessities of the resource support
- Performance standard for critical equipment for next year
- Budget for site management and crew manning for next year
- Next year’s plans including the followings:
  - Maintenance plans
  - Training and drill for site board personnel
  - Training and drill for shore-based personnel
  - Annual detailed objectives and objective achieving plan

### 2.5 Recording, documented Information, delivery {#np-01-2-5-recording-documented-information-delivery}
- Decisions of the meeting shall be recorded “(Form NP01-02 Management Review Meeting Plan)”.
- Matters for Decisions shall be documented according to the instructions of CEO, if necessary.
- Followings should be delivered to each team and technical sites:
  - Analysis Matrix for management review
  - Management Review Meeting.
  - Decisions of Meeting and CEO’s instructions

### 2.6 Measuring and Improvement {#np-01-2-6-measuring-and-improvement}
- SM shall review and analyse the Report submitted by team leader and Site Manager, and evaluate the effectiveness of it.
- As a result of assessment, the matters that is either insufficient or to be improved shall be Reflected in either the “Site Safety Quality & Environmental Committee” or next Management Review.


## Chapter 3. Objective Control {#np-01-3-objective-control}

### 3.1 Establishing/amendment NP-SSHQE Objectives {#np-01-3-1-establishing-amendment-np-sshqe-objectives}
When CEO and SM identify the necessity for establishing/amendment of NP-SSHQE objectives the following should be reviewed:
- Customer feedback
- Statutory and regulatory requirement
- Results of accident analysis
- Result of process measurement and analysis
- Result of external survey and inspection
- Performance of environmental impact improvement
- Performance of risk improvement

#### Establishing/amendment procedure of NP-SSHQE objectives {#np-01-3-1-1-establishing-amendment-procedure}
- Establishing/amendment of long-term objectives should be decided through Management Review Meeting and approved by CEO.
- Establishing/amendment of short-term objectives (annual objectives) should be decided through Site Safety Security Quality Environmental Committee and approved by SM.
- Approved objectives shall be registered and delivered according to the Document Control Procedure (NP07 Ch.3).
- Short term objectives (annual objectives) shall be measurable and consistent with the company (Nias Site) management policy.
- Annual short-term objectives shall be planed in detail. It should be measurable and possible translating to numerical value for measurement
- HSE OFFICER leader shall distribute approved annual objectives to each team

### 3.2 Establishing and implementation of objective achieving plan {#np-01-3-2-establishing-and-implementation-objective-plan}
- Team leader of HSE OFFICER shall establish the “Objective Achieving Plan” for annual objectives and submit it to SM.
- Objective Achieving Plan shall be mentioned deadline and means of driving for each Objective by each team
- SM shall review the Objective Achieving Plan and approve it.
- Approved Objective Achieving Plan should be delivered to each team.
- Team leader and Site Manager shall implement the Objective Achieving Plan.

### 3.3 Assessment the implementation of the objective achieving plan {#np-01-3-3-assessment-implementation-objective-plan}
- Site Manager shall assessment the results of implementation for the Objective Achieving Plan through monthly periodic SSHQE Meeting.
- Head Office Team shall assessment the results of implementation for the Objective Achieving Plan through quarterly periodic SSHQE Meeting.
- HSE Officer shall record assessment results to SSHQE Meeting.
- HSE Officer shall submit SSHQE Meeting Minutes to SM
- SM shall review the SSHQE Meeting Minutes which was submitted by HQ.

### 3.4 Monitoring Performance of Objectives {#np-01-3-4-monitoring-performance-of-objectives}
- SM shall analyse and assess the performance of objectives through the Site Safety Security Quality Environmental Committee every 3 month.
- Shall be held Safety Quality Environmental Committee for analysis and assessment as mentioned above
- SM shall take following action according to the results of analysis and assessment:
  - When established annual objectives have not been achieved, the reason should be analyzed and corrective action should be taken.
  - When established annual objectives have been achieved, new objectives should be established.
- SM shall review whether the objective’s achieving plan is effective to reach NP- SSHQE long term objectives.
- The results of analysis and assessment shall be input to the Management Review:
  - The extent to which legal requirements and other requirements are fulfilled
  - Its activities and operations related to identified hazards, risks and opportunities;
  - Progress towards achievement of the company’s OH&S objectives;
  - Effectiveness of operational and other controls.

### 3.5 Prize system {#np-01-3-5-prize-system}
- HSE Officer Leader shall assess the implementation of Objective Achieving Plan, register best practice on the Best Practice Log and address it to relevant team.
- The Company (Nias Site) can properly award best team


## Chapter 4. Communication {#np-01-4-communication}

### 4.1 Purpose {#np-01-4-1-purpose}
This chapter specifies communication procedure which senior managements shall communicate their commitment and the concepts of safety quality & environmental excellence to company (Nias Site) personnel and ensure the effective implementation of the NP-SSHQE.

### 4.2 Identification of communication items {#np-01-4-2-identification-of-communication-items}
- Company (Nias Site) policy and NP-SSHQE objectives
- Results of management review
- Customer requirement
- Site information
- Accident information
- Top management’s instructions
- Recommendation for improvement
- Proposal
- SSHQE Meeting
- Among contractors and visitors to the workplace;
- Among other interested parties

### 4.3 Means of communication {#np-01-4-3-means-of-communication}
- Company (Nias Site) Circular including followings:
  - Site information (for engine, common)
  - Company (Nias Site) circular for accident
  - Information for major inspection
  - Company (Nias Site) instructions
- Proposal through the Site Safety Quality and Environmental Committee
- Proposal system
- Periodical seminar which attended by Site personnel
- Periodical meeting

### 4.4 How to communicate {#np-01-4-4-how-to-communicate}

#### Communication for NP-SSHQE policy and objectives {#np-01-4-4-1-communication-policy-objectives}
- The company (Nias Site) shall provide posters for NP-SSHQE policy and objectives, and these posters should be displayed in open place such as Office, Regas Facility.
- The annual plan shall be controlled according to chapter 3 of this procedure.

#### Communication for the results of management review {#np-01-4-4-2-communication-results-management-review}
- Communication for the results of management review shall be controlled according to chapter 2 of this procedure.

#### Communication with External Parties {#np-01-4-4-3-communication-with-external-parties}
- The company (Nias Site) shall ensure that the views of external interested parties are considered in establishing its communication process (es).
- When establishing its communication process (es), the company shall:
  - Take into account its legal requirements and other requirements;
  - Ensure that OH&S information to be communicated is consistent with information generated within the OH&S management system, and is reliable.

#### Company Circular {#np-01-4-4-4-company-circular}
- Documentation, Distribution and Receipt confirmation of Company Circular shall follow the ‘Control of System Document (NP-07)’.
- Site Manager shall familiarize to related personnel with the Company (Nias Site) Circular through circulation or training.
- Familiarization and Confirmation of Familiarization for Company Circular shall follow the ‘Control of System Document (NP-07)’.
- Company (Nias Site) Instructions described its implementation due date, which is one of the Company Circular, should be implemented by due date.
- The Manager Site shall submit the Report to SM with relevant evidence.
- HSE Officer shall analyse and evaluate the Report (Evidence) submitted by the team leader or the Manager Site.
- The results of analysis/evaluation shall be used as data for improvement through the Management Review or the Safety Quality and Environmental Committee.

#### Proposal through SSHQE meeting {#np-01-4-4-5-proposal-through-sshqe-meeting}
- Manager Site shall collect the proposal at any time and record it in the Periodical SSHQE Meeting Minutes.
- Head Office shall collect the proposal of Site personnel at any time and report it to the SSHQE Committee.
- SSHQE Committee shall review the proposal of Site personnel at the periodical meeting.
- Results of review/discussion for proposal should be informed to proposer.

#### Joint Seminar with Site and shore personnel {#np-01-4-4-6-joint-seminar}
- SM shall hold NP-SSHQE seminar where Site personnel is participating in, above one time per year.
- Seminar program shall include the following:
  - System operation status and its assessment
  - Merit and problem express of NP-SSHQE operation of Site
  - Announcement of best practice for NP-SSHQE operation
  - Discussion of Improving Measures for NP-SSHQE
  - Free discussion
- Results of seminar shall be used to improving of NP-SSHQE System and NP-SSHQE System operation and the implementation of system

### 4.5 Prize {#np-01-4-5-prize}

#### Prize for Best Practice {#np-01-4-5-1-prize-for-best-practice}
The company (Nias Site) shall analyse the Report (evidence) for company instructions, and shall select the best practice. Company (Nias Site) shall distribute selected Best Practice to all Site to promote the concept of NP-SSHQE.

#### Prize for Proposal {#np-01-4-5-2-prize-for-proposal}
The company (Nias Site) shall prize for accepted Proposal. The company (Nias Site) shall establish the standard of Prize for Proposal by separated regulation.
