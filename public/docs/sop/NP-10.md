### 1. Purpose {#np-10-1-1-purpose}

This SOP defines the procedure for environmental monitoring at LNG Nias Regasification Terminal. Its objectives are:
- To ensure systematic and consistent monitoring of environmental aspects (air, water, soil, noise and waste).
- To verify compliance with Indonesian regulations, company standards, and ISO 14001 requirements.
- To provide data for decision making, environmental performance evaluation, and continual improvement.
- To serve as an early warning system to prevent environmental pollution and incidents.

### 2. Context of the Organization {#np-10-1-2-context-of-the-organization}

**Company Information & Context**

LNG Nias Regasification Terminal is a strategic facility designed to store and regasify liquefied natural gas (LNG) to supply natural gas to power plants in the region.

Key activities include:
- LNG unloading from carriers at the jetty
- LNG storage in cryogenic tanks
- Regasification through vaporizers (AAV)
- Handling of boil-off gas (BOG) through compressors
- Gas send-out to transmission pipelines

These activities generate waste streams such as:
- Non-hazardous domestic waste from offices, canteens, and accommodation
- Hazardous waste (used oil, chemical sludge, oily rags, expired chemicals, contaminated PPE)
- Wastewater (process effluent, stormwater runoff, domestic sewage)

**Scope**

This SOP applies to all monitoring activities covering:
- Air quality (ambient, stack emissions)
- Water quality (wastewater effluent, seawater, groundwater)
- Soil quality (periodic sampling in THWS and chemical storage areas)
- Noise and vibration (from compressors, vaporizers, power generators)
- Biodiversity monitoring (as required by AMDAL/UKL-UPL)

Out of scope:
- Occupational health monitoring (covered under Industrial Hygiene Procedure)

### 5. Procedure Section {#np-10-1-5-procedure-section}

#### 5.1 Leadership and Commitment {#np-10-1-5-1-leadership-and-commitment}

Management ensures provision of resources, equipment and competent personnel for monitoring.

#### 5.2 Planning {#np-10-1-5-2-planning}

The Environmental Policy of LNG Nias Regasification Terminal states the company’s commitment to:
- Compliance with laws and regulations.
- Pollution prevention and waste minimization.
- Transparent communication with stakeholders.
- Implementation of ISO 14001 principles.

##### 5.2.1 Monitoring Objectives and Targets {#np-10-1-5-2-1-monitoring-objectives-and-targets}

- Compliance with wastewater discharge standards.
- Compliance with air and noise quality standards.
- Zero exceedance of regulatory limits.

##### 5.2.2 Regulatory and Other Requirements {#np-10-1-5-2-2-regulatory-and-other-requirements}

Monitoring must follow AMDAL commitments, UKL-UPL and environmental permits.

#### 5.3 Implementation and Operation {#np-10-1-5-3-implementation-and-operation}

##### 5.3.1 Monitoring Parameters and Frequency {#np-10-1-5-3-1-monitoring-parameters-and-frequency}

- Air Quality: SO₂, NOx, CO, PM₁₀, HC – Quarterly.
- Stack Emission: CO₂, NOx, O₂, SO₂ – Semi-annual.
- Wastewater Effluent: pH, TSS, Oil & Grease, COD, BOD – Monthly.
- Seawater (Marine Monitoring): DO, salinity, turbidity, temperature – Quarterly.
- Noise: dBA at site boundary – Quarterly.
- Soil: Heavy metals, hydrocarbons – Annual.

##### 5.3.2 Monitoring Locations {#np-10-1-5-3-2-monitoring-locations}

- CPI/OWS outlet, STP outlet, guard basin.
- Seawater at jetty and boundary.
- Ambient air at administration building, accommodation, fence line.
- Noise at power generation, jetty, boundary line.

##### 5.3.4 Calibration and QA/QC {#np-10-1-5-3-4-calibration-and-qa-qc}

- Monitoring equipment calibrated annually.
- Field duplicates and blank samples used for QA/QC.

##### 5.3.5 Data Recording and blank samples used for QA/QC {#np-10-1-5-3-5-data-recording}

- All results recorded in Monitoring Log Sheets.
- Monthly review by Environmental Officer.
- Quarterly reporting to DLH and KLHK.

#### 5.4 Roles, Responsibilities, and Competence {#np-10-1-5-4-roles-responsibilities-competence}

- SSHQE Team: overall responsibility, liaison with regulators.
- Environmental Officer: plan monitoring, supervise sampling, prepare reports.
- Operations Team: provide access, support sampling.
- Contracted Laboratory: conduct accredited analysis, issue results.

#### 5.5 Communication and Documentation Control {#np-10-1-5-5-communication-and-documentation-control}

- Internal: Monthly Environment Meetings, awareness posters.
- External: Quarterly regulatory reports, ISO audits.
- Documentation controlled under SSHQE Document Control Procedure.

#### 5.6 Emergency Monitoring (Spill/Accident Events) {#np-10-1-5-6-emergency-monitoring}

In case of major spill, LNG release, or fire, immediate monitoring of air, water, and soil must be conducted.

### 6. Performance Evaluation {#np-10-1-6-performance-evaluation}

#### 6.1 Internal Audit {#np-10-1-6-1-internal-audit}

- Annual audit of monitoring program.
- Nonconformities recorded and corrective actions assigned.

#### 6.2 Management Review {#np-10-1-6-2-management-review}

### 7. Improvement {#np-10-1-7-improvement}

### 1. Purpose {#np-10-2-1-purpose}

The purpose of this document is to establish a structured and comprehensive procedure for the management of all waste streams generated at LNG Nias Regasification Terminal. This includes hazardous (B3) and non-hazardous waste, wastewater, and specific operational guidelines for the Temporary Hazardous Waste Storage (THWS) facility.

The objectives are:
- To prevent pollution and minimize negative environmental impacts arising from LNG regasification activities.
- To ensure full compliance with Indonesian laws, regulations, and international best practices such as ISO 14001 and IMO IGC Code.
- To define responsibilities of all personnel in waste management activities.
- To provide guidance for proper handling, segregation, packaging, temporary storage, and disposal of wastes.
- To promote the principles of Reduce, Reuse, Recycle (3R) within company operations.
- To ensure systematic monitoring, recording, and reporting of waste management activities to stakeholders and regulatory bodies.

#### 5.3 Implementation and Operation {#np-10-2-5-3-implementation-and-operation}

##### 5.3.1 Business Process {#np-10-2-5-3-1-business-process}

The waste management business process at LNG Nias Regasification Terminal is structured as follows:

| Process Step | Description |
| ------ | ------ |
| Waste Generation | Waste originates from LNG unloading, regasification processes, utility operations, maintenance activities, and administrative facilities. |
| Segregation at Source | Waste is separated into three categories: Non-hazardous, Hazardous (B3), and Wastewater. |
| Collection & Packaging | Each category is collected in designated containers. Hazardous waste is packaged according to PP No.22/2021 requirements. |
| Temporary Storage | Non-hazardous waste → segregated storage area or municipal landfill.<br>Hazardous waste → THWS facility, with maximum storage duration as per regulation.<br>Wastewater → CPI/OWS or STP. |
| Treatment & Disposal | Non-hazardous → recycle, compost, incinerate, or landfill.<br>Hazardous → transported to licensed contractors for treatment/disposal.<br>Wastewater → discharged after meeting quality standards. |
| Monitoring and Reporting | Data recorded in logbooks, monthly reviews, quarterly reports to regulators. |

**Waste Flow Summary:**
`Finding` → `Recording` → `Root Cause Analysis` → `Corrective Action` → `Verification` → `Close-out`

#### 5.6 Operation {#np-10-2-5-6-operation}

##### 5.6.1 Operational Control {#np-10-2-5-6-1-operational-control}

**Non-Hazardous Waste:**
- Collected daily, segregated into organic and non-organic.
- Organic waste → compost/incinerator.
- Non-organic waste → recycling (paper, plastics, metal).

**Hazardous Waste:**
- Identified with waste codes (PP No.22/2021).
- Packaged in drums/IBC with hazard labels.
- Stored at THWS facility with proper secondary containment.
- Manifest issued for transfer to licensed contractor.

**Wastewater:**
- Process wastewater → CPI/OWS, effluent tested before discharge.
- Domestic wastewater → STP, effluent monitored per PermenLHK 68/2016.

##### 5.6.2 Emergency Preparedness and Response {#np-10-2-5-6-2-emergency-preparedness-response}

Emergency scenarios include:
- Oil/chemical spill at THWS.
- Fire incident in hazardous waste storage.
- LNG spill incident leading to contaminated waste.

Preparedness measures:
- Spill kits available at THWS and operational areas.
- Fire extinguishers (foam, CO₂) in waste storage areas.
- Emergency drills conducted quarterly.

##### 5.6.3 Performance Evaluation {#np-10-2-5-6-3-performance-evaluation}

Performance evaluation of waste management is carried out through:
- Monitoring: Regular inspection of segregation, storage, and disposal activities.
- Measurement: Tracking waste volume (non-hazardous, hazardous, wastewater) and treatment efficiency.
- Internal Audit: Conducted annually to ensure compliance with ISO 14001 and regulations.

### 6. Evaluation of Compliance {#np-10-2-6-evaluation-of-compliance}

#### 6.1 Purpose {#np-10-2-6-1-purpose}

Evaluation of compliance ensures that LNG Nias Regasification Terminal meets all environmental obligations, including waste management requirements set by Indonesian regulations, ISO 14001, and internal policies.

#### 6.2 Methodology {#np-10-2-6-2-methodology}

Compliance is evaluated through:

| Method | Description |
| ------ | ------ |
| Regulatory Review | Monitoring changes in regulations (PP 22/2021, PermenLHK 6/2021, 68/2016, ISO standards). |
| Internal Audit | Annual internal audit of waste management practices. |
| External Audit | Periodic audit by certification bodies (ISO 14001), government inspections (KLHK, DLH). |
| Permit Compliance Monitoring | Ensuring all waste permits (THWS, wastewater discharge, incinerator) are valid and requirements fulfilled. |
| Reporting | Quarterly reports to regulators, annual sustainability/environment report to stakeholders. |

#### 6.3 Compliance Indicators {#np-10-2-6-3-compliance-indicators}

- Waste segregation compliance rate (% of correctly segregated waste).
- Hazardous waste storage duration (≤ 90 days as per regulation).
- Wastewater discharge quality (in compliance with PermenLHK 68/2016).
- Training coverage (% of employees trained annually).
- Regulatory findings (number of nonconformities from inspections).

#### 6.4 Noncompliance Management {#np-10-2-6-4-noncompliance-management}

- Any noncompliance identified must be recorded in the Compliance Register.
- Immediate corrective action must be taken.
- Root cause analysis conducted for significant violations.
- Reports submitted to SSHQE Manager and Top Management.
