# Section 1: The General {#np-12-1-the-general}

## 1.1 Purpose {#np-12-1-1-purpose}

The purpose of this procedure is to reduce the risk of incidents through managing unexpected changes which happens during the Regasification Terminal operation.

## 1.2 Identification of Change {#np-12-1-2-identification-of-change}

- Changes in Company (Nias Site) structure
- Replacement or employment of key members on site
- Amendment to NP-SSHQE document
- Reconstruction of structure, new installation or modification of critical equipment and systems
- Alternation of employee levels on site

## 1.3 Levels of Authority & Responsibility, Assignment {#np-12-1-3-levels-of-authority-and-responsibility}

| No. | Kind of change | Responsible team | Assistance team | Approval |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Changes in Company (Nias Site) structure | SYSMAN | (HSE Site) | SM |
| 2 | Replacement or employment of key members on site | SYSMAN | (HSE Site), Related team | SM |
| 3 | Amendment to SSHQE document | SYSMAN | (HSE Site), Related team | SM |
| 4 | Reconstruction of site structure, new installation or modification of critical equipment and systems | TECHNICAL | | SM |
| 6 | Alternation of Worker levels on site | HRD | TECHNICAL, SYSMAN (HSE Site) | SM |

## 1.4 Process of Change {#np-12-1-4-process-of-change}

- Planning of change
- Approval
- Implementation
- Measurement and analysis
- Improvement

# Section 2: Management of Change {#np-12-2-management-of-change}

## 2.1 Establishment of Change Plan {#np-12-2-1-establishment-of-change-plan}

Organization shall establish a process (es) for the implementation and control of planned temporary and permanent changes that impact OH&S performance, including:

- new products, services and processes, or changes to existing products, services & processes, including:
  - workplace locations and surroundings;
  - work organization;
  - working conditions;
  - equipment;
  - work force;
- changes to legal requirements and other requirements;
- Changes in knowledge or information about hazards and OH&S risks;
- developments in knowledge and technology.
- maintaining the environmental management system
- environmental aspects
- internal communication
- operational control
- intemal audit programme and
- management review

Site Manager in charge of this matter shall draw up the ‘Plan of change’ (NP12-01), if want to carry out the discriminated change including following.

- Documented information No.
- Object of change (site/team/person)
  - If the object is reconstruction of the Regasification Facility, establishment or reconstruction of new critical equipment, Important Spare Part List should be attached certainly.
- Kind of change & details
- Team & person in charge of change
- Purpose or reason of change
- Permanent or temporary change
- In case of temporary change, setup of time
- Items relative to procedure and form
- Conducted procedure to decrease the risks
- Contents of risk assessment
- Data, sheet Doc No.
- Possibility of acceptance for assessment result
- Improvement for risk – In case that result of assessment not accept
- Conducted contents of to improve the risks
- Date of re-risk assessment after improvement
- Possibility of acceptance for re-assessment result
- On-site equipment, team, person effected by change
- Discrimination and implementation of additional education & training
- An expected cost
- The expected time to start change
- The expected time to finalize change

### Risk Assessment {#np-12-2-1-risk-assessment}

Responsible team implements the safe measure according to each procedure to decrease risks accompanied by change will be conducted.

According to the NP09 Procedure for Risk Assessment and Safety management, implement the risk assessment including followings.

- International convention, municipal law & suitability of organization policy
- Influence on environment.
- Influence on Organization management system.
- Qualifications for the staff.
- Specification of the equipment.
- Matters relating to safety & health.
- Matters relating to security.
- Influence on organization structure.

After conducting the risk assessment, the risk rated as 1 or 2 (risk rating 1 or 2) is acceptable and need not additional mitigation.

The risk rated as 4 or 5 (risk rating 4 or 5) is not acceptable and needs additional mitigation in accordance with organization procedure NP09.

For the risk rated as 3 (risk rating 3), the necessity of mitigation should be decided through further review.

After additional mitigation for unacceptable risk, the risk should be reassessed to confirm its risk rating.

## 2.2 Approval of the 'Plan of Change' {#np-12-2-2-approval-of-plan-of-change}

Team leader shall submit the following to SITE MANAGER in order to get approval for the implementation of the ‘Plan of Change’.

- Plan of change
- Risk assessment sheet
- Risk improvement plan and risk re-assessment sheet after termination regarding items of 4 – 5 grades in 1st risk assessment

### SITE MANAGER's Approval {#np-12-2-2-site-manager-approval}

SITE MANAGER shall review submitted documented information and verify that all necessary procedures have been followed and that all potential risks have been mitigated.

If the result of verification is suitable, SM shall approve the implementation of change.

## 2.3 Implementation and Completion of Change {#np-12-2-3-implementation-and-completion-of-change}

Responsible team shall distribute the approved plan to relevant teams/Site/persons. Receipt of documented information should be confirmed in accordance with organization procedure.

Leading team shall monitor the implementation process of change until its completion.

After completion of change, team leader shall draw up the report of ‘Completion of change’ (NP12-02) including the following.

- Doc No of the Plan of Change
- Object of change and details
- Responsible team or person for change
- Permanent or temporary change
- Additional mitigation actions to reduce the risk of incident, if any
- Appropriate training completed to increase the effectiveness of the change
- Summary of change process completed
- Details of change communicated
- Appropriate manual/procedure/drawings updated
- Final Risk assessment
- In case that permanent change needs the extension of targeted completion date
  - Contents of extension
  - Planned completion date
  - Targeted completion date to extend
- In case that temporary change needs extension
  - Contents of extension
  - Planned time scale
  - Proposed time scale to extend

Final risk assessment should be done in order to ensure that unacceptable risk has been perfectly removed, if necessary.

### Extension of Time Scale for Temporary Change {#np-12-2-3-extension-of-time-scale}

If temporary change has not been completed within approved time scale, additional extension should be requested.

SITE MANAGER shall review the requisition of extension, and approve to extend target date, if applicable.

## 2.4 Measure, Analysis and Improvement {#np-12-2-4-measure-analysis-and-improvement}

SITE MANAGER shall review the following to analyze the effectiveness of change.

- Was change procedure followed?
- Was change completed within time scale?
- Were all risk reduction measures taken?
- Have all manuals, drawings been updated?
- Did change meet objective of change?
- Was the management of change process effective?
  - If not, identify amendment required to system documented information.

All reviewed result should be dealt as priority agenda through the quarterly SSHQE-meeting and yearly management review.

SITE MANAGER shall actively improve identified problem, if any.
