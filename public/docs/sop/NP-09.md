## NP09-01 Risk Assessment Procedure {#np-09-np09-01-risk-assessment-procedure}

### 1. PURPOSE {#np-09-1-purpose}
The purpose of this procedure is to provide a systematic approach to risk assessment, ensuring that risks associated with tasks are reduced to an acceptable level, personnel are competent in evaluating risks, assessments are reviewed periodically or when changes occur, and compliance with applicable legislative requirements is maintained.

*(Tujuan dari prosedur ini adalah untuk menetapkan pendekatan sistematis dalam penilaian risiko, memastikan bahwa risiko yang terkait dengan tugas dikurangi hingga tingkat yang dapat diterima, personel kompeten dalam mengevaluasi risiko, penilaian ditinjau secara berkala atau ketika terjadi perubahan, serta kepatuhan terhadap persyaratan peraturan yang berlaku tetap terjaga).*

### 2. SCOPE {#np-09-2-scope}
This procedure applies to the assessment, documentation, and review of occupational health and safety risks arising from work activities at production facilities, both onshore and offshore, including brown-field and green-field projects. It excludes major hazards or process safety issues such as loss of containment, fire, and explosion, which are addressed in separate procedures. The standards outlined herein may also serve as minimum performance requirements for contractors, including those providing services activities, and must be integrated with the Permit to Work (PTW) and Job Safety Analysis (JSA) systems to ensure hazard identification and effective controls.

*(Prosedur ini berlaku untuk penilaian, pendokumentasian, dan peninjauan risiko kesehatan dan keselamatan kerja yang timbul dari aktivitas di fasilitas produksi, baik di darat maupun lepas pantai, termasuk proyek brown-field dan green-field. Prosedur ini tidak mencakup bahaya besar atau isu keselamatan proses seperti kehilangan containment, kebakaran, dan ledakan, karena hal tersebut diatur dalam prosedur terpisah. Standar yang dijabarkan di sini juga dapat digunakan sebagai persyaratan kinerja minimum bagi kontraktor, termasuk penyedia jasa, serta wajib diintegrasikan dengan sistem Permit to Work (PTW) dan Job Safety Analysis (JSA) untuk memastikan identifikasi bahaya dan pengendalian yang efektif).*

### 5. HAZARD IDENTIFICATION {#np-09-5-hazard-identification}
When a Job is to be undertaken, the Originator / Responsible Person or the worker who will perform the work will identify if a Risk Assessment is required. The Risk Assessment Process Flowchart shows the various stages.

Using the Preliminary Risk Assessment Checklist (PRAC) as a guide he will identify any hazards present in the work activity and answer either Yes or No on the checklist, in Column 1. This is Stage 1.
If ‘Yes’ is entered in Column 1, details of how the risk will be minimized must be entered in Column 2. These may include existing Risk Assessments, Chemicals/Materials Assessment, Job Step Plan, etc.
In Column 3 the question ‘Is the residual risk ALARP (As Low As Reasonably Practicable)’ is answered. If there is any doubt that the action or procedures written in Column 2 do not satisfactorily reduce the potential risk to ALARP, then a No is entered into Column 3.

Any ‘No’ Answers in Column 3 require that further Risk Assessment be carried out. Also the inclusion of multiple ‘generic’ Risk Assessments may indicate the need for a job specific risk assessment.
The Responsible Person then reviews the completed PRAC with the Work Leader.

*(Apabila suatu pekerjaan akan dilaksanakan, Originator / Penanggung Jawab atau pekerja yang akan melakukan pekerjaan tersebut harus mengidentifikasi apakah diperlukan Risk Assessment. Alur Proses Risk Assessment menunjukkan tahapan-tahapan yang harus dilakukan.*
*Dengan menggunakan Preliminary Risk Assessment Checklist (PRAC) sebagai panduan, individu tersebut akan mengidentifikasi potensi bahaya dalam aktivitas pekerjaan dan mengisi jawaban Ya atau Tidak pada Kolom 1. Tahapan ini disebut Stage 1.*
*Apabila jawaban Ya dicantumkan pada Kolom 1, maka rincian mengenai cara risiko akan diminimalkan harus diisi pada Kolom 2. Hal ini dapat mencakup Risk Assessment yang sudah ada, Chemicals/Materials Assessment, Job Step Plan, dan lain-lain.*
*Pada Kolom 3, pertanyaan “Apakah risiko residual sudah ALARP (As Low As Reasonably Practicable)?” harus dijawab. Jika terdapat keraguan bahwa tindakan atau prosedur yang tertulis pada Kolom 2 belum secara memadai menurunkan potensi risiko hingga ALARP, maka jawaban Tidak harus diisi pada Kolom 3.*
*Setiap jawaban Tidak pada Kolom 3 mengharuskan dilakukannya Risk Assessment lebih lanjut. Selain itu, penggunaan banyak generic Risk Assessments dapat mengindikasikan perlunya Risk Assessment yang lebih spesifik terhadap pekerjaan tersebut.*
*Setelah itu, Penanggung Jawab meninjau PRAC yang telah dilengkapi bersama Work Leader).*

### 6. RISK ASSESSMENT PROCEDURE STAGES {#np-09-6-risk-assessment-procedure-stages}

#### 6.1 Further Risk Assessment Required {#np-09-6-1-further-risk-assessment-required}
For a job specific risk assessment that has a clear job step, a Job Safety Analysis methodology and guidance to fill out JSA Form shall be conducted at minimum.
For a non-job specific risk assessment (i.e. to determine a high-level identification of hazard for an overall project scope), Hazid methodology (inductive), or other type of Hazid methodology (deductive), can be used.

The Responsible Person will select a team comprising a minimum of two persons. This will include a Team Leader for the Risk Assessment, who must be Risk Assessment trained. The team must have the correct skills, information and knowledge to undertake an assessment.
Risk Assessment is completed using the standard LNG NIAS GASIFIKASI system Job Safety Analysis Form. Where Risk Assessment identifies that the overall residual risk is low, then the PRAC and Risk Assessment sheet are reviewed by the Responsible Person, if they are satisfied then they will sign both the PRAC and the Risk Assessment.

The Work Leader then takes them forward to the next stage, Work Permit application approval and follow LNG NIAS GASIFIKASI HSE Standard Permit to Work unless already approved as a non-permitted task.
If the risk cannot be reduced to an acceptably low level despite reassessing the work and introducing alternative methods, then the Responsible Person must bring this to the attention of the Facility Responsible Person (e.g. OIMs) who will refer the issue to HSE Jakarta for guidance.

*(Untuk penilaian risiko spesifik terhadap suatu pekerjaan yang memiliki langkah kerja yang jelas, metodologi Job Safety Analysis (JSA) serta panduan pengisian Formulir JSA wajib dilakukan sebagai standar minimum.*
*Untuk penilaian risiko yang tidak spesifik pada pekerjaan tertentu (misalnya untuk mengidentifikasi bahaya secara umum dalam lingkup proyek secara keseluruhan), metodologi Hazid (induktif), atau jenis metodologi Hazid lainnya (deduktif), dapat digunakan.*
*Penanggung Jawab akan memilih tim yang terdiri dari minimal dua orang. Tim ini harus mencakup seorang Team Leader untuk Risk Assessment, yang wajib memiliki pelatihan Risk Assessment. Tim tersebut harus memiliki keterampilan, informasi, dan pengetahuan yang memadai untuk melaksanakan penilaian.*
*Risk Assessment diselesaikan dengan menggunakan Formulir Job Safety Analysis standar system LNG NIAS GASIFIKASI.*
*Apabila hasil Risk Assessment menunjukkan bahwa risiko residual secara keseluruhan rendah, maka PRAC dan lembar Risk Assessment akan ditinjau oleh Penanggung Jawab. Jika dinyatakan memadai, Penanggung Jawab akan menandatangani PRAC dan Risk Assessment tersebut.*
*Selanjutnya, Work Leader membawa dokumen tersebut ke tahap berikutnya, yaitu persetujuan aplikasi Work Permit, dan mengikuti LNG NIAS GASIFIKASI HSE Standard Permit to Work, kecuali bila pekerjaan tersebut telah disetujui sebagai tugas non-permit.*
*Apabila risiko tidak dapat diturunkan hingga tingkat yang dapat diterima meskipun telah dilakukan penilaian ulang dan penerapan metode alternatif, maka Penanggung Jawab wajib melaporkannya kepada Facility Responsible Person (misalnya OIM), yang selanjutnya akan meneruskan isu tersebut kepada HSE Jakarta untuk mendapatkan arahan lebih lanjut).*

#### 6.2 Risk Assessment Not Required {#np-09-6-2-risk-assessment-not-required}
If the completed PRAC indicates that a full Risk Assessment is not required the Responsible Person can sign the PRAC, this takes them forward to the next stage which is to determine whether a PTW is required, if ‘Yes’, he proceeds as under Work Permit Application as per HSE PTW Procedure.
If a PTW is not required, then the Responsible Person completes a PJSM with the Work Leader to ensure they understand their duties and responsibilities at the work site. The Work Leader then continues to the worksite to carry out a PJSM with their team. The Work Leader is responsible to ensure all personnel involved in the work understand the result of risk assessment.

*(Apabila PRAC yang telah diselesaikan menunjukkan bahwa Risk Assessment lengkap tidak diperlukan, maka Penanggung Jawab dapat menandatangani PRAC tersebut. Selanjutnya proses dilanjutkan ke tahap berikutnya, yaitu menentukan apakah Permit to Work (PTW) diperlukan. Jika Ya, maka proses dilanjutkan sesuai dengan Work Permit Application sebagaimana diatur dalam Prosedur HSE PTW.*
*Apabila PTW tidak diperlukan, maka Penanggung Jawab melaksanakan Pre-Job Safety Meeting (PJSM) bersama Work Leader untuk memastikan bahwa mereka memahami tugas dan tanggung jawabnya di lokasi kerja. Setelah itu, Work Leader melanjutkan ke lokasi kerja untuk melaksanakan PJSM bersama timnya. Work Leader bertanggung jawab memastikan seluruh personel yang terlibat dalam pekerjaan memahami hasil dari Risk Assessment).*

#### 6.3 Work Permit Application {#np-09-6-3-work-permit-application}
After completing the PRAC and, if required, a Risk Assessment the Originator will raise a Work Permit, following the LNG NIAS GASIFIKASI Regas Permit to Work Procedure, and will attach the completed PRAC, Risk Assessment and any supporting documentation (see also HSE Standards Manual).
A pre-job safety meeting (PJSM) is held between the Responsible Person and the Work Leader to ensure proper briefing on these points, and to ensure full awareness of all aspects of the work and the requirements for completing all tasks in a safe and effective manner (Stage 6). The Work Permit and attachments will be submitted to the Permit Authoriser for approval (Stage 7).

Where the Work Permit Authoriser cannot accept the Risk Assessment measures or considers that all the hazards have not been fully covered, the Authoriser will discuss with the Originator / Responsible Person any amendments to the assessment. If necessary, the assessment process will be repeated from Stage 2.
Approved Work Permit applications will be signed by the Permit Authoriser and issued by the Permit Issuer to the Work Leader, where he assures himself that the Work Leader fully understands his responsibilities under the Permit to Work Procedure (Stage 8).

*(Setelah menyelesaikan PRAC dan, apabila diperlukan, Risk Assessment, Originator akan mengajukan Work Permit sesuai dengan Prosedur LNG NIAS GASIFIKASI Regas Permit to Work, dengan melampirkan PRAC yang telah diselesaikan, Risk Assessment, serta dokumen pendukung lainnya (lihat juga HSE Standards Manual).*
*Sebelum pekerjaan dimulai, dilakukan Pre-Job Safety Meeting (PJSM) antara Penanggung Jawab dan Work Leader untuk memastikan penyampaian pengarahan yang tepat terkait poin-poin tersebut, serta memastikan pemahaman menyeluruh mengenai seluruh aspek pekerjaan dan persyaratan penyelesaian tugas secara aman dan efektif (Tahap 6). Work Permit beserta lampirannya kemudian diserahkan kepada Permit Authoriser untuk persetujuan (Tahap 7).*
*Apabila Permit Authoriser tidak dapat menerima langkah-langkah Risk Assessment atau menilai bahwa seluruh potensi bahaya belum sepenuhnya dicakup, maka Authoriser akan mendiskusikan dengan Originator / Penanggung Jawab mengenai perubahan yang diperlukan pada assessment. Jika perlu, proses assessment akan diulang mulai dari Tahap 2.*
*Aplikasi Work Permit yang telah disetujui akan ditandatangani oleh Permit Authoriser dan diterbitkan oleh Permit Issuer kepada Work Leader, dengan memastikan bahwa Work Leader sepenuhnya memahami tanggung jawabnya sesuai dengan Prosedur Permit to Work (Tahap 8)).*

#### 6.4 Workforce Briefing {#np-09-6-4-workforce-briefing}
A Pre-Job Safety Meeting (PJSM) will be conducted for all tasks. This may be formal, with topics discussed fully documented, or informal, where only the signature of attendees is recorded. The PJSM will take place at the worksite following the issue of the Work Permit.
The Work Leader will ensure that the work team members are fully conversant with the work scope, any Work Permit requirements, procedures, assessment issues and risk reduction measures. The basis of the discussion will be the work scope, how it is to be achieved safely, the workplace, the hazards and protective / control measures in place to protect the team (Stage 10).
The whole purpose of this section is to ensure that every member of the team understands all the requirements of the task, any new member joining must be briefed in the same way (Stage 11).

*(Pre-Job Safety Meeting (PJSM) wajib dilaksanakan untuk setiap pekerjaan. PJSM dapat dilakukan secara formal, dengan seluruh topik diskusi didokumentasikan secara lengkap, atau secara informal, dimana hanya tanda tangan peserta yang dicatat. PJSM dilaksanakan di lokasi kerja setelah Work Permit diterbitkan.*
*Work Leader bertanggung jawab untuk memastikan bahwa seluruh anggota tim kerja benar-benar memahami lingkup pekerjaan, persyaratan Work Permit, prosedur, hasil assessment, serta langkah-langkah pengendalian risiko. Dasar diskusi mencakup lingkup pekerjaan, cara pelaksanaannya secara aman, kondisi lokasi kerja, potensi bahaya, serta langkah-langkah perlindungan/pengendalian yang tersedia untuk melindungi tim (Tahap 10).*
*Tujuan utama dari bagian ini adalah untuk memastikan bahwa setiap anggota tim memahami seluruh persyaratan pekerjaan. Setiap anggota baru yang bergabung wajib diberikan pengarahan dengan cara yang sama (Tahap 11)).*

#### 6.5 Workforce Briefing Confirmation {#np-09-6-5-workforce-briefing-confirmation}
Following the PJSM discussion, the Work Leader enters his name, signature and activity start date on the PJSM sheet, to confirm that he has conducted a full work briefing and that, to the best of his knowledge, the work team understands the issues.
Each member of the work team involved in any aspect of the work will enter their name, sign and date, to confirm that they have been briefed by the Work Leader, that they understand the essential work requirements, work hazards, risks and all the measures in place to reduce the risks.

*(Setelah diskusi PJSM selesai, Work Leader mencantumkan nama, tanda tangan, dan tanggal mulai aktivitas pada lembar PJSM untuk mengonfirmasi bahwa ia telah memberikan pengarahan kerja secara menyeluruh serta memastikan, sejauh pengetahuannya, bahwa tim kerja memahami isu-isu yang dibahas.*
*Setiap anggota tim kerja yang terlibat dalam aspek pekerjaan wajib mencantumkan nama, tanda tangan, dan tanggal pada lembar PJSM untuk mengonfirmasi bahwa mereka telah menerima pengarahan dari Work Leader, memahami persyaratan utama pekerjaan, potensi bahaya, risiko, serta seluruh langkah pengendalian yang telah ditetapkan untuk meminimalkan risiko).*

#### 6.6 Risk Assessment Revalidation {#np-09-6-6-risk-assessment-revalidation}
The PRAC/Risk Assessment must be revalidated whenever:
- there has been a break in continuity of the work for more than 24 hours,
- if the Work Leader or Responsible Person has changed (e.g. at night shift handover),
- or there has been a significant change to the method of work,
to ensure that no further risks have arisen. If the change in the method of work is significant enough it may require the raising of a new PRAC/Risk Assessment and Work Permit.

*(PRAC/Risk Assessment wajib divalidasi ulang apabila:*
*- terjadi jeda dalam kesinambungan pekerjaan lebih dari 24 jam,*
*- terjadi pergantian Work Leader atau Responsible Person (misalnya saat serah terima shift malam),*
*- atau terdapat perubahan signifikan pada metode kerja,*
*untuk memastikan tidak muncul risiko tambahan. Apabila perubahan metode kerja dianggap cukup signifikan, maka diperlukan penerbitan PRAC/Risk Assessment baru beserta Work Permit yang baru).*

#### 6.7 Non-Work Permit TED Tasks {#np-09-6-7-non-work-permit-ted-tasks}
To ensure that routine and other non-Work Permit tasks are assessed to an equal standard, the Responsible Person will ensure that these tasks are subject to the same steps in this procedure as Work Permit tasks, that is a PRAC/Risk Assessment for these tasks is raised. These should be revalidated as per section 6.6. Certain routine tasks especially Operations duties will be Risk Assessed and reviewed every 12 months or if the task changes. For routine work of low-risk activity, a PRAC / Risk Assessment may not be required – refer to the HSE Advisor Jakarta for advice.

*(Untuk memastikan bahwa pekerjaan rutin maupun pekerjaan lain yang tidak memerlukan Work Permit tetap dinilai dengan standar yang sama, Penanggung Jawab wajib memastikan bahwa pekerjaan tersebut mengikuti tahapan yang sama dalam prosedur ini sebagaimana pekerjaan yang memerlukan Work Permit, yaitu dengan menyusun PRAC/Risk Assessment untuk pekerjaan tersebut.*
*Dokumen ini harus divalidasi ulang sesuai dengan bagian 6.6. Pekerjaan rutin tertentu, khususnya tugas Operasi, wajib dilakukan Risk Assessment dan ditinjau setiap 12 bulan atau apabila terjadi perubahan pada pekerjaan tersebut.*
*Untuk pekerjaan rutin dengan tingkat risiko rendah, PRAC/Risk Assessment dapat saja tidak diperlukan – untuk hal ini, rujuk pada HSE Advisor Jakarta untuk mendapatkan arahan).*

#### 6.8 Training {#np-09-6-8-training}
Training will be carried out for all personnel who are likely to form part of a team for Risk Assessments covered under this procedure. For more training requirements, see HSE Training Matrix Procedure.

*(Pelatihan akan diberikan kepada seluruh personel yang kemungkinan menjadi bagian dari tim Risk Assessment sebagaimana diatur dalam prosedur ini. Untuk kebutuhan pelatihan lebih lanjut, dapat merujuk pada HSE Training Matrix Procedure).*

#### 6.9 Review {#np-09-6-9-review}
All PRAC/Risk Assessment of routine non-Work Permitted tasks such as Operations routine tasks, are treated as ‘live’, and must be reviewed every 12 months or sooner if a significant change occurs. For Risk Assessment attached to Work Permits the Review Team must verify the contents to ensure that significant changes have not occurred since it was produced, and the Responsible Person must sign to confirm this has been carried out to his satisfaction.

*(Seluruh PRAC/Risk Assessment untuk pekerjaan rutin yang tidak memerlukan Work Permit, seperti tugas rutin Operasi, dianggap sebagai dokumen “aktif” dan wajib ditinjau setiap 12 bulan sekali atau lebih cepat apabila terjadi perubahan signifikan.*
*Untuk Risk Assessment yang dilampirkan pada Work Permit, Tim Review harus melakukan verifikasi isi dokumen guna memastikan bahwa tidak ada perubahan signifikan sejak dokumen tersebut dibuat. Penanggung Jawab wajib menandatangani dokumen untuk mengonfirmasi bahwa proses verifikasi telah dilaksanakan sesuai dengan standar yang berlaku).*

#### 6.10 Post Job Review {#np-09-6-10-post-job-review}
It is essential that lessons learnt from the performance of jobs are captured and fed back to the staff involved and into the task Risk Assessment process, so that the knowledge is captured for next time. It is the responsibility of the Work Leader to identify the level of feedback required. This could range from a full task de-brief with the team through to the addition of a few notes to the task Risk Assessment which could benefit the next Work Leader when assessing the task.

*(Sangat penting untuk memastikan bahwa pelajaran yang diperoleh dari pelaksanaan pekerjaan terdokumentasi dan disampaikan kembali kepada personel yang terlibat, serta dimasukkan ke dalam proses Risk Assessment pekerjaan, sehingga pengetahuan tersebut dapat dimanfaatkan untuk pelaksanaan berikutnya.*
*Work Leader bertanggung jawab untuk menentukan tingkat umpan balik yang diperlukan. Bentuk umpan balik ini dapat berupa de-briefing lengkap bersama tim hingga penambahan beberapa catatan pada Risk Assessment pekerjaan, yang dapat memberikan manfaat bagi Work Leader berikutnya ketika melakukan penilaian terhadap pekerjaan tersebut).*

#### 6.11 Record Retention {#np-09-6-11-record-retention}
A copy of the PRAC should be kept for at least 1 year and archived with the work permit for a further 5 years. Risk Assessment is to be stored electronically in discreet asset / function folders.

*(Salinan PRAC harus disimpan setidaknya selama 1 tahun dan diarsipkan bersama Work Permit selama 5 tahun berikutnya. Risk Assessment wajib disimpan secara elektronik dalam folder aset/fungsi yang terpisah).*

### 7. OTHER RISK ASSESSMENT TYPES {#np-09-7-other-risk-assessment-types}
The JSA process outlined in this procedure is expected to be sufficient for the majority of the tasks carried out at LNG NIAS GASIFIKASI Regas facilities. For example, a task, which involves some manual handling, some chemicals, PPE and noise, can usually be covered on the one Risk Assessment. If an assessment already exists it can be attached to the PRAC. Outlined below is guidance on when an additional assessment may be needed.

*(Proses JSA yang dijelaskan dalam prosedur ini diharapkan sudah memadai untuk sebagian besar pekerjaan yang dilakukan di fasilitas LNG NIAS GASIFIKASI Regas, sebagai contoh, suatu pekerjaan yang melibatkan manual handling, penggunaan bahan kimia, APD, dan paparan kebisingan, biasanya dapat dicakup dalam satu Risk Assessment, apabila penilaian risiko sudah tersedia, maka dokumen tersebut dapat dilampirkan pada PRAC).*

#### 7.1 Hazardous Chemical or Material {#np-09-7-1-hazardous-chemical-or-material}
All tasks using hazardous chemicals or materials require a risk/task Assessment to be completed. Chemical Task Assessments should be carried out with a qualified person, as require additional skills not provided during Risk Assessment training.
The Assessment form shown in Appendix E will be used for carrying out these assessments. Chemical Task Assessments should be reviewed every 12 months.

*(Seluruh pekerjaan yang menggunakan bahan kimia berbahaya atau material berbahaya wajib dilengkapi dengan Risk/Task Assessment.*
*Chemical Task Assessment harus dilakukan bersama personel yang berkualifikasi, karena penilaian ini memerlukan keahlian tambahan yang tidak diberikan dalam pelatihan Risk Assessment.*
*Formulir Assessment yang ditunjukkan pada Lampiran E akan digunakan untuk melaksanakan penilaian tersebut. Chemical Task Assessment wajib ditinjau ulang setiap 12 bulan).*

#### 7.2 Manual Handling {#np-09-7-2-manual-handling}
Although many jobs will have a Manual Handling element to them, only those jobs with a significant amount of manual handling will require a separate manual handling assessment. One example is moving baggage from a helicopter where significant, repetitive, manual handling is involved. Generally, the Risk Assessment can cover Manual Handling aspects of a job with only a few specific tasks requiring a full Manual Handling Assessment. The results from these should be used to determine whether the task should go ahead or whether further controls are required.

*(Meskipun banyak pekerjaan memiliki unsur Manual Handling, hanya pekerjaan yang melibatkan manual handling dalam jumlah signifikan yang memerlukan penilaian manual handling terpisah, sebagai contoh, pemindahan bagasi dari helikopter yang melibatkan manual handling berulang dalam jumlah besar.*
*Secara umum, Risk Assessment sudah dapat mencakup aspek Manual Handling dalam suatu pekerjaan, dengan hanya beberapa tugas spesifik yang memerlukan Manual Handling Assessment secara penuh, hasil dari penilaian tersebut harus digunakan untuk menentukan apakah pekerjaan dapat dilanjutkan atau apakah diperlukan pengendalian tambahan).*

#### 7.3 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) {#np-09-7-3-personal-protective-equipment-ppe}
The PRAC/Risk Assessment will give some indication of the level of the hazard effect involved. From this the performance and selection of the PPE can be assessed and included in the PRAC/Risk Assessment. For example, a splashing risk will indicate goggles or visor are needed as opposed to spectacles.
Similarly, for Respiratory Protective Equipment (RPE), the type of chemical and level of risk involved will give a good indication as to the type of respirator needed. This will also be covered under the assessment for chemical tasks.
Only when a more complex assessment of PPE is needed is the Risk Assessment process insufficient. It is expected that specialists in the area of PPE being assessed will be utilised in carrying out the Risk Assessment.

*(PRAC/Risk Assessment akan memberikan indikasi mengenai tingkat dampak bahaya yang terlibat. Dari sini, kinerja dan pemilihan Alat Pelindung Diri (APD) dapat dinilai serta dicantumkan dalam PRAC/Risk Assessment, sebagai contoh, risiko percikan akan menunjukkan perlunya penggunaan goggle atau visor dibandingkan dengan kacamata biasa.*
*Demikian pula, untuk Respiratory Protective Equipment (RPE), jenis bahan kimia dan tingkat risiko yang terlibat akan memberikan gambaran jelas mengenai jenis respirator yang dibutuhkan. Hal ini juga akan dicakup dalam assessment untuk pekerjaan dengan bahan kimia.*
*Hanya ketika penilaian APD yang lebih kompleks diperlukan, proses Risk Assessment menjadi tidak memadai. Dalam hal ini, diharapkan spesialis di bidang APD yang dinilai dilibatkan dalam pelaksanaan Risk Assessment).*

#### 7.4 Noise {#np-09-7-4-noise}
A Noise Assessment is required when noise levels of 85dB(A) for 8 hours exposure or 83dB(A) for 12 hours exposure occurs. This is a comprehensive assessment identifying the high noise areas and control measures needed. Trained personnel therefore carry out Noise Assessments, and a full written report identifying the noisy areas is provided. The noise element expected in a JSA is therefore at a relatively low level, e.g. identification of noise as a hazard and of those ear defenders that will provide suitable control. If the PRAC indicates that a more detailed Noise Assessment should be carried out then this should be referred to the HSE Advisor. For example, ear defenders may allow the job to go ahead but a more permanent form of noise reduction may need to be found.

*(Noise Assessment diperlukan apabila tingkat kebisingan mencapai 85 dB(A) untuk paparan 8 jam atau 83 dB(A) untuk paparan 12 jam. Penilaian ini merupakan assessment komprehensif yang mengidentifikasi area dengan tingkat kebisingan tinggi serta langkah-langkah pengendalian yang diperlukan. Oleh karena itu, Noise Assessment hanya dapat dilakukan oleh personel yang terlatih, dan harus disertai laporan tertulis lengkap yang mengidentifikasi area bising.*
*Unsur kebisingan yang diharapkan tercakup dalam JSA umumnya berada pada tingkat yang relatif rendah, misalnya identifikasi kebisingan sebagai potensi bahaya serta penentuan jenis pelindung telinga (ear defenders) yang sesuai sebagai langkah pengendalian.*
*Apabila PRAC menunjukkan bahwa diperlukan Noise Assessment lebih detail, maka hal ini harus dirujuk kepada HSE Advisor. Sebagai contoh, pelindung telinga mungkin memungkinkan pekerjaan tetap dilaksanakan, tetapi bentuk pengendalian kebisingan yang lebih permanen mungkin perlu ditemukan).*

#### 7.5 Computer Workstations {#np-09-7-5-computer-workstations}
All workstations should be assessed, and health and safety risks to personnel minimised. Where work with computer screens forms only part of the task and is not a major part then the Risk Assessment form can be used to cover this requirement.
Where work with display screens is a substantial part of the job, e.g. continuous use of more than 1 hour every day, then a ‘self-assessment’ of the workstation by the user needs to be carried out. An assessment must also be completed if any workstation changes occur. The Information System (IS) department will provide guidance.

*(Seluruh workstation harus dinilai (assessed) untuk memastikan risiko kesehatan dan keselamatan terhadap personel dapat diminimalkan, apabila pekerjaan dengan layar komputer hanya merupakan bagian kecil dari tugas dan bukan bagian utama, maka Formulir Risk Assessment dapat digunakan untuk memenuhi persyaratan ini.*
*Namun, apabila pekerjaan dengan display screen merupakan bagian substansial dari pekerjaan, misalnya penggunaan terus-menerus lebih dari 1 jam setiap hari, maka diperlukan self-assessment workstation oleh pengguna. Assessment juga wajib dilakukan apabila terjadi perubahan pada workstation. Departemen Information System (IS) akan memberikan panduan terkait pelaksanaannya).*

#### 7.6 Provision and Use of Work Equipment {#np-09-7-6-provision-and-use-of-work-equipment}
Work equipment should not give rise to health and safety problems irrespective of its age or place of origin. Carrying out a Risk Assessment will identify all the proactive and preventative measures that have to be taken to comply with these regulations.
If a more detailed Risk Assessment is required, e.g. for the selection of a new piece of equipment, then the HSE Advisor should be consulted.
All work equipment on the asset which poses a significant risk should be assessed using the form in Appendix G, large pieces of equipment i.e. lathe, drills etc. will require their own form. A record of the assessments should be maintained on the asset and reviewed yearly. Personal hand tools need to be inspected as per the HSE Standards Manual.

*(Peralatan kerja tidak boleh menimbulkan masalah kesehatan dan keselamatan, tanpa memandang usia maupun asal peralatan tersebut. Pelaksanaan Risk Assessment akan mengidentifikasi seluruh langkah proaktif dan pencegahan yang harus dilakukan untuk memastikan kepatuhan terhadap regulasi.*
*Apabila diperlukan Risk Assessment yang lebih detail, misalnya dalam proses pemilihan peralatan baru, maka HSE Advisor harus dikonsultasikan.*
*Seluruh peralatan kerja di aset yang memiliki risiko signifikan harus dinilai menggunakan Formulir di Lampiran G. Peralatan berukuran besar, misalnya mesin bubut, bor, dan sebagainya, harus memiliki formulir penilaian tersendiri.*
*Catatan hasil assessment wajib dipelihara di lokasi aset dan ditinjau ulang setiap tahun. Untuk peralatan tangan pribadi, inspeksi harus dilakukan sesuai dengan ketentuan pada HSE Standards Manual).*

#### 7.7 Lead Exposure {#np-09-7-7-lead-exposure}
This Risk Assessment procedure does not cover lead and specialist advice from HSE Jakarta should be sought for any tasks involving lead exposure.

*(Prosedur Risk Assessment ini tidak mencakup pekerjaan yang berhubungan dengan paparan timbal (lead), dan untuk setiap tugas yang melibatkan paparan timbal harus meminta saran khusus dari HSE Jakarta).*

#### 7.8 Confined Space Entry {#np-09-7-8-confined-space-entry}
Where confined space entry is required, the risk should be assessed on the PRAC/Risk Assessment form using the ‘Confined Space Risk Model’ (Appendix F) as guidance to identify the specific hazards that could be involved in confined space entry.

*(Apabila pekerjaan mengharuskan masuk ke ruang terbatas (confined space), maka risiko harus dinilai pada formulir PRAC/Risk Assessment dengan menggunakan Confined Space Risk Model (Lampiran F) sebagai panduan untuk mengidentifikasi potensi bahaya spesifik yang mungkin timbul dalam aktivitas masuk ruang terbatas tersebut).*

#### 7.9 Lifting Operations {#np-09-7-9-lifting-operations}
Lifting Operations and Lifting Equipment require all tasks to be assessed using a PRAC/Risk Assessment Form.

*(Seluruh kegiatan Lifting Operations dan penggunaan Lifting Equipment wajib dinilai menggunakan Formulir PRAC/Risk Assessment).*

### 8. COMPLIANCE {#np-09-8-compliance}
This Risk Assessment Procedure complies with Indonesian K3/Migas regulations and international standards (ISO 45001). It ensures that hazards are identified, risks are assessed, mitigation is applied, and controls are implemented before any work begins.
All jobs must go through JSA and Permit to Work. PRAC/Risk Assessments must be signed, revalidated if changes occur, and stored as required.
Assessments are conducted by trained teams and cover specific tasks such as chemicals, manual handling, noise, confined space, lifting, and equipment. Lessons learned must be documented for continuous improvement.

Any actual or suspected breach of this Procedure and its overall complementary Procedure shall be reported to Compliance Committee, who will report to the President Director.
Failure to comply with this Procedure may result in disciplinary actions against the relevant Personnel.
Any Personnel found not in compliance with this Procedure and its overall complementary Procedure will be subjected to sanctions as set forth in Code of Conduct (CoC) and Company Regulation.
Any exception to this Procedure shall require approval from President Director, Operation Director and Technical Director.

*(Prosedur Risk Assessment ini sesuai dengan regulasi K3/Migas Indonesia dan standar internasional (ISO 45001). Prosedur ini memastikan bahwa bahaya diidentifikasi, risiko dinilai, mitigasi diterapkan, dan pengendalian dilaksanakan sebelum pekerjaan dimulai.*
*Seluruh pekerjaan wajib melalui JSA dan Permit to Work. PRAC/Risk Assessment harus ditandatangani, divalidasi ulang bila ada perubahan, serta disimpan sesuai ketentuan.*
*Penilaian dilakukan oleh tim yang terlatih dan mencakup pekerjaan khusus seperti bahan kimia, manual handling, kebisingan, ruang terbatas, lifting, dan peralatan. Lessons learned wajib dicatat untuk perbaikan berkelanjutan.*
*Setiap pelanggaran nyata atau dugaan pelanggaran terhadap Prosedur ini dan Prosedur pelengkapnya harus dilaporkan kepada Komite Kepatuhan (Compliance Committee), yang selanjutnya akan melaporkannya kepada Presiden Direktur.*
*Kegagalan dalam mematuhi Prosedur ini dapat mengakibatkan tindakan disipliner terhadap Personel terkait. Personel yang terbukti tidak mematuhi Prosedur ini maupun Prosedur pelengkapnya akan dikenakan sanksi sesuai dengan Code of Conduct (CoC) dan Peraturan Perusahaan.*
*Setiap pengecualian terhadap Prosedur ini harus mendapatkan persetujuan dari Presiden Direktur, Direktur Operasi, dan Direktur Teknik).*

## NP09-02 Risk Management Procedure {#np-09-np09-02-risk-management-procedure}

### 1. PURPOSE AND CYCLE {#np-09-2-1-purpose-and-cycle}
To establish a systematic process for identifying, assessing, controlling, and monitoring health, safety, environmental, and operational risks associated with all activities at the mini-LNG regasification facility. The goal is to eliminate or reduce risks to a level that is As Low As Reasonably Practicable (ALARP).
This procedure follows a continuous cycle: **Identify → Assess → Control → Monitor & Review**

### 2. PHASE 1: RISK ASSESSMENT {#np-09-2-2-phase-1-risk-assessment}

#### 2.1 Step 1: Hazard Identification {#np-09-2-2-1-step-1-hazard-identification}
Hazard identification using methods as follows:
- **Process Hazard Analysis (PHA):** A formal HAZOP (Hazard and Operability Study) for the entire process.
- **Job Safety Analysis (JSA):** For routine tasks (e.g., truck unloading, filter maintenance).
- **Management of Change (MOC):** For all modifications to equipment, processes, or high level personnel.
- **Workplace Inspections & Audits:** Daily walks, formal weekly inspections.
- **Incident & Near-Miss Investigation:** To identify underlying causes.
- **Hazard Reporting:** An open culture where all employees report hazards.

##### Common LNG Hazards {#np-09-2-2-1-common-lng-hazards}
- **Cryogenic Leaks:** LNG release causing frostbite, material embrittlement, and pool formation.
- **Flammable Gas Releases:** Forming explosive vapor clouds (potentially with Rapid Phase Transition - RPT over water).
- **Fire & Explosion:** Jet fires, pool fires, flash fires, and vapor cloud explosions (VCE).
- **Overpressure/Underpressure:** Of storage tanks and process piping.
- **Asphyxiation:** From nitrogen purging or displacing oxygen with natural gas.
- **Physical Hazards:** High-pressure systems, noise, rotating equipment.

#### 2.2 Step 2: Risk Analysis & Evaluation (The Risk Matrix) {#np-09-2-2-2-step-2-risk-analysis-evaluation-the-risk-matrix}

##### Severity Rating Scale (Consequence) {#np-09-2-2-2-severity-rating-scale-consequence}
| Score | Personnel Safety & Health | Environmental Impact | Asset/Financial Impact | Reputation Impact |
| ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| **5 - Catastrophic** | Fatality or permanent total disability. | Major release with severe, long-term environmental damage. Regulatory shutdown. | > $1,000,000; Total loss of facility. | National media attention, major legal charges, loss of license to operate. |
| **4 - Major** | Severe injury, permanent partial disability. | Significant release requiring external cleanup and major regulatory reporting. | $100,000 - $1,000,000; Major equipment loss. | Regional media attention, significant regulatory fines. |
| **3 - Moderate** | Lost Time Injury (LTI). | Localized containment failure, reportable release to authorities. | $10,000 - $100,000; Significant repair needed. | Local media attention, complaints from local community. |
| **2 - Minor** | Medical Treatment Case (MTC) or First Aid. | Minor spill contained on-site, minor cleanup. | < $10,000; Minor equipment damage. | Internal investigation, no external attention. |
| **1 - Negligible** | No injury or minor first aid. | No impact or negligible spill. | Negligible repair cost. | No impact. |

##### Likelihood Rating Scale (Probability) {#np-09-2-2-2-likelihood-rating-scale-probability}
| Score | Descriptor | Frequency Definition | Example for Mini-LNG |
| ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| **5 - Almost Certain** | Expected to occur | Once per year or more | Minor LNG spill during routine truck unloading. |
| **4 - Likely** | Will probably occur | Once every 1-3 years | Failure of a pump seal causing a localized leak. |
| **3 - Possible** | Might occur at some time | Once every 3-10 years | Overpressure event contained by a safety valve. |
| **2 - Unlikely** | Could happen but rare | Once in 10-30 years | Major leak from a primary process line. |
| **1 - Rare** | May occur only in exceptional circumstances | Less than once in 30 years | Vapor Cloud Explosion (VCE) with blast effects. |

##### Risk Matrix Table (5x5) {#np-09-2-2-2-risk-matrix-table-5x5}
Calculate **Risk Score = Severity x Likelihood**. This score determines the priority and required action.

| Likelihood \ Severity | 1 (Negligible) | 2 (Minor) | 3 (Moderate) | 4 (Major) | 5 (Catastrophic) |
| ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| **5 (Almost Certain)** | 5 (Medium) | 10 (High) | 15 (Extreme) | 20 (Extreme) | 25 (Extreme) |
| **4 (Likely)** | 4 (Low) | 8 (Medium) | 12 (High) | 16 (Extreme) | 20 (Extreme) |
| **3 (Possible)** | 3 (Low) | 6 (Medium) | 9 (High) | 12 (High) | 15 (Extreme) |
| **2 (Unlikely)** | 2 (Low) | 4 (Low) | 6 (Medium) | 8 (High) | 10 (High) |
| **1 (Rare)** | 1 (Low) | 2 (Low) | 3 (Low) | 4 (Low) | 5 (Medium) |

##### Risk Acceptance Criteria {#np-09-2-2-2-risk-acceptance-criteria}
- **EXTREME (15 - 25): UNACCEPTABLE.** Immediate action required to mitigate risk. Operations MUST NOT commence or must cease until adequate controls are implemented. Requires senior management intervention and a detailed risk reduction plan.
- **HIGH (8 - 12): UNDESIRABLE.** Senior management attention needed. Specific mitigation plans with defined responsibilities and timelines must be established. Requires temporary controls until permanent solutions are in place.
- **MEDIUM (4 - 6): TOLERABLE.** Must be managed by specific procedures and controls. Responsibility for monitoring must be assigned. Efforts to reduce risk further should be evaluated based on cost/benefit (ALARP principle).
- **LOW (1 - 3): ACCEPTABLE.** Managed by routine procedures. No additional controls are typically required, but monitoring is necessary to ensure the risk level does not increase.

### 3. PHASE 2: RISK CONTROL AND MITIGATION {#np-09-2-3-phase-2-risk-control-and-mitigation}

#### Hierarchy of Controls {#np-09-2-3-hierarchy-of-controls}
1. **Elimination:** Physically remove the hazard. *(Example: Design facility without complex piping by using modular integrated skids).*
2. **Substitution:** Replace the hazard with a less hazardous alternative. *(Example: Using less hazardous chemicals for cleaning).*
3. **Engineering Controls:** Isolate people from the hazard. *(Example: Pressure Safety Valves (PSVs), gas detection systems with automatic ESD, firewalls, blast walls, double-walled pipes, containment dikes).*
4. **Administrative Controls:** Change the way people work. *(Example: Permits to Work (PTW), Lockout-Tagout (LOTO) procedures, detailed SOPs, emergency drills, comprehensive training).*
5. **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** Protect the worker with personal equipment. *(Example: FR Clothing, cryogenic gloves, safety glasses, SCBA).*

### 4. PHASE 3: MONITORING, REVIEW, AND CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT {#np-09-2-4-phase-3-monitoring-review-and-continuous-improvement}
- **Performance Monitoring:**
  - *Leading Indicators:* Gas detector alarms responded to, PTW audits completed, training compliance %, safety observations reported.
  - *Lagging Indicators:* TRIR, Lost Time Injuries, releases of product.
- **Formal Review:** Entire Risk Register reviewed annually by HSE & Operations Manager. Specific risk assessment re-done after major incidents or MOC.
- **Audit & Verification:** Internal and external audits conducted to verify controls are in place and effective.

### 5. IDENTIFYING NEW RISKS AND NEAR-MISSES {#np-09-2-5-identifying-new-risks-and-near-misses}
- **On-Site Reporting:** Personnel who find new risks or near-misses shall report to Safety & Environmental Officer orally or in writing.
- **Investigation Report:** SSHQE Officer records all risks/near-misses and reports to Site Manager, who draws "Form Incident Accident Investigation Report (NP11-01)" to submit to Company.
- **Addition to Risk Register:** Team leader of SYSMAN reviews Form NP11-01 and identifies objects for risk assessment.

### PPE Definitions and Specific Requirements {#np-09-app-01-ppe-definitions-and-specific-requirements}
- **FR (Flame Resistant) Clothing:** Must cover torso, arms, and legs. Cotton or inherent FR material. NO synthetic materials (nylon, polyester) in process areas.
- **Cryogenic Gloves:** Loose-fitting, designed to protect against temperatures down to -162°C. NOT liquid-proof. Designed to shed cryogenic liquid quickly without absorbing it.
- **Face Shield:** Must be used IN ADDITION TO safety glasses, not as a replacement. Provides full-face protection from splashes.
- **Hearing Protection:** Required in areas where 8-hour TWA exceeds 85 dBA or 12-hour TWA exceeds 83 dBA.

## Appendix 03. Flow Chart Risk Assessment Procedure {#np-09-app-03-flow-chart-risk-assessment-procedure}

### Risk Assessment Workflow Steps {#np-09-app-03-risk-assessment-workflow-steps}
- **Stage 1:** Originator/Responsible Person assesses hazard using the PRAC Process. Determines if Full Risk Assessment is required.
- **Stage 2:** Responsible Person selects a suitable Assessment Team (minimum 2 persons, led by trained Team Leader).
- **Stage 3:** Team carries out Risk Assessment of work activities using standard JSA Form.
- **Stage 4:** If risks are acceptable, process proceeds. If not acceptable, Responsible Person reassesses activities, procedures, and risks to reduce risk to acceptable level.
- **Stage 5:** If risks cannot be reduced to ALARP, Responsible Person refers issue to Facility Manager / OIM, who refers to HSE Jakarta.
- **Stage 6:** Responsible Person reviews Permit, attaches PRAC/Risk Assessment, and briefs Work Leader on main points on PJSM.
- **Stage 7:** Authorizer reviews Permit and Risk Assessment for approval.
- **Stage 8:** Approved Permit and Risk Assessment issued to Work Leader for activity implementation.
- **Stage 9:** If PTW is not required, Responsible Person briefs Work Leader on main points written on PJSM form.
- **Stage 10:** Work Leader briefs workforce on hazards and risk reduction measures at PJSM prior to work execution.
- **Stage 11:** All workforce members sign PJSM Sheet to confirm risks/measures are understood. Work activities proceed. Post Job Review captured upon completion.

- **Phase 2 (ESD Activation & Response):**
  - Immediate Activation Triggers: Manual E-Stop button, high gas detection (>60% LEL), fire detection, or low line pressure.
  - Logic Sequence: a) Close skid inlet valve, b) Close tank outlet valve, c) Stop unloading pump, d) Initiate plant emergency alarm.
