## 1. LNG Trucking ISO Tanks Operational Guideline {#np-03-1-0-operational-guideline}

### Executive Summary {#np-03-1-0-1-executive-summary}
This document serves as a comprehensive Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for the operation of a Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) regasification system. The procedure is designed to provide a framework for the safe, reliable, and efficient conversion of cryogenic LNG into a gaseous state suitable for distribution through pipeline networks. The protocols detailed herein are founded on a meticulous analysis of industry best practices and mandated regulatory standards, including NFPA 59A and API 625. The SOP establishes clear guidelines for all operational phases, from startup to emergency response, and defines the roles and responsibilities required to maintain continuous compliance, operational excellence, and a robust safety culture. This report is intended to function as both a strategic reference for management and a practical, actionable guide for all operational personnel.

### 3.1 Purpose & Objectives {#np-03-1-1-purpose-and-objectives}
This procedure provides standardized operational guidelines for the safe and efficient transportation of Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) using ISO tanks via road trucks. It ensures all activities loading, transportation and unloading are conducted with strict adherence to safety, environmental protection, and equipment reliability.

### 3.2 Scope & Applicability {#np-03-1-2-scope-and-applicability}
This SOP applies to all LNG trucking and ISO tank operations, including loading at LNG terminals, transportation by road, and unloading at receiving stations or regasification units.

### 3.3 Roles & Responsibilities {#np-03-1-3-roles-and-responsibilities}
Clear definition of roles and responsibilities is paramount to ensuring safe and efficient operations. In a high-stakes environment, unambiguous lines of authority and accountability are critical for preventing procedural errors and ensuring swift, coordinated action during an emergency. The following matrix outlines the specific duties for each key role within the LNG regasification facility.

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Site Manager | Approves the SOP and all subsequent revisions. Possesses final authority for critical operational decisions during emergencies and planned outages. |
| Operation Leader | Directs daily operations. Acts as the primary liaison between the Control Room and field personnel. Ensures full compliance with this SOP. May assume the role of Incident Commander during an emergency until relieved by an external authority. |
| Control Room Operator | Manages the regasification process via the Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) or Distributed Control System (DCS). Monitors all operating parameters and initiates procedural steps, including emergency shutdowns. |
| Field Operator | Executes physical tasks as instructed, including valve operations, equipment monitoring, and local safety checks. Reports all anomalies, alarms, and equipment statuses to the Control Room Operator. |
| E&I Technician | Conducts maintenance, calibration, and repair of all electrical and instrumentation equipment. Verifies the functionality and communication of all sensors, control systems, and safety systems. |
| HSE Officer | Enforces safety protocols and the use of proper Personal Protective Equipment (PPE). Conducts routine safety inspections and audits. Leads and documents safety training and emergency response drills. |
| Driver/Operator | Conducts inspection, loading, transport and unloading |

### 3.4 Definitions & Acronyms {#np-03-1-4-definitions-and-acronyms}
* **LNG (Liquefied Natural Gas):** Natural gas that has been cooled to its liquid state at approximately −162°C, which reduces its volume by about 600 times, making it efficient to store and transport.
* **Regasification:** The process of converting LNG from its liquid state back to a gaseous state by adding heat.
* **ISO Tank:** A cryogenic storage vessel designed to hold Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG) at approximately −162°C. It serves as the primary containment unit for LNG prior to regasification.
* **PBU (Pressure Build-Up Unit):** An externally installed unit, independent of the ISO tank, designed to maintain stable supply pressure for LNG vaporization. When the system detects a drop in pressure, the PBU vaporizes LNG and circulates the gas to the top of the ISO tank to sustain a supply pressure of 11.7 barg to the vaporizer. This ensures consistent feed conditions for regasification without relying on internal tank mechanisms.
* **AAV:** Ambient Air Vaporizer. A type of vaporizer that uses atmospheric heat to convert LNG to gas. This is often considered an eco-friendly option.
* **NG Buffer Tank:** A pressurized vessel that stores regasified natural gas and stabilizes outlet pressure above 10 barg. It also recycles gas back to the ISO tank send-out line when pressure drops below threshold.
* **Gas Metering System:** An integrated system for measuring and analyzing natural gas flow and composition.
* **ESD:** Emergency Shutdown. An integrated system that automatically or manually isolates LNG flow and shuts down equipment to bring the facility to a safe state during a critical event.
* **BOG:** Boil-Off Gas. Natural gas that vaporizes from the liquid LNG due to heat ingress, particularly in storage tanks. Managing BOG is crucial to prevent pressure build-up within the storage system.

### 3.5 Safety Requirements {#np-03-1-5-safety-requirements}
* **Mandatory PPE:** Cryogenic gloves, anti-static FR coverall, helmet, face shield, safety goggles, safety boots.
* **Truck Safety Devices:** ESOV, PRV, grounding cable, gas detector, fire extinguisher (dry powder & CO₂ type).
* **Area Safety:** 15 m exclusion zone, no open flame, no smoking, no ignition sources.
* **Inspection:** All safety valves, hoses, and instruments must be inspected daily before use.

### 3.6 Operational Procedures {#np-03-1-6-operational-procedures}
#### Pre-Operation Check {#np-03-1-6-1-pre-operation-check}
1. Verify all permits, tank certificates, and driver competency.
2. Check tank pressure, valve condition, and insulation integrity.
3. Ensure vehicle brake, lighting, and communication systems function.
4. Ensure grounding and fire extinguisher are in ready condition.

#### Loading LNG {#np-03-1-6-2-loading-lng}
1. Park truck in designated loading bay.
2. Apply wheel chocks and connect grounding cable.
3. Perform pre-cooling of hose line.
4. Begin transfer slowly, monitoring temperature and pressure.
5. Fill up to 95% of tank capacity.
6. Disconnect hose, seal valves, and record data.

#### Unloading LNG {#np-03-1-6-3-unloading-lng}
1. Maintain steady driving speed (≤50 km/h).
2. Avoid rough roads, sharp turns, and sudden braking.
3. Monitor tank pressure through onboard instruments.
4. Stop immediately if leakage or high pressure occurs.

#### Shutdown Procedure {#np-03-1-6-4-shutdown-procedure}
1. Stop operations immediately upon detecting gas leak or fire.
2. Activate ESD and emergency alarm.
3. Evacuate area within a minimum radius of 100 meters.
4. Contact emergency response team and local authorities.

### 3.7 Training {#np-03-1-7-training}
Personnel shall be trained in:
* LNG properties and cryogenic safety.
* ISO tank operation and maintenance.
* Emergency response procedures.
* Firefighting and first aid.
* Refresher training: every 2 years.

### 3.8 Documentation {#np-03-1-8-documentation}
All operational and safety records shall be maintained, including:
* Pre- and post-operation checklists.
* Loading/unloading logs.
* Maintenance reports.
* Training and certification records.
* Retention period: minimum 3 years.

## 2. LNG Trucking Security Guideline {#np-03-2-0-security-guideline}

### 2.1 Purpose {#np-03-2-1-purpose}
Establish comprehensive and detailed security procedures for LNG (Liquefied Natural Gas) transportation using tank trucks/ISO tanks so that transportation activities are carried out safely, in a controlled manner, in accordance with the law, and are able to prevent criminal acts, sabotage, terrorism, and the potential for loss/damage to cargo.

### 2.2 Scope {#np-03-2-2-scope}
This SOP applies to all LNG transportation activities covering:
* **Chain of activities:** route planning, vehicle preparation, filling at origin, travel, transit, unloading, and return to depot.
* **Parties involved:** drivers, dispatchers, loading/unloading operators, security officers (escorts), operations managers, security managers, and emergency response teams.
* **Coverage area:** all domestic and cross-border land routes in accordance with national and international regulations.
* **Limitations:** does not include detailed engineering (valve installation, tank technical repairs) that comply with technical HSE SOP.

### 2.3 Definitions {#np-03-2-3-definitions}
| Definition | Description |
| --- | --- |
| LNG (Liquefied Natural Gas) | Natural gas cooled to approximately -160°C, turning it into a liquid. LNG is colorless, odorless, non-corrosive, and has a volume approximately 1/600th that of normal gas. The cryogenic nature of LNG can potentially cause frostbite if it comes into direct contact with the skin. |
| ISO Tank / Truk Tangki | International standard tank (ISO 1496/3) specially designed to transport cryogenic materials such as LNG. Has a double vacuum insulation layer, safety valve, manifold, and pressure and temperature monitoring system. Mounted on a standard frame so it can be transported by truck, ship or train. |

| Dispatcher (Petugas Pengendali Logistik) | Control center personnel monitor vehicle movements via GPS and regular communications. They are responsible for creating schedules, managing routes, recording driver check-ins, activating the ERP during incidents, and liaising between drivers, management, and security authorities. |
| Security Escort | Security guards (armed or unarmed) are assigned to accompany vehicles on high-risk routes. They serve as an additional layer of protection, assist with threat detection, and coordinate with drivers and dispatchers in the event of potential crime. |

| ERT (Emergency Response Team) | An emergency response team consisting of trained personnel (HSE, security, and technical) to handle incidents such as leaks, traffic accidents, fires, or security threats. ERT coordinates directly with dispatchers and local authorities to mitigate incidents. |

### 2.6 Procedure {#np-03-2-6-procedure}
#### Route Planning {#np-03-2-6-1-route-planning}
* **Route Risk Assessment:** Logistics and security teams conduct a comprehensive evaluation of the route to identify crime-prone areas (deserted roads, unofficial rest areas, history of hijacking) and non-security disruptions (demonstrations, natural disasters, damaged roads, construction projects).
* **Preparation of Main & Alternative Routes:** Primary routes are determined based on safety, travel time, and access to emergency facilities. Alternative routes must be prepared for emergencies. All routes are equipped with official rest stops and verified safe gas stations.
* **Driver Check:** Every driver must have valid documents (B2 General Driving License, B3 Driving Certificate, ID Card) and undergo a health check including alcohol and drug tests. Drivers with positive results or fatigue are prohibited from departing.
* **Travel Documents:** All legal documents must be available (cargo manifest, delivery note, hazardous cargo permit, chain-of-custody form, emergency contact list).
* **Escort Determination:** Escort vehicles staffed by security personnel or internal security are deployed for high-risk routes, maintaining 100–200 m distance from the truck.

#### Pre-Trip Check {#np-03-2-6-2-pre-trip-check}
* **Vehicle Inspection by Driver & Mechanic:**
  * Brakes, tires (no excessive wear, correct pressure), suspension, lights, horn, wipers.
  * Engine oil, radiator, fuel level.
  * ISO LNG Tank: verify no leaks, abnormal frost buildup, or damage to valve and dome cover.
* **Mandatory Equipment Inspection:**
  * Fire extinguishers (at least 2 units, CO₂/dry chemical type).
  * Spill kits for minor leaks.
  * Traffic cones & safety triangles for emergency stops.
  * Active GPS, radio communication, satellite phone (optional).
* **Document Check:** Manifest, travel document, B3 transport permit, driver's license, ID card verified by dispatcher.
* **Security Seal:** Manifolds, valves, and dome covers sealed by authorized personnel; seal numbers recorded in Seal Log.
* **Dispatcher Validation:** Departure authorization issued by dispatcher before vehicle leaves.

#### Loading LNG at Origin {#np-03-2-6-3-loading-lng-at-origin}
* **Access Control:** Restricted to authorized personnel with complete PPE (helmet, cryogenic gloves, protective goggles).
* **Area Security Check:** CCTV and fire suppression active; perimeter sterile.
* **LNG Filling:** ISO tanks filled to maximum 90% capacity to allow room for LNG expansion; pressure & temperature monitored.
* **Seal & Documentation:** Seal applied after filling; volume, temperature, and pressure recorded in handover document for driver and dispatcher.

#### Departure & Transit {#np-03-2-6-4-departure-and-transit}
* **Check-Out:** Driver reports location, ETA, and seal condition to dispatcher.
* **Check-In Rules:** Mandatory check-in every 1 hour, when entering/exiting major cities, and during official rest stops.
* **Prohibitions:** Additional passengers prohibited; random stopping prohibited; unauthorized seal opening prohibited.
* **Escort Rules:** Maintain 100–200 m distance, focusing on high-risk or heavy traffic zones.

#### Travel Monitoring {#np-03-2-6-5-travel-monitoring}
* **GPS Monitoring:** Active 24/7. Deviation >1 km sends automatic alarm.
* **Deviation Follow-up:** Dispatcher contacts driver within 2 minutes. If no response, lost contact procedure activated.
* **Automatic Logging:** All communications recorded for audit & investigation.

#### Official Checkpoint / Authority Inspection {#np-03-2-6-6-official-checkpoint}
* **Obligations:** Driver submits manifest, waybill, driving license, and B3 permit.
* **Seal Opened by Authorities:** Record officer's name, institution, time, old seal number, and new seal number. Dispatcher notified immediately.

#### Security Threats {#np-03-2-6-7-security-threats}
* **Driver Actions:** Do not resist; activate panic button immediately if available.
* **Dispatcher Actions:** Contact Security Manager, local police, and company ERT immediately; log details in incident log.

#### Technical Incidents / Leaks {#np-03-2-6-8-technical-incidents-leaks}
* **Small Leaks:** Stop in safe location, turn on hazard lights, place traffic cones/safety triangles, contact dispatcher & ERT.
* **Major Leak / Fire:** Evacuate within 100-meter radius, prohibit cell phones/open flames, wait for ERT/authorities.

#### Arrival & Unloading at Destination {#np-03-2-6-9-arrival-and-unloading-at-destination}
* **Arrival Confirmation:** Dispatcher confirms arrival before entry.
* **Initial Examination:** Receiving operator checks seal condition, ISO tank, and handover documents.
* **Unloading Process:** Executed by authorized operators with full PPE in sterile area; record volume, temperature, and pressure.
* **Post-Operation Check:** Close valve tightly, inspect equipment, clean area, complete handover paperwork.

#### Compliance {#np-03-2-6-10-compliance}
* **Mandatory Compliance:** Full adherence required; violations subject to disciplinary action (warning to termination).
* **Registration & Records:** All activities, check-ins, logs, and inspections recorded and stored for audits.
* **Legal & Regulatory Compliance:** Comply with national and international dangerous goods regulations.
* **Inspections:** Fleet & equipment undergo regular technical/security inspections by qualified personnel.
* **Drills & Evaluation:** Regular emergency response drills conducted and evaluated with corrective action follow-ups.

## 4. Traffic Management Plan for LNG Trucking Operation {#np-03-4-0-traffic-management-plan}

The Traffic Management Plan (TMP) for LNG Trucking Operations establishes a comprehensive framework for the safe, secure, and efficient movement of LNG road tankers within plant facilities and along approved transportation routes. This plan forms an integral part of the company's HSE Management System and supports compliance with ISO 39001 (Road Traffic Safety), NFPA 59A, and relevant MIGAS transport regulations.

### 4.1 Purpose & Objectives {#np-03-4-1-purpose-and-objectives}
This SOP defines the traffic management system and procedures to ensure safe, efficient, and controlled movement of LNG trucks within operational facilities and during road transport.

### 4.2 Scope & Applicability {#np-03-4-2-scope-and-applicability}
Applies to all LNG road transport activities using ISO tanks, including loading/unloading at terminal sites and movement within plant or port areas.
Includes:
* On-site traffic management

* Vehicle scheduling and queuing

### 4.4 Definitions & Acronyms {#np-03-4-4-definitions-and-acronyms}
* **Traffic Management Plan (TMP):** A structured approach for organizing and controlling vehicle movement to ensure safety.
* **Access Control Point (ACP):** Controlled gate where vehicle entry/exit is verified.
* **Escort Vehicle:** Security or support vehicle accompanying LNG transport.
* **Staging Area:** Designated waiting area for trucks before loading/unloading.

### 4.5 Traffic Management Requirements {#np-03-4-5-traffic-management-requirements}
* **Access Control:** All vehicle entries must be approved and recorded at Access Control Point (ACP). ID check for drivers and cargo documentation required. Escort vehicle must accompany all loaded LNG trucks inside terminal zones.
* **Vehicle Movement within Site:** Speed limit: 10 km/h inside plant, 20 km/h in open yard. Follow directional signage and one-way system. Overtaking is prohibited within plant boundary.
* **Staging and Queuing:** Staging area must be clearly marked and sufficient for vehicle queueing. Only authorized drivers allowed in cab during waiting. No engine idling beyond 10 minutes.
* **Route Management:** Route plan must be reviewed quarterly by Transport Supervisor. Avoid congested or restricted zones. Pre-approved rest and refueling stops only.
* **Signage and Marking:** Install directional, speed limit, and warning signs at key points. Paint road markings (arrows, lanes, loading bay lines) with reflective paint.
* **Parking and Loading Area:** Separate parking zones for loaded and empty LNG tanks. Emergency access lanes must remain clear at all times.

### 4.6 Traffic Flow Control {#np-03-4-6-traffic-flow-control}
Traffic flow diagrams and signs shall be displayed at entry gates and main intersections showing one-way paths, no-entry zones, and emergency exits.
Control Methods:
1. **Security Checkpoints:** Verify entry, check documents.
2. **Traffic Marshals:** Guide vehicle movement during peak hours.
3. **Radio Communication:** Coordinate entry/exit sequence.
4. **Barrier Gates:** Limit access to critical zones.

### 4.7 Emergency Traffic Control {#np-03-4-7-emergency-traffic-control}
During emergency (spill, fire, or accident), access roads must be closed immediately. Establish emergency assembly points for drivers. Prioritize emergency vehicle movement (ambulance, fire truck).

### 4.8 Training {#np-03-4-8-training}
All drivers and traffic personnel must receive training in:
* Traffic safety awareness
* Route risk management
* Emergency vehicle movement procedures

## 5. Inspection Truck and Trailer Procedure {#np-03-5-0-inspection-truck-and-trailer-procedure}

### Executive Summary {#np-03-5-0-1-executive-summary}
This SOP establishes the standardized procedure for inspecting trucks and trailers used for LNG ISO tank transport. The goal is to ensure that all vehicles and equipment meet technical, safety, and operational requirements before being released for service.

### 5.1 Purpose & Objectives {#np-03-5-1-purpose-and-objectives}
To ensure the safety, reliability, and compliance of all trucks and trailers used in LNG ISO tank operations through systematic inspection procedures.

### 5.2 Scope & Applicability {#np-03-5-2-scope-and-applicability}
This procedure applies to all transport vehicles, including tractors, trailers, and ISO tank chassis used for LNG logistics covering pre-operation, periodic and post-operation inspections.

### 5.3 Roles & Responsibilities {#np-03-5-3-roles-and-responsibilities}
| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Site Manager | Approves the SOP and all subsequent revisions. Possesses final authority for critical operational decisions during emergencies and planned outages. |
| Operation Leader | Directs daily operations. Acts as the primary liaison between the Control Room and field personnel. Ensures full compliance with this SOP. May assume the role of Incident Commander during an emergency until relieved by an external authority. |
| Transport Supervisor | Plans and approves route schedules and traffic arrangements. |
| Traffic Coordinator | Supervises traffic flow, vehicle dispatch, and staging order. |
| HSE Officer | Enforces safety protocols and the use of proper Personal Protective Equipment (PPE). Conducts routine safety inspections and audits. Leads and documents safety training and emergency response drills. |
| Driver/Operator | Conducts inspection, loading, transport and unloading. |
| Maintenance | Repair and replace defective parts. |

### 5.4 Definitions & Acronyms {#np-03-5-4-definitions-and-acronyms}
* **Periodic Inspection:** Scheduled inspection according to maintenance plan.
* **Post-Operation Inspection:** Inspection conducted after returning from operation to detect any damage.
* **Pre-Operation Inspection:** Vehicle inspection conducted before operation to ensure readiness.
* **PRV (Pressure Relief Valve):** Valve designed to release pressure when limits are exceeded.

## App. 1 Regulation Reference {#np-03-6-0-regulation-reference}
* **NFPA 59A – Standard for the Production, Storage, and Handling of Liquefied Natural Gas (LNG):** Foundational consensus standard providing minimum requirements for fire protection and safety across the lifecycle of an LNG plant.
* **API 625 – Tank Systems for Refrigerated Liquefied Gas Storage:** Overarching framework for the design and performance of LNG storage tank systems, structural design, material selection for cryogenic temperatures, insulation, and seismic loads.
* **ISO 12991 – Transportable Vacuum Insulated Vessels for LNG:** Requirements for design, construction, inspection, testing, and operation of transportable vacuum-insulated vessels for LNG.
* **ISO 16924 – Natural Gas Fuelling Stations:** Design, construction, installation, operation, maintenance, and safety requirements for LNG fuelling stations.
* **ISO 39001 – Road Traffic Safety (RTS) Management Systems:** Framework for road transport safety management systems to reduce traffic accidents.
* **ISO 31000 – Risk Management Principles and Guidelines:** Principles and guidelines for risk identification, evaluation, and management.
* **OGP Report 365 – Land Transportation Safety:** Framework for managing land transport safety in oil and gas operations.
