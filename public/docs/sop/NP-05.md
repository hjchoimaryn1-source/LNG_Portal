## Chapter 1: The General {#np-05-ch-1-the-general}

### 1.1 Purpose {#np-05-1-1-purpose}
This procedure aims to ensure the safe and uninterrupted operation of the LNG Regasification Unit (hereinafter referred to as “ORU”) by establishing standardized maintenance practices for process equipment, piping systems, instrumentation, and safety systems.

### 1.2 Definition of Terms {#np-05-1-2-definition-of-terms}
- **Critical equipment and systems**: Equipment whose failure may result in operational shutdown, safety hazards, or environmental incidents (e.g., ISO Tank, Ambient Air vaporizers, NG Buffer Tank, NG Metering Skid).
- **Other equipment**: Equipment and systems on LNG Regasification unit except clause 1.2-(1) of this chapter.
- **Periodical inspection**: Inspection conducted periodically to check the condition for the ORU & ISO Tank by means of forms provided by Organization.
- **Routine Inspection**: Visual and functional checks to verify normal operating conditions.
- **Maintenance**: Repairs or replacements performed in response to identified faults or failures.
- **Overhaul**: Renewal of spare part and rectification of deficiency.
- **Calibration**: Inspection, test or measuring performed by Experts, the member of Maintenance team.
- **Detailed test/inspection**: Detailed test/inspection such as function test, hydraulic test, measurement with the method and/or tool recommended by the maker, specialist.
- **Maintenance by Maintenance Team**: Maintenance carried out by the member of Maintenance Team on the NIAS Site.
- **Maintenance by subcontractors**: Maintenance conducting by subcontractors.
- **PMS**: Planned Maintenance System.

### 1.3 Responsibility and Authority {#np-05-1-3-responsibility-and-authority}

#### Site Manager {#np-05-1-3-site-manager}
- Shall approve long term maintenance plan & yearly maintenance plan for ORU.
- Shall review yearly budget for Site maintenance and report it to the CEO.
- Shall approve subcontractor’s maintenance for ORU.
- Shall finally review the draft letter for Major repair.
- Shall have the responsibility and authority to report the condition of equipment on ORU, and to request Organization's assistance, if necessary.
- Shall submit the data of "Yearly maintenance plan" for next year to the Organization.
- Shall approve the Monthly Maintenance Plan & submit it to the Organization.
- Shall approve "Repair Application" and submit them to the Organization.
- Shall approve the Monthly Maintenance Report and submit it to the Organization.

#### Superintendent {#np-05-1-3-superintendent}
Superintendent shall conduct the following for ORU charged with their control:
- Establish the long-term maintenance plan.
- Prepare & distribute the Yearly maintenance plan.
- Establish the budget for next year.
- Make plans for running repair and Major repair.
- Visit ORU periodically to check present condition.
- Preserve performance record related to equipment maintenance.
- Keep & management all record regarding maintenance for the Site ORU.

#### Sr. Operation Team Leader {#np-05-1-3-sr-operation-team-leader}
- Has the responsibility and authority to supervise and direct the maintenance team members to ensure that all maintenance activities are properly and effectively carried out.
- Shall maintain and manage the PMS program about the hardware on the Site.
- Shall prepare the data of the Yearly maintenance plan for next year and submit to the SM.
- Shall prepare “Monthly Maintenance plan” and implement it after getting SM’s approval.
- Shall prepare “Repair application” & shall make and examine the “Repair application”.
- Shall approve the Monthly Maintenance Report and submit it to the Site manager.

#### Maintenance Team Leader {#np-05-1-3-maintenance-team-leader}
- Is the person in charge of maintenance and repair for the LNG Regasification Unit, including ISO Tanks, and holds the responsibility and authority to supervise the maintenance team members to ensure that all maintenance activities are properly and effectively carried out.
- Shall maintain and manage the PMS program about the hardware on the Site.
- Shall prepare the data of the Yearly maintenance plan for next year and submit to the Sr. OP Team Leader.
- Shall prepare “Monthly Maintenance plan” and implement it after getting Sr. OP Team Leader's approval.
- Shall prepare the “Repair application” & shall make and examine the “Repair application”.
- Shall approve the Monthly Maintenance Report and submit it to the Sr. OP Team Leader.

### 1.4 Summary of Maintenance Procedure {#np-05-1-4-summary-of-maintenance-procedure}
Serves as a principal member of the maintenance team responsible for executing all maintenance activities in accordance with the Yearly and Monthly Maintenance Plans. The Gas Team at LNG shall continuously monitor the maintenance status of the ORU to ensure compliance and operational integrity.
- Continuous monitoring includes the periodical visit/checking for Superintendent.
- “Yearly Maintenance Plan” & “Monthly Maintenance Plan” should be prepared for good maintenance.
- Critical equipment and systems are to be included in “Yearly Maintenance Plan” & “Monthly Maintenance Plan”.
- Finding any defect of critical equipment and systems through periodical inspection, even though the maintenance interval is not due, proper maintenance for defect item should be carried out immediately.
- Critical equipment and systems are treated as top priority in any cases.
- Organization shall provide “Yearly Maintenance Plan” & “Monthly Maintenance Plan” to Maintenance team on the ORU site for good maintenance.
- Patch up repair or double up repair/maintenance for major parts such as Cryogenic pipe and all pipes related to the Regasification unit should not be allowed.
- All inspection and maintenance should be carried out in accordance with the instruction and information provided by maker and Organization.

### 1.5 Yearly Maintenance Plan {#np-05-1-5-yearly-maintenance-plan}
Sr. Operation Team Leader shall prepare the data of the Yearly maintenance plan for next year which includes followings and submit it to the Site manager not later than 20th, November every year:
- Periodical inspection items of ORU System
- Periodical inspection items of ISO Tank
- PMS items
- Other important repair items

Site Manager shall review the data of the Yearly maintenance plan for next year prepared by Sr. Operation Team leader and submit it to Organization until 30th November every year.

Referring to the Data of Yearly maintenance plan for next year submitted by the Site Manager, superintendent shall prepare the “Yearly maintenance plan for next year”, including:
- Maintenance plan reflected ORU operation schedule.
- Yearly Budget.
- New installation of important equipment and systems or the modification of the Site.
- Scheduled important repair.
- Competency standard for critical equipment’s.

Superintendent shall distribute the “Yearly maintenance plan” to the ORU Site until 20th, December after getting approval of LNG Manager.
The Site Manager shall display the “Yearly maintenance plan” at the public place on ORU site.

### 1.6 Monthly Maintenance Plan {#np-05-1-6-monthly-maintenance-plan}
Sr. Operation Team Leader shall prepare the “Monthly maintenance plan” and submit it to the Site manager. The plan applies the term from 21st present month to 20th next month and includes the following:
- Overdue items on PMS.
- Repair plan for unsettled defect items.
- Items scheduled to repair by Maintenance team's member.
- Items to be repaired by subcontractors.

The Site Manager shall approve the Monthly maintenance plan and display it at public place on Site. 1 (one) copy of the plan shall be submitted to the Organization.

### 1.7 Conducting Planned Maintenance {#np-05-1-7-conducting-planned-maintenance}
- Site Manager shall make Sr. Operation Team Leader & Maintenance Team Leader conduct planned maintenance & reports the result to the Organization.
- Unsettled items among the monthly maintenance plan should be reflected on the next month’s maintenance plan.

### 1.8 Record {#np-05-1-8-record}
- Enter the item of “Yearly Maintenance Plan” & “Monthly Maintenance Plan”, and PMS.
- The item not included in the “Yearly Maintenance Plan” & “Monthly Maintenance Plan” should be recorded on Daily Maintenance Report.
- The important one of the above items should be recorded in the Log Book.

### 1.9 Document to be Reported to the Organization {#np-05-1-9-document-to-be-reported}
**“Monthly maintenance plan” including:**
- Maintenance plan for unsettled overdue items
- Maintenance plan for overdue items coming next month
- Maintenance plan for unsettled defect items

**“Monthly maintenance report” including:**
- Maintenance job history for this month printed from computerized PMS program
- Maintenance items conducted this month but not recorded on PMS program

**The following should be reported regarding PMS program every month:**
- Overdue Items on PMS
- Unsettled defect Items on PMS
- Other reports or records identified on the “Organization form list” related to maintenance should be submitted to the Organization according to defined interval.

## Chapter 2: Periodical Inspection {#np-05-ch-2-periodical-inspection}

### 2.1 Object of Periodical Inspection {#np-05-2-1-object-of-periodical-inspection}
- **Main Regasification Process Systems**: LNG ISO Tank, Regasification System (Ambient Air Vaporizer/AAV, NG Buffer Tank, NG Metering Skid, Vent Stack).
- **Critical Safety Systems (Safety Critical Elements)**: Pressure Safety Valves (PSV), Emergency Shutdown (ESD) Valves, Fire & Gas Detection System, and other safety equipment related to personnel and environmental protection.

### 2.2 Periodical Inspection and Recording on Site {#np-05-2-2-periodical-inspection-recording-on-site}

#### Generals {#np-05-2-2-generals}
- Organization shall provide PMS program identified periodical inspection items.
- Organization shall provide other formal forms for the periodical inspection items not included in PMS program.
- Referring to the following, Sr. Operating Team Leader shall decide the inspection interval and the judging standard of propriety for inspection items:
  1. Mandatory Rules and Regulations
  2. Results of Risk-Based Inspection (RBI) for Critical Site and Piping
  3. Condition Monitoring (CBM) Data from Equipment (if available)
  4. Present Condition of Machinery or Equipment
  5. Manufacturer's Instructions
- PIC of Inspection shall check inspection items in accordance with identified interval, and submit prepared inspection record to Sr. Operating Team Leader.
- Finding any defect on inspection record:
  1. Sr. Operating Team Leader shall confirm the scene by himself.
  2. Sr. Operating Team Leader shall take proper action against identified defect according to clause 2.3.

#### Recording and Reporting {#np-05-2-2-recording-and-reporting}
- Contents of checking should be recorded on the check list provided by Organization and related photos are to be attached on the record if necessary.
- The result of checking should be recorded on PMS program.
- Records identified as reporting items should be submitted to Organization in accordance with defined interval.
- For deficiency identified during checking, actions taken by personal site should be recorded on the reports.

### 2.3 Action to be Taken for Defect {#np-05-2-3-action-to-be-taken-for-defect}
- If the defect can be maintained by Site's members, Sr. Operating Team Leader shall maintain it according to Maintenance procedure (Chapter 5/Maintenance procedure by site's member).
- If the defect cannot be maintained by Site's members, Sr. Operating Team Leader shall maintain it according to Chapter 6/Maintenance by subcontractors.

## Chapter 3: Management of Critical Equipment and System {#np-05-ch-3-management-critical-equipment-system}

### 3.1 Identification of Critical Equipment and Systems {#np-05-3-1-identification-critical-equipment-systems}

| System Category | Component 1 | Component 2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **LNG Storage System** | LNG ISO Tanks and their cryogenic insulation systems | Pressure Relief Valve (PSV) Systems and associated Vent Piping |
| **Regasification System** | Ambient Air Vaporizers (AAV) and their associated drainage/defrosting systems | Main Piping and Valving for the LNG (Cryogenic) and Natural Gas (NG) lines |
| **Critical Control and Safety Systems (Safety Critical Elements)** | Emergency Shut Down (ESD) System (including Push Buttons and Logic Controller), Critical Isolation Valves (Critical Shutdown Valves) at main process inlet/outlet | Pressure Safety Valves (PSV) on Vaporizer and Buffer Tank, Fixed Gas Detection Systems (Gas/Fire Detectors) and Fire Fighting System |
| **Gas Handling System** | NG Buffer Tank | NG Metering Skid (custody transfer and quality control) |
| **Essential Power Systems** | Emergency Generator and its associated control panel | |

### 3.2 Implementation for Reliability {#np-05-3-2-implementation-for-reliability}
The Nias Site shall implement the following to ensure the reliability of Critical equipment and Systems:
- Identification of Critical equipment and systems
- Establishment and implementation of PMS
- Establishment and implementation of joint maintenance system between NIAS SITE
- Careful control of major spare parts
- Identification and maintenance of competence standard

### 3.3 Joint Maintenance System Between NIAS Site {#np-05-3-3-joint-maintenance-system}
The Nias Site shall establish and implement the Joint maintenance system including:
- Incident management including reporting and action to be taken
- Periodical report for condition, defect and routine maintenance
- Monitoring of PMS implementation
- Competence control
- Inventory control of major spare parts
- Record control

#### Corrective Action for Defect of Critical Equipment {#np-05-3-3-corrective-action}
- Finding any defect in critical equipment and systems, the SITE MANAGER should immediately report it to the Nias Site.
- When accident occurs in Critical equipment and systems, the SITE MANAGER should immediately submit the Accident/near-accident report to the Nias Site.
- Identified defect should be treated as soon as possible. If it cannot be repaired by Site's members, the Repair requisition sheet should be submitted to the Nias Site.
- The defect in Critical equipment includes the case not up to the competence standard identified by Nias Site.
- Receiving any report of accident or defect for critical equipment from the Site, the Nias Site should treat this matter as top priority.

#### Maintenance Permit System {#np-05-3-3-maintenance-permit-system}
- The SITE MANAGER shall get permission from Nias Site if maintenance is occurred regarding critical equipment.
- The Nias Site provides the form “Permit of Critical equipment maintenance”.
- For the case that Critical equipment needs to shut down for routine planned maintenance, the Nias Site shall provide the “Risk Assessment Sheet”.
- Before carrying out maintenance for Critical equipment, the SITE MANAGER shall review and observe safety measures for potential risk on the Risk Assessment Sheet, recorded on the sheet.
- The SITE MANAGER shall submit the “Permit of critical equipment maintenance” with “Risk Assessment Sheet” to the Nias Site and require permission.
- Superintendent shall review requisition, get approval by SM, and advise to SITE MANAGER.
- When maintenance work cannot be completed within approved term, SITE MANAGER may request extension of Shut down period in the “Permit for critical equipment maintenance”.

#### Recording and Reporting for Maintenance {#np-05-3-3-recording-and-reporting}
- SITE MANAGER shall briefly report contents and result of maintenance after completion by e-mail.
- SR. OPERATION TEAM LEADER shall record contents and result of maintenance in PMS Record, Log Book and Measuring report.
- SR. OPERATION TEAM LEADER shall record details of maintenance in “Monthly Maintenance report” and submit to Nias Site.

### 3.4 Competence Control for Critical Equipment and Systems {#np-05-3-4-competence-control-critical-equipment}
- **Annual Maintenance Plan**: The LNG shall prepare “Competence and Maintenance Plan” containing competence standards for next year and distribute to each NIAS SITE.
- **Competency Control**: Define competence standards for Critical equipment regarding Operation, Maintenance and repair, and amending parameters (e.g. changing alarm set points).
- **Definition of Competence Standard**: LNG shall define competence standards and distribute in Yearly Maintenance Plan by 20th Dec. every year.
- **Preservation of Performance**: Site personnel shall cooperate to maintain defined competence standards under Joint maintenance system. Finding insufficiency, SITE MANAGER shall report to Nias Site immediately.
- When subcontractor’s repair is necessary, SITE MANAGER shall submit Repair Requisition.
- LNG shall check and analyze Competence of Critical equipment for fleet every 3 months (Feb, May, Aug, Nov).

### 3.5 Inventory Control for Major Spare Parts {#np-05-3-5-inventory-control-major-spare-parts}
- SR. OPERATION TEAM LEADER shall identify major spare parts and proper numbers of stock for Critical equipment.
- Above shall be comprised in computerized PMS program and joint controlled by Site staff.
- SR. OPERATION TEAM LEADER shall immediately request major spare parts to Nias Site when stock is insufficient.
- Nias Site should immediately supply shortage of major spare parts.

## Chapter 4: PMS (Planned Maintenance System) {#np-05-ch-4-pms}

### 4.1 Purpose {#np-05-4-1-purpose}
PMS means Planned Maintenance System, which allows the operators of a Regasification Facility (ORU/Site) to maintain all equipment and systems according to a predetermined schedule, ensuring sustained Operational Reliability and Asset Integrity.

### 4.2 Responsibility and Authority {#np-05-4-2-responsibility-and-authority}
- **SITE MANAGER**: Monitor establishment and implementation of PMS and send data to organization every month. Manage Certificate Group on PMS.
- **Sr. Operation Team Leader**: General manager for control of PMS. Manage all records on PMS except certification group.

### 4.3 Object of PMS {#np-05-4-3-object-of-pms}
- Main Regasification Process Equipment: LNG ISO Tank, Ambient Air Vaporizer (AAV), NG Buffer Tank, NG Metering Skid.
- Critical Safety Systems (Safety Critical Elements): ESD Valves, Pressure Safety Valves (PSV), Fire & Gas Detection System, and Fire Fighting System.
- Auxiliary/Utility Equipment (e.g., Emergency Generator, Instrument Air System).
- Electrical and Instrumentation/Electronic Systems.
- Process Piping and Related Structures.

### 4.4 Maintenance Contents of PMS {#np-05-4-4-maintenance-contents-of-pms}
- **Condition-Based (CBM) and Predictive Maintenance (PdM)**: Triggered by Condition Monitoring results (vibration, thermography, differential pressure) to optimize work intervals.
- **Risk-Based Inspection (RBI)**: Scheduling inspections for pressure vessels, cryogenic tanks, and process piping based on risk assessment.
- **Preventive Maintenance (Time/Run-hour Based)**: Overhauls, cleaning, tests, and periodic adjustments based on Manufacturer recommendations and operational experience.
- **Regulatory Compliance**: Overhauls, tests, measurements, and Calibration according to schedules mandated by Regulations and Classification Bodies.
- **Parts Replacement**: Replacement of critical spare parts (life-expired parts).
- **Specialist Maintenance**: Periodic maintenance performed by specialized professionals, coordinated by Sr. Operation Team Leader.

## Chapter 6: Maintenance by Subcontractors {#np-05-ch-6-maintenance-subcontractors}

### 6.1 Object of Maintenance by Subcontractors {#np-05-6-1-object-maintenance-subcontractors}
- In case maker’s engineer, specialist or special tools are needed.
- In case site’s man-hour is not sufficient.
- Items that superintendent decides as item to be maintained by subcontractors.

### 6.2 Requisition for Sub-contractor's Maintenance {#np-05-6-2-requisition-subcontractor-maintenance}
- Apply "Repair Requisition" form provided by organization.
- Prepared by Team Leader of each department (Maintenance & Operation) and approved by Site Manager after Sr. Operation Team Leader review.
- Separate requisition sheet and No. for each repair item.
- Describe position, specification, symptom, cause of trouble, and Sr. Operation Team Leader’s opinion. Attach diagrams/photos if necessary.
- Request necessary spare parts according to procedure.

### 6.3 Approval and Ordering of Sub-contractor's Maintenance {#np-05-6-3-approval-ordering-subcontractor-maintenance}
LNG team shall assist subcontractor’s maintenance process:
1. Review possibility of site crew maintenance, details, proper time, insurance coverage.
2. Nominate subcontractor and receive quotation.
3. Obtain approval with Repair Requisition form, quotation, time/place/period, estimated expense vs budget.
4. Send repairing order application to nominated subcontractor.

### 6.4 Notification of Subcontractor’s Maintenance {#np-05-6-4-notification-subcontractor-maintenance}
LNG shall inform documented information for subcontractor’s maintenance to business team and agent if necessary.

### 6.5 Implementation of Subcontractor’s Maintenance {#np-05-6-5-implementation-subcontractor-maintenance}
- **Preparation**: Sr. Operation Team Leader prepares shutdown items, tools, special tools. Submit Site Entry/Exit Request Form for SM approval.
- **Safety Practice**: Follow Work Permit system for dangerous work. Sr. Operation Team Leader and shore worker responsible person sign “Safety practice for shore worker” before work.
- **Supervising**: Superintendent or proxy attends major work scene. Ensure work conducts per order, on schedule, safely.
- **Confirming Result**: Review “Work completion report” prepared by subcontractor (work items, labor man-hours, dates, consumed materials, defects). Conduct test/trial run under normal conditions.

### 6.6 Closing of Subcontractor’s Maintenance {#np-05-6-6-closing-subcontractor-maintenance}
- Superintendent receives “Repair result report” from Site and processes closing.
- Receive signed invoice, negotiate final amount, record and sign.
- Draw up completion draft for president approval. Request tax bill issue.

## Chapter 8: ISO Tank LNG Maintenance and Inspection Procedure {#np-05-ch-8-iso-tank-lng-maintenance}

### 8.1 Purpose {#np-05-8-1-purpose}
To establish a comprehensive, structured, and standardized maintenance guideline for CIMC T75 LNG ISO Tanks to ensure safe, effective, and traceable inspection, preventive maintenance, corrective maintenance, and component replacement. Ensures compliance with ISO 1496-3, IMDG Code, ASME VIII, and NB/T47059.

### 8.2 Scope {#np-05-8-2-scope}
Applies to all CIMC T75 40ft LNG ISO Tanks operated or stored within Nias facilities. Covers daily, weekly, monthly, annual inspections, 2-year and 5-year PSV/safety device replacements, valve/piping overhauls, instrument calibration, vacuum integrity checks, structural inspections, and painting.

### 8.3 Definitions {#np-05-8-3-definitions}

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| LNG (Liquefied Natural Gas) | Natural gas liquefied at approx –162 °C, flash point 32 °C (90 °F), for safe transportation and storage. |
| LNG ISO Tank Container | Internationally standardized container to transport LNG in liquid form under cryogenic conditions. |
| Lifting Equipment | Mechanical devices (cranes, reach stackers) to safely lift, move, and position LNG ISO tanks. |
| Lifting Gear | Supporting accessories (slings, shackles, spreader bars) used with lifting equipment. |

### 8.4 References {#np-05-8-4-references}

| Document Type | Document Number / Title |
| --- | --- |
| NFPA | CIMC ISO Tank Manual |
| ISO | ISO 1496-3: Tank Containers |
| ASME | ASME VIII Div.1 Pressure Vessel Code |
| CGA | CGA 341 and NB/T47059 (Cryogenic Vessel Standards) |
| IMDG Code | Chapter 6.7 – Design, construction, inspection, and testing of portable tanks |
| SKK Migas | Applicable local and national regulations concerning transportation, safety, and environment |

### 8.5 Role and Responsibilities {#np-05-8-5-role-and-responsibilities}

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| **Site Manager (SM)** | Oversees entire maintenance operation of LNG ISO Tanks. Instructs HSE Officer, coordinates departments, reviews and approves plans/documentation. |
| **Sr. Operation & Maintenance Leader (SOML)** | Reviews and approves maintenance plan, supervises safety compliance, verifies final results, reports to SM. |
| **Maintenance Team Leader (MTL)** | Develops structured maintenance schedule, allocates personnel/resources, leads equipment preparation, documents activities. |
| **Spare part Logistic Manager (SLM)** | Manages spare parts inventory, verifies genuine parts, expiration dates, and storage conditions. |
| **Maintenance Worker / Electrician (MO)** | Disassembles/replaces valves/piping, cleans/dries components, performs leak tests (soap/helium), calibrates instruments. |
| **HSE Officer (SO)** | Conducts safety inspections, hazard assessments, PPE checks, equipment isolation verification, gas/flame detector monitoring. |

### 8.6 General Requirements {#np-05-8-6-general-requirements}
- Tank must be fully emptied, drained, and purged with nitrogen ($N_2$) until oxygen concentration $\le 3\%$.
- Personnel must wear complete PPE: cryogenic gloves, face shield, anti-static coverall, safety helmet, safety shoes.
- Hot work strictly prohibited unless certified gas-free by HSE personnel with PTW.
- Tools must be cryogenic service rated, anti-static, non-sparking, and calibrated.
- All maintenance recorded in official logbook and verified by assigned Leader.
- Abnormal conditions (vacuum loss, leak, frost, structural damage) reported immediately; withdraw tank from service.

### 8.7 Periodic Inspection and Maintenance {#np-05-8-7-periodic-inspection-and-maintenance}

#### Daily Inspection {#np-05-8-7-daily-inspection}
- Visual inspection of tank body and frame for cracks, corrosion, dents, or deformation.
- Check nameplates, warning labels, UN T75 markings.
- Inspect liquid, vapor, and drain valves for leaks, frost build-up, abnormal vibration/noise.
- Record pressure gauge (0–1.6 MPa) and differential pressure level gauge readings.
- Ensure safety and relief valves are free of frost, dirt, or blockages.

#### Weekly Inspection {#np-05-8-7-weekly-inspection}
- Test emergency shut-off valves (A-3, A-5, A-17) by full open/close movement.
- Check vacuum gauge (VM-1): insulation vacuum must remain $\le 0.1 	ext{ Pa}$ under ambient conditions.
- Monitor LNG holding performance / natural evaporation rate (holding time $\ge 71	ext{ days}$ at 90% filling ratio).

#### Monthly Inspection {#np-05-8-7-monthly-inspection}
- Calibrate pressure gauges (0–1.6 MPa) and differential pressure level gauges.
- Functional test pipeline relief valves (S-3, S-6) and safety vent valve (A-13) using $N_2$.
- Leak test DN50 PN40 flange joints using low-pressure $N_2$ and bubble/spray method.
- Visual inspection of outer shell (Q345R) and frame for corrosion or coating damage.

#### Annual Inspection {#np-05-8-7-annual-inspection}
- Ultrasonic thickness testing (UT) on inner vessel (S30408) and outer shell (Q345R).
- Verify insulation vacuum level $\le 0.1 	ext{ Pa}$; arrange re-vacuuming and helium leak test if degraded.
- Repaint tank and frame with 3-layer coating system (primer, intermediate, finish).

### 8.9 Biennial Maintenance (Every 2 Years) {#np-05-8-9-biennial-maintenance}
- Replace all Pressure Safety Valves (PSVs: S-1, S-2, S-3, A-13) with new or recalibrated units from certified manufacturers (HEROSE, REGO).
- Operate 3-way changeover valve (A-14) to isolate PSV being replaced while keeping alternate PSV active.
- Verify changeover valves and vent lines operate correctly during replacement.
- Conduct additional functional tests on emergency shut-off valves (A-3, A-5, A-17) under pressure.
- Record replaced PSV serial numbers, calibration certs, dates, and signatures in logbook.

### 8.10 Five-Yearly Maintenance (Every 5 Years) {#np-05-8-10-five-yearly-maintenance}
- Full internal inspection of inner vessel via manhole under certified gas-free conditions.
- Replace or reactivate adsorbent material (molecular sieve / activated charcoal) inside vacuum space (CIMC / licensed provider only).
- Conduct full vacuum re-pull ($\le 0.1 	ext{ Pa}$) and helium leak test.
- Comprehensive UT thickness testing of inner vessel (S30408) and outer shell (Q345R).
- Overhaul all valves, gauges, fittings, PSVs, level gauges, and pressure indicators.
- Repaint exterior with certified multi-layer system; inspect gooseneck groove, lifting pads, corner castings.
- Arrange recertification inspection with authorized classification society (BV, LR, CCS).

### 8.11 Maintenance Methods {#np-05-8-11-maintenance-methods}

#### Valve & Piping {#np-05-8-11-valve-piping}
1. Confirm tank empty and purged with $N_2$ ($O_2 \le 3\%$).
2. Close target valve by tag (A-3: Liquid inlet, A-5: Vapor inlet, A-17: Drain). Isolate line.
3. Loosen DN50 PN40 flange bolts in crisscross sequence to release pressure.
4. Remove valve carefully. Clean flange face from frost, rust, gasket residue.
5. Install new gasket and replacement valve. Torque bolts in crisscross pattern.
6. Conduct $N_2$ leak test. Return valve to normal operating position and log details.

#### Pressure Safety Valve (PSV - Tags S-1, S-2, S-3, A-13) {#np-05-8-11-psv}
1. Confirm tank empty and purged ($O_2 \le 3\%$).
2. Operate 3-way changeover valve A-14 to isolate target PSV while keeping backup PSV active.
3. Release residual pressure slowly to safe vent. Remove PSV with proper spanner.
4. Clean seat/threads, install new gasket/sealing tape.
5. Install replacement PSV (set pressure rating **0.79–0.83 MPa**). Torque bolts/threads.
6. Nitrogen leak test at **0.2–0.3 MPa**. Apply wire seal, tag label, protective cap. Log details.

#### Pressure Gauge (Tags G-1, G-3) {#np-05-8-11-pressure-gauge}
1. Close corresponding gauge valves (A-16/A-15 or G-1/G-3) to isolate instrument.
2. Remove gauge with calibrated spanner. Clean valve seat, replace O-ring/sealing tape.
3. Install new/calibrated gauge (range 0–1.6 MPa).
4. Reopen gauge valve slowly. Monitor needle movement and reading stability. Log details.

#### Vacuum System (Tag VM-1) {#np-05-8-11-vacuum-system}
1. Confirm tank empty and purged ($O_2 \le 3\%$).
2. Check VM-1 reading. If $> 0.1 	ext{ Pa}$ under warm condition, schedule re-vacuuming.
3. Connect certified vacuum pump via VM-1 and V-1 tubing.
4. Perform helium leak test around insulation space and connections.
5. Pull pressure down to $\le 0.1 	ext{ Pa}$. Close vacuum valve, seal port. Log details (CIMC/licensed workshop only).

#### Outer Shell & Frame {#np-05-8-11-outer-shell-frame}
1. Visual inspection of Q345R outer shell and supporting frame (gooseneck groove, corner castings, lifting pads, bottom rails).
2. Clean rust/peeling paint, apply 3-layer recoating system (primer, intermediate, finish).
3. Perform UT thickness measurement on suspected thin areas.
4. Check data/certification plates and weld seam integrity. Repaint per standard color code.

### App 02.7 Maintenance & Inspection Procedures {#np-05-app-02-7-maintenance-inspection-procedures}
- **Daily Inspection**: Visual check of tubes/fins for dust, frost, icing. Monitor vibration/noise. Document in logbook.

- **Monthly PM**: Non-abrasive cleaning of tube bundles/fins (compressed air / soft brush / low-pressure water). Torque verification on mounting hardware. Frost thermal imaging. Airflow verification via handheld anemometer. Trend analysis.

### App 02.8 Corrective Maintenance {#np-05-app-02-8-corrective-maintenance}
- **Fins Replacement**: Isolate AAV, LOTO. Remove damaged fins from mounting clips. Install new fins with original pitch/orientation. Verify airflow with anemometer.
- **Tube Replacement**: Depressurize, purge with $N_2$. Certified welders with hot work permit for cutting/welding. Install new tube, $N_2$ leak test with soap solution/detector.
- **Mounting & Structural Repair**: Retighten or replace loose/corroded bolts/brackets with calibrated torque wrench. Check laser/straight-edge alignment.
- **Frost / Icing Removal**: Stop AAV. Controlled natural thawing or warm air blowers at safe distance. Mechanical scraping/hammering strictly prohibited.
- **Restoring Reduced Vaporization Efficiency**: Anemometer airflow measurement, thermal imaging, clean fins/replace tubes.
- **Documentation**: Log cause, method, parts used, test results.

- Structural integrity & laser alignment verification.
- Nondestructive Testing (NDT) on tubes (ultrasonic thickness, dye penetrant).

- Airflow & environmental obstacle assessment (vegetation trimming).

## Appendix 03: Maintenance and Inspection Gas Sales Metering Procedure {#np-05-app-03-gas-sales-metering-procedure}

### App 03.1 Purpose {#np-05-app-03-1-purpose}
To define systematic maintenance and inspection for gas sales metering system to ensure measurement accuracy, reliability, and contractual/regulatory compliance.

### App 03.2 Scope {#np-05-app-03-2-scope}
Applies to flow meters, transmitters, prover connections, valves, strainers/filters, and associated instrumentation.

### App 03.3 Definitions {#np-05-app-03-3-definitions}
- **Preventive Maintenance (PM)**: Scheduled activities.
- **Corrective Maintenance (CM)**: Repair/restore after fault.
- **Inspection**: Systematic condition/performance checking.

### App 03.4 References {#np-05-app-03-4-references}
ISO 5167, AGA standards for gas flow measurement.

### App 03.5 Role and Responsibilities {#np-05-app-03-5-role-and-responsibilities}
Site Manager, Sr. O&M Leader, Operation Leader, Field Operator, Mechanical Engineer, Mechanical Operator, E/I Technician, Spare Parts Coordinator, HSE Officer.

### App 03.6 Procedure {#np-05-app-03-6-procedure}
1. **Preparation**: PTW, JSA, LOTO approved by HSE Officer. Confirm schedule with Offtaker. Toolbox meeting.
2. **Isolation & Line Switching**: Inform Control Room. Open MV-1B-IN/OUT (Run B). Slowly close MV-1A-IN/OUT (Run A). Monitor SCADA. Depressurize Run A via DV-1A / VV-1A to flare/vent.
3. **Mechanical Maintenance**: Inspect/clean strainers/filters, check meter body/flanges for leaks/wear, lubricate turbine meter, tighten bolts.
4. **Instrument & Electrical Maintenance**: Verify PT/TT with portable calibrator, check flow computer I/O vs SCADA, inspect junction boxes/grounding, test alarms & ESD interlocks.
5. **Preventive Inspection**: Compare active vs standby run readings, monitor strainer $\Delta P$, observe vibration/noise, inspect calibration seals.
6. **Functional Test**: Pressurize isolated line gradually, leak test flanges/gaskets, verify signal stability.
7. **Line Reinstatement**: Close vent/drain valves, slowly open MV-1A-IN/OUT to return Run A to service.
8. **Documentation & Handover**: Record in log sheet, sign-off, update equipment history card.

National regulations, AGA guidelines, API standards, ISO 9001, ISO 45001, Offtaker GSA contractual obligations.

## Appendix 04: Gas Sales Meter Calibration Procedure {#np-05-app-04-gas-sales-meter-calibration-procedure}

### App 04.1 Purpose {#np-05-app-04-1-purpose}
Ensure accuracy and reliability of gas sales meter measurements used for custody transfer at Nias Regasification Terminal.

### App 04.2 Scope {#np-05-app-04-2-scope}
Applies to flow meters, pressure/temperature transmitters, flow computers, and related custody transfer devices.

### App 04.3 Definitions {#np-05-app-04-3-definitions}
- **Custody Transfer**: Transfer of natural gas ownership where meter accuracy is contractually binding.
- **Meter Factor (MF)**: Correction factor applied to meter reading.
- **Prover**: Calibration reference device.
- **Flow Computer**: Calculates standardized gas volume and energy.

### App 04.4 References {#np-05-app-04-4-references}
AGA Report No. 3, 7, 9, 10; ISO 5167; OIML R140.

### App 04.5 Role and Responsibilities {#np-05-app-04-5-role-and-responsibilities}
Site Manager, Sr. O&M Leader, Operation Leader, Field Operator, Mechanical Engineer, Mechanical Operator, E/I Technician, Spare Parts, HSE Officer.

### App 04.6 Calibration Procedure {#np-05-app-04-6-calibration-procedure}
1. **Preparation**: Review P&ID, approve PTW/JSA/Risk Assessment. Inform Control Room & Offtaker. Dedicated radio channel. Gas test ($<10\%	ext{ LEL}$), barricades.
2. **Line Switching**: Confirm Run A duty, Run B standby. Open MV-1B-IN/OUT. Throttle MV-1A-IN, shift flow to Run B. Close MV-1A-OUT when flow in Run A is zero.
3. **Meter Isolation & Depressurization**: Verify MV-1A-IN/OUT closed. Open BV-1A-DRN and BV-1A-VENT ($0	ext{ barg}$). Apply LOTO, verify zero energy.
4. **Meter Calibration**: Connect Master Meter/Prover via MV-PROVER. Set flow computer to calibration mode. Perform calibration runs at low, medium, high flow rates. Record $\Delta P$, $T$, $P$. Verify within contractual tolerance.
5. **Line Reinstatement**: Disconnect prover, close vent/drain. Slowly open MV-1A-IN to pressurize Run A. Open MV-1A-OUT. Leak test all flanges. Shift flow back if returning Run A to duty.
6. **Documentation**: Complete calibration report (date, meter ID, before/after MF, test results, signatures). Archive in logbook.

### App 04.7 Compliance {#np-05-app-04-7-compliance}
AGA Report 3, 7, 8, 9; ISO 5167; ISO 17025; OIML R117; SNI; SKK Migas; MIGAS regulations; GSA accuracy requirements; PTW/JSA/LOTO policies.
