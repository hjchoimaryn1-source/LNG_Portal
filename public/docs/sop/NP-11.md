## 1. Emergency Response LNG Regas Facility {#np-11-1-emergency-response-lng-regas-facility}

### 1.1 Responsibility and Authority {#np-11-1-1-responsibility-and-authority}

**Site Manager (SM)**
* As incident commander that ultimate authority & responsibility for the emergency response.
* Establishes objectives, strategy, and liaises with external agencies.
* Shall establish Emergency Response System and test periodically in order to secure the ability of effective Accident Response.
* Shall secure always 'Company's Emergency Response Table' and keep a 24 hours communication system.
* Held SSHQE Committee if necessary.
* Shall organize 'Emergency Response Team' and assign the duty.
* Shall make minimize the damage of accident and diffusion of accident by proper Emergency Response.

**Operational & Maintenance Team Leader (OTL)**
* In case of accidents, directs all tactical field operations to carry out the Site Manager objectives and command.
* Manages the On-site Response Team.
* If there are no specific instructions, the OTL should perform the duties of the pollution prevention responsible officer.
* Shall assist Site Manager's duty relating to Emergency Response.

**Emergency Team**
* Emergency Response Team consist supervising team and supporting team.
* Emergency Response Team shall assist Site Manager who grasp the accident detail and prompt.
* Performing fire-fighting, isolation, and rescue tasks under the Operations Leader's direction.

**SSHQE Officer**
* Monitors safety conditions, advises the SM on all HSE matters, and has the authority to stop unsafe acts.
* Keep communicating with the accident field team.

**Control Room Operator**
* The nerve center for information, monitoring, and initiating key process actions (ESD, isolation).
* Other Operator, Mechanic and Electrician perform as Emergency Team.

**Logistic, Admin, HR-GA**
* Provide logistical support (resources, transport), accounting for personnel, and managing communications with families and the public.

### 1.2 On-site Emergency Response Procedure {#np-11-1-2-on-site-emergency-response-procedure}

#### 1.2.1 Recognition of accidents and Initial action {#np-11-1-2-1-recognition-of-accidents-and-initial-action}
The person who finds an accident for the first time should notice this to other worker near him by shouting loudly and take initial actions for the accident. The worker who heard the accident notice should report this to the supervisor and Site Manager immediately.
First of all, the Site Manager should check the status of the accident promptly and enforce proper initial actions considering the "App 02 Emergency Muster List" to prevent damage to lives, the on-site equipment machinery or the environment.

#### 1.2.2 Prompt Accident Report {#np-11-1-2-2-prompt-accident-report}
The Site Manager shall report accident contents directly to Head Office Technical and SysMan emergency telephone number of emergency contacting table using communication method as rapid as possible after completion of initial action.
Where it is impossible to contact with Technical and SysMan using emergency telephone, shall try to contact him using his home telephone number or mobile phone number.
Where it is impossible to contact with Technical and SysMan, shall report it to 1st Substitute.
Where it is impossible to contact with 1st Substitute, shall report it to 2nd Substitute.

#### 1.2.3 Grasp of situation and Initial Report {#np-11-1-2-3-grasp-of-situation-and-initial-report}
After reporting emergency accident, Site Manager shall grasp the situation considering "App 02 Emergency Muster List" and send written initial accident report.
Shall report using form "Incident Accident investigation report".
Shall describe the accident detail on XX/column of additional information of initial report considering "App 02 Emergency Muster List".
Where the initial report was made by oral, shall make written report using mail, etc as prompt as possible.

#### 1.2.4 Emergency Response and Accident Handling {#np-11-1-2-4-emergency-response-and-accident-handling}
The Site Manager shall execute emergency responding and accident handling considering the "App 02 Emergency Muster List".
Shall follow the Company instruction keeping the communication with the Head Office during the accident handling.
The Site Manager shall report the situation continuously following initial report.
Shall scribe on-site name/accident name/serial number on the report.

#### 1.2.5 Closing and Final Accident Report {#np-11-1-2-5-closing-and-final-accident-report}
The emergency response situation can be closed by SM's closing message.
After finishing the accident handling, the Site Manager shall submit the "Form Incident Accident investigation report (NP11-01)". Including followings. This report shall be described in detail for using of submission to parties concerned:
1. Incident classification
2. Description of accident
3. Immediate action
4. Cause of accident
5. Corrective/preventive action for the accident cause
6. Incident investigation team
7. Site Manager opinion for the Recurrence prevention measure
8. Statement by personnel concerned
9. Drawings and photos concerned

## 2. Incident Reporting and Investigation {#np-11-2-incident-reporting-and-investigation}

### 2.1 Purpose {#np-11-2-1-purpose}
It is a requirement of PT. LNG's SSHQE Management System Element 8 that incidents will be reported, investigated, analysed and communicated to prevent recurrence and improve our SSHQE performance. Incident Investigation will focus on identifying root causes and/or system failures while corrective actions will be utilized to reduce future incident and losses. Lessons learned from investigations will be shared and personnel will take action to prevent recurrence of similar events.
The purpose of this procedure is to assure that incidents are reported such that response actions are effectively provided, that steps are taken to prevent reoccurrence of similar incidents, and that legal requirements are met. This purpose is achieved by establishing the following framework for all incidents:
* Standardize the classification and reporting of incidents.
* Assure that incident statistics are consistent with world-wide standards of reporting.
* Identify reporting requirements.
* Provide a standard approach for incident reporting.
* Provide guidelines in conducting incident investigation using **Root Cause Analysis (RCA)** method and in the preparation of associated reports.
* Identify methods of sharing lessons learned.

### 2.2 Scope {#np-11-2-2-scope}
**In Scope:**
This procedure applies to all incidents occurring on PT. LNG premises controlled by PT. LNG and/or its contractors ("The Work Environment"). This includes all PT. LNG activities whether at the plant site in Sulawesi, in the Jakarta headquarters office, or employees on business anywhere in the world.
The Work Environment includes not only the physical location, but also the equipment or materials used by the employee or contractor during the course of his or her assigned work. Specifically included are:
* Contractor motor vehicles working exclusively for PT. LNG at the time of the exposure;
* PT. LNG workforce members whose normal place of work is at a customer site.

An injury or illness must be considered 'work related' if an event or exposure in the work environment caused or contributed to the resulting condition or significantly aggravated a pre-existing injury or illness. Work relatedness is presumed for injuries and illnesses resulting from events or exposures occurring in the work environment unless one of the following exceptions applies in its entirety:
1. Occurs when the employee was present in the work environment as a member of the general public.
2. Results solely from voluntary participation in a wellness program or in a medical, fitness, or recreational activity.
3. Involves signs or symptoms that surface at work but result solely from a non-work related event or exposure.
4. Is solely the result of an employee doing personal tasks at the establishment outside of the employee's assigned working hours.
5. Is solely the result of personal grooming, self-medication for a non-work related condition or is intentionally self-inflicted.
6. Is caused by a vehicle accident and it occurs on a company owned parking lot or road while the employee is commuting.
7. Is the common cold or flu, and/or
8. Is a mental illness.

**Out of Scope:**
This procedure does not apply to any incident involving:
* Contractor personnel being mobilized prior to starting work or being demobilized after finishing work.
* Contractors working at a location controlled by their employer, employing contractors or themselves.
* Workforce of a public utility company coming within the PT. LNG Work Environment to maintain, install or dismantle equipment owned by the utility.

### 2.5 Incident Reporting {#np-11-2-5-incident-reporting}

#### 2.5.1 Major Incident Notification (Level 5-4) {#np-11-2-5-1-major-incident-notification-level-5-4}
* Any individual involved or witnessing a Major Incident shall immediately report to the emergency line and the Team Leader.
* Team Leader notifies Responsible Manager. Emergency line notifies Incident Commander (Site Manager) and HSE Officer.
* If confirmed Major Incident (Level 5 or 4), Site Manager advises Operation Director and President Director.
* PT. LNG Responsible Manager completes PT. LNG Major Incident Announcement Form to Board of Directors within 8 hours.

#### 2.5.2 Days Away From Work Case (DAFWC) / Level 3 Notification {#np-11-2-5-2-dafwc-level-3-notification}
* Report immediately to Team Leader and emergency number.
* Responsible Manager notifies Site Manager and HSE Officer.
* Initial Incident Announcement Form completed and emailed to Operation Director, Site Manager, and Senior Managers within 24 hours.

#### 2.5.3 Restricted Work / Medical Treatment / First Aid / Near Miss (Level 2-1) Reporting {#np-11-2-5-3-level-2-1-reporting}
* Report immediately to Team Leader.
* Responsible Manager completes Initial Incident Announcement Form within 24 hours.

#### 2.5.4 Government Reporting (MIGAS) {#np-11-2-5-4-government-reporting-migas}
* **Injuries**: Work-related fatalities and DAFWC > 21 days reported using Form IIIi/IIIp signed by Kepala Teknik Pemurnian & Pengolahan within 24 hours.
* **Environmental**: Hydrocarbon/chemical spills > 15 bbls reported to MIGAS using Form LPL-1 within 48 hours (2 x 24 hours). Spills < 15 bbls recorded in CPL Book and reported 6-monthly.

### 2.6 Incident Investigation Process {#np-11-2-6-incident-investigation-process}
* Root Cause Analysis (RCA) methodology shall be used for all investigations.
* **Level 5-4**: Investigation report finalized and submitted per Term of Reference (ToR) approved by Operation Director.
* **Level 3**: Investigation report submitted to HSE Officer within 7 days.
* **Level 2-1**: Investigation report submitted to HSE Officer within 3 days.
* All action items uploaded into PT. LNG Tracking System within 1 day after final report approval.

### 2.7 Competency and Training {#np-11-2-7-competency-and-training}
* All personnel involved in incident reporting and investigation shall be trained and competent.
* Site Managers, Team Leaders, and HSE Officers must complete Incident Reporting System and Root Cause Analysis training.

### 2.8 Lesson Learned Document {#np-11-2-8-lesson-learned-document}
* For Major Incidents, HSE Officer creates a 1-page Lesson Learned Flyer in Bahasa Indonesia and English within 3 days after investigation report completion.
* Distributed to site notice boards and reviewed in monthly/6-monthly HSE campaigns.

### 2.9 Incident Classification {#np-11-2-9-incident-classification}
* US OSHA 29 CFR 1904 classification rules apply for work-related injuries and illnesses.
* Incident Risk Assessment Matrix (IRAM) classifies severity (Level 1 to Level 5) across Health & Safety, Environmental, Asset/Business, Security, and LOPC categories.

### 2.10 Roles & Responsibilities {#np-11-2-10-roles-and-responsibilities}
* **Workforce**: Report events immediately, provide accurate info during investigations.
* **Team Leader**: Secure scene/evidence, notify Responsible Manager, assist investigation.
* **Site Manager**: Ensure HSE reporting compliance, issue announcements, develop ToR for Level 3 incidents, assign resources.
* **HSE Officer / SSHQE Team**: Classify incidents, manage notification, maintain tracking system, distribute lessons learned, report to authorities.
* **Investigation Team**: Conduct RCA investigation per ToR, develop corrective actions, maintain confidentiality.
* **President Director**: Lead major accident investigations and provide necessary resources.

## App 01. Root Cause Analysis Methodology {#np-11-app-01-root-cause-analysis-methodology}

### App 01.1 Implementation Phase {#np-11-app-01-1-implementation-phase}

#### Team Preparation {#np-11-app-01-1-1-team-preparation}
The investigation team leader shall arrange a kick off meeting prior to conduct the investigation to ensure:
* The team has a good composition to deal with the subject of investigation. Additional experts may be called to support the team if required.
* Investigation's data is properly managed. Appoint one of the team member to be data coordinator (secretary) to ensure all data are well documented.
* All investigation kit is available for use (e.g. digital camera, book notes, ruler, etc).
* Logistics is available for team deployment (e.g. transportation arrangement, on site accommodation – typically it takes 2 days for site evidence collection).

#### Preserve Evidence at Scene {#np-11-app-01-1-2-preserve-evidence-at-scene}
At the earliest opportunity, it is important for the team member to contact site Supervisor to ensure:
* Actions have been taken to prevent secondary incident from happening.
* Site evidences are well secured – install scotch tape to prevent people entering the incident scene.
* Those who are involved in the incident are identified for interview purpose.

#### Collecting Evidence (4 Ps) {#np-11-app-01-1-3-collecting-evidence-4-ps}
Information on the incident can be obtained by collecting information from:
1. **Positions**: relative locations of people, parts, materials. Photography, video, simple sketches with dimensions/rulers.
2. **People**: witness interviews conducted individually as soon as possible after the incident. One team member interviews while a second records.
3. **Parts**: parts, tools, equipment, materials contributing to failure. Physical or expert examination.
4. **Papers**: documentation such as way bills, work permits, JSAs, maintenance records, training logs, operating logs, charts.

### App 01.2 Research Phase {#np-11-app-01-2-research-phase}
Upon completion of collecting evidence, the team needs to do the following if required:
* Ensure that all information are well documented and cover all 4 Ps.
* Conduct further calculations to check data.
* Identify and conduct technical analysis (e.g., macro/micro visual examination, NDT, chemical analysis, mechanical testing).

### App 01.3 Analysis Phase {#np-11-app-01-3-analysis-phase}
* Organize evidence and establish Sequence of Events / Chronology by date, time, and place.
* Identify Critical Factors (negative events or unsafe conditions which if eliminated would have prevented occurrence or reduced severity).
* Identify Immediate Causes (Substandard Actions & Substandard Conditions) and System Causes (Personal Factors & Job Factors) using the PT. LNG Comprehensive List of Causes (CLC) Chart in Appendix 21.

### App 01.4 Corrective Phase {#np-11-app-01-4-corrective-phase}
Develop proposed corrective actions ranked by priority:
1. Requires immediate action before activity at the site resumes.
2. Must be completed to an agreed plan.
3. Should be considered but not a priority.

### App 01.6 Appendix 21: Comprehensive List of Causes (CLC) {#np-11-app-01-6-comprehensive-list-of-causes-clc}

#### Possible Immediate Causes - Actions {#np-11-app-01-6-1-immediate-causes-actions}
* **Following Procedures**: Violation (individual/group/supervisor), Operation of equipment without authority, Improper position/posture, Overexertion, Work/motion at improper speed, Improper lifting, Improper loading, Shortcuts.
* **Use of Tools or Equipment**: Improper use of equipment/tools, Use of defective equipment/tools (aware), Improper placement, Operation at improper speed, Servicing equipment in operation.
* **Use of Protective Methods**: Lack of knowledge of hazards, PPE not used/improperly used/not available, Servicing energized equipment, Equipment/materials not secured, Disabled or removed guards/safety devices.
* **Inattention / Lack of Awareness**: Improper decision making, Distracted, Inattention to footing/surroundings, Horseplay, Acts of violence, Failure to warn, Drugs or alcohol, Routine activity without thought.

#### Possible Immediate Causes - Conditions {#np-11-app-01-6-2-immediate-causes-conditions}
* **Protective Systems**: Inadequate or defective guards/protective devices/PPE/warning systems/safety devices, Inadequate isolation.
* **Tools, Equipment & Vehicle**: Defective, inadequate, or improperly prepared equipment, tools, or vehicles.
* **Work Exposure to**: Fire and explosion, Noise, Energized electrical or mechanical systems, Radiation, Temperature extremes, Hazardous chemicals, Mechanical hazards, Clutter/debris, Storms/acts of nature, Slippery walkway.
* **Work Place Environment / Layout**: Congestion, Inadequate/excessive illumination, Inadequate ventilation, Unprotected height, Inadequate workplace layout.

#### Possible Root (System) Causes - Personal Factors {#np-11-app-01-6-3-root-causes-personal-factors}
* **Physical Capabilities**: Sensory deficiency (vision, hearing), Reduced respiratory capacity, Physical disabilities, Inability to sustain position, Restricted range of movement, Substance sensitivities, Inadequate size/strength.
* **Physical Conditions**: Previous injury/illness, Fatigue, Diminished performance, Blood sugar deficiency, Impairment due to drugs/alcohol.
* **Mental State**: Poor judgment, Memory failure, Poor coordination/reaction, Emotional disturbance, Fears/phobias, Low mechanical/learning aptitude.
* **Mental Stress**: Preoccupation with problems, Frustration, Confusing/conflicting demands, Meaningless activity, Emotional overload, Extreme concentration demands, Boredom.
* **Behavior**: Improper performance rewarded, Improper supervisory example, Inadequate reinforcement of safe behavior, Inappropriate aggression, Production incentives misaligned, Supervisor/employee implied haste.
* **Skill Level**: Inadequate skill assessment, Inadequate practice, Infrequent performance, Lack of coaching.

#### Possible Root (System) Causes - Job Factors {#np-11-app-01-6-4-root-causes-job-factors}
* **Training / Knowledge Transfer**: Inadequate knowledge transfer, Inadequate recall, Inadequate training effort, No training provided.
* **Management / Supervision / Leadership**: Conflicting roles, Inadequate leadership, Inadequate hazard correction/identification, Inadequate Management of Change (MOC), Inadequate incident reporting/investigation system, Inadequate safety meetings, Inadequate performance measurement.
* **Contractor Selection & Oversight**: Lack of/inadequate contractor pre-qualification, Inadequate selection, Use of non-approved contractor, Inadequate oversight.
* **Engineering / Design**: Inadequate technical design, Inadequate standards/specifications, Inadequate failure assessment, Inadequate ergonomic design, Inadequate monitoring of construction/initial operation, Inadequate MOC evaluation.
* **Work Planning**: Inadequate work planning, Inadequate preventive/reparative maintenance, Excessive wear and tear, Inadequate reference materials/publications, Inadequate audit/inspection, Inadequate job placement.
* **Purchasing & Material Control**: Incorrect item received, Inadequate research on materials, Inadequate mode of shipping, Improper handling/storage, Expired shelf life, Improper identification of hazardous materials, Improper disposal.
* **Tools & Equipment**: Inadequate assessment of needs/risks, Inadequate ergonomics, Inadequate availability/maintenance/records.
* **Work Rules / Policies / Standards / Procedures (PSP)**: Lack of PSP, Inadequate development/implementation/enforcement/communication of PSP.

* **Internal**: Alarm systems (sirens, PA), emergency contact lists, mobile alerts, two-way radios (intrinsically safe).
* **External**: Emergency service liaisons (fire, police, ambulance), stakeholder notification, community and media relations.
* **Public Awareness**: API RP 1162 alignment, BMKG early warning integration.

#### Hazard-Specific Emergency Procedures {#np-11-app-03-1-4-hazard-specific-procedures}
* **Tsunami**:
  * Prevention/Mitigation: Site selection, structural resilience, elevated structures/piles for electrical/system equipment, tsunami walls/flood gates, land use planning.
  * Detection/Warning: InaTEWS (BMKG) integration (<5 min warnings), local sirens/messaging.
  * Response: Immediate controlled ESD, vertical evacuation to designated reinforced concrete/steel multi-story structures, headcount, post-tsunami recovery/assessment.
* **Earthquake**:
  * Prevention/Mitigation: Seismic design standards (API 620 Annex L, Operating Base Earthquake - OBE, Safe Shutdown Earthquake - SSE), seismic isolation pendulum devices, equipment anchoring, pipeline flexibility.
  * Detection/Warning: BMKG EEWS integration, on-site accelerographs, automated ESD triggered by seismic sensors.
  * Response: "Drop, Cover, Hold On", ESD activation (in-tank shut-off valves close), evacuation to outdoor assembly points, initial damage assessment for LNG leaks/secondary fires.
* **Fire**:
  * Prevention/Mitigation: Ignition source control, flammable gas detection (infrared/ultrasonic at <25% LFL), DFOS optical sensing, spill impounding, passive fire protection (FOAMGLAS PFS modules).
  * Response Scenarios:
    * **LNG Pool Fire**: Do NOT use water directly on pool. Apply high-expansion foam blanket or passive foam modules. Cool adjacent equipment with water deluge/spray monitors.
    * **LNG Jet Fire**: Isolate fuel source via Emergency Isolation Valves (EIVs), cool surrounding surfaces, apply dry chemical powder (potassium sulphate/bicarbonate) to collapse flame.
    * **Electrical Fire**: De-energize circuit, use CO2, clean agent, or dry chemical powder. Avoid water on live equipment.
    * **Structural/Building Fire**: Evacuate building, operate automatic/fixed sprinklers or dry chemical/foam.
* **LNG Leakage**:
  * Prevention/Mitigation: Cryogenic materials selection, methane sensors, DTS fiber optic temperature sensing, impounding basins, EIVs, API RP 750/754 process safety management.
  * Response: Continuous monitoring, automated alarm at >25% LFL, initiate ESD and EIV closure, forced ventilation in enclosed spaces, water spray curtains for vapor cloud dispersion, cryogenic PPE (full face mask, cold-resistant gloves, protective clothing), first aid for cold burns/asphyxiation.
* **Dangerous Reptile Encounters (Snakes, Crocodiles)**:
  * Prevention/Mitigation: Vegetation control, rodent pest control, waste/drainage control, snake-proof fencing, lighting, awareness training.
  * Response: Maintain distance, secure area, call internal response team or BKSDA/JAAN/BARC.
  * First Aid: Snakebite — immobilize limb with splint/bandage, keep victim calm, transport immediately to hospital. Do NOT apply tourniquets, cuts, or suction. Antivenom administered only at hospital. Crocodile attack — immediate trauma care, control bleeding.

### App 04.4 Emergency Shutdown (ESD) Procedures {#np-11-app-04-4-emergency-shutdown-procedures}
* **ESD Activation Levels**:
  * **Level 1**: Unit ESD (specific equipment).
  * **Level 2**: Process ESD (unit isolation).
  * **Level 3**: Full Facility ESD.
* **ESD Sequence**: Stop LNG Cryogenic Pumps immediately -> Close all LNG/NG Control Valves -> Isolate ISO Tank connections -> Secure BOG Compressor -> Initiate process depressurization -> Maintain essential Fire & Gas System.
* **ESD Action Steps**:
  1. Emergency Condition Identification (leak, fire, abnormal pressure, critical equipment failure).
  2. ESD Activation (Field Operator / nearest personnel presses local or remote ESD button).
  3. Automatic Actions (ESDV closes, flow isolated, audio-visual alarms activate, non-essential power cut).
  4. Evacuation and Area Isolation (evacuate to assembly point, HSE Officer secures perimeter, ERT mobilizes).
  5. Situation Control (minor leak: portable gas detector, N2 purging; fire: DCP or water spray cooling, no direct water on LNG spill).
  6. Coordination & Communication (Operation Leader reports to SM, uses emergency radio channel).
  7. Recovery & Normalization (venting until gas < LEL, mechanical/electrical inspection, spare part replacement, SM official authorization).

## App 10. Emergency Preparedness For ISO Tank Handling Procedure {#np-11-app-10-emergency-preparedness-iso-tank-handling}

### App 10.1 Purpose and Scope {#np-11-app-10-1-purpose-and-scope}
* Establish comprehensive framework for preparing, responding, and recovering from emergencies in ISO Tank Yard, Unloading Skid, Regasification Unit, or supporting facilities in Nias.
* Protect human life, minimize environmental impact, safeguard company assets, ensure compliance with ESDM/Permenhub/NFPA 59A/ISO 45001 regulations, establish clear roles and communication, and provide systematic post-incident review.

### App 10.2 Definitions and References {#np-11-app-10-2-definitions-and-references}
* **LNG**: Liquefied Natural Gas (-162°C, cryogenic).
* **LNG ISO Tank Container**: Standardized cryogenic container for LNG transport.
* **ESD**: Emergency Shutdown safety system.
* **BLEVE**: Boiling Liquid Expanding Vapor Explosion.
* **Popping**: Sudden pressure release through safety valve.
* **Danger Zone**: Minimum 50m radius from leak per NFPA 59A.
* **References**: NFPA 59A, ISO 45001, ISO 16924, ISO 20421-1, API RP 752/753, IMDG Code, Permenhub PM 16/2021 & PM 60/2019.

### App 10.3 Role and Responsibilities {#np-11-app-10-3-role-and-responsibilities}
* **Site Manager (SM)**: Ensure SOP/HSE compliance, approve Journey Management Plan, coordinate with external parties.
* **HSE Officer (SO)**: Hazard identification, risk assessment, lead toolbox talks, monitor cryogenic PPE, gas testing.
* **Logistics Officer (LO)**: Permits, ISO Tank certificates, shipping requirements, transporter/port coordination.
* **Cargo Logistic (CO)**: Cargo planning, yard-to-port transfer scheduling, stevedoring coordination.
* **Stevedoring Team (ST)**: Execute port loading/unloading, operate port handling equipment safely.
* **Lifting Equipment Operator (LEO)**: Operate reach stacker/crane, daily pre-use inspection, safe ISO Tank positioning.
* **Transporter / Driver**: Vehicle roadworthiness pre-trip inspection, drive per JMP route/speed limits.
* **Emergency Response Team (ERT)**: Standby during mobilization, execute emergency actions per ERP, coordinate with authorities.

### App 10.4 Hazard Identification {#np-11-app-10-4-hazard-identification}
* **Operational**: LNG leakage (valves, flanges, hoses), High Pressure / Overpressure (PSV activation, popping), Fire/Ignition, ESD Failure, Structural Damage / Rollover, Lifting Failure (sling break, spreader bar fault, crane instability), BLEVE risk.
* **Environmental**: LNG spill with soil/water contamination, abnormal vapor release, odorant (ethyl mercaptan) spill.
* **Natural Disasters**: Earthquake, flooding/heavy rain, lightning strike, strong wind/storm.
* **System Failures**: Power outage, instrumentation failure, SCADA/control system failure.
* **Security Threats**: Sabotage/terrorism, civil disturbance, unauthorized access.
* **Medical Emergencies**: Cryogenic frostbite/cold burns, vapor inhalation/asphyxiation, non-LNG workplace accidents.

### App 10.5 Incident Command System (Three-Tier) {#np-11-app-10-5-incident-command-system-three-tier}
* **Level 1 – Local Response**: First Intervention Team (FIT) handles minor leaks, alarms, equipment faults.
* **Level 2 – Facility Response**: ERT & Regasification Team mobilized for technical shutdown, isolation, larger evacuation.
* **Level 3 – External & Corporate Escalation**: Fire Dept Nias, BPBD, PLN, BSGL Corporate HSSE Team.

### App 10.6 Preparedness {#np-11-app-10-6-preparedness}
* **Pre-Operation Inspections**: MSDS/manifest check, ISO Tank walk-around (dents, corrosion, frosting, PRV seal, pressure 3-7 bar), manifold/hose check (torque, gasket alignment), grounding clamp (resistance ≤ 5 Ω), anti-spark tools only, reach stacker certification & signed lifting plan, radio check.
* **Equipment Readiness**: Daily visual (extinguishers in green, gas detectors charged/bump tested, hydrants closed, cryogenic PPE at skid), weekly functional tests (sirens/PA, gas detector calibration, fire pump run 5-10 min), monthly/quarterly maintenance.
* **Lifting Preparedness**: Pre-lift meeting, pre-lift inspection (exclusion zone ≥ 10m), test lift (raise 0.5-1m for stability check), slow execution with spotter, emergency stop procedure.
* **Natural Disaster Preparedness**: Wheel chocks for trailers/stackers in earthquake; raise equipment in flood; suspend lifting/unloading in lightning/storm.
* **Training & Drills**: Annual drill schedule (leak, popping, lifting accident, fire, disaster), coordinate with external agencies at least once per year, debrief and drill reports.

### App 10.7 Emergency Plan {#np-11-app-10-7-emergency-plan}
* **Objectives**: Protect human life (top priority), prevent escalation, safeguard critical assets, minimize environmental impact, maintain business continuity, regulatory compliance, community trust.
* **Activation**: Level 1 (FIT), Level 2 (ERT / Incident Commander), Level 3 (Site Manager / full external mobilization). Logged in Emergency Log Book.
* **Alarm Tones**: Continuous 10s tone = vapor/leak; Intermittent bursts = fire; Prolonged 30s tone = full evacuation.
* **Evacuation**: Stop work immediately, deactivate active machinery if safe within seconds, move calmly via illuminated escape routes to primary/secondary muster points, headcount roll call, injured to triage area. Re-entry prohibited until official All-Clear.
* **Shelter-in-Place**: Ordered when outdoor evacuation unsafe (vapor cloud drift). Close doors/windows, disable HVAC, gather in interior rooms away from outer walls, turn off electrical appliances, headcount, await All-Clear.
* **Communication**: CCR hub, standard reporting template (`[Type] - [Location] - [Impact] - [Action Taken] - [Assistance Needed]`), UHF/VHF emergency radio channels, read-back protocol, backup runners/megaphones if comms fail.
* **Resources**: Extinguishers, hydrants, water mist, high-expansion foam, dry powder, gas detectors, EIVs, first aid/AEDs/oxygen, cryogenic PPE, generators, monthly inventory logs.
* **External Interface**: Fire Dept (fire/rescue), KSOP (port/maritime), PLN (power isolation), BPBD (disaster relief), Hospitals (triage/ambulance), Police (security/perimeter). Liaison Officer greets external teams at staging area.
* **Community & Stakeholders**: Verified statements by official spokesperson only. No comments by unassigned staff.
* **Documentation**: Record book, RCA report, drill debriefs, annual plan review, digital/physical archive for 5 years.

### App 10.8 Emergency Response Actions (Scenario-Specific) {#np-11-app-10-8-emergency-response-actions}
* **LNG Leak**: Raise alarm, move upwind away from low areas, IC assesses scale, attempt controlled EIV closure, water mist/DCP cooling (no direct water on liquid LNG), partial/full evacuation, medical standby, HSE atmospheric test before re-entry.
* **ISO Tank Popping**: Evacuate 50m radius, IC evaluates active water spray cooling from distance, no approach during active venting, water curtains on adjacent tanks, re-entry when pressure stabilized <0.70 MPa and gas clear.
* **Lifting Accident**: Stop equipment immediately, clear lifting zone, lifting supervisor engages safety locks, IC executes leak response if tank damaged, medical treatment/ambulance.
* **Fire and Explosion**: Raise alarm, DCP or foam for LNG fire, continuous water mist cooling, evacuate non-essential staff, external fire dept reinforcement for Level 3, monitor re-ignition risks before All-Clear.
* **Static Electricity / Spark**: Stop operations, remove non-spark tools, connect grounding/bonding clamps on tanks/hoses/manifolds, test atmosphere before resuming.
* **Vehicle / Reach Stacker Collision**: Shut engine, report to IC, isolate area, assess structural damage/leaks, execute leak procedure if needed, first aid/hospital transfer.
* **Manifold Impact**: Stop unloading, close EIVs, inspect for cracks/leaks/misalignment, keep manifold out of service until repaired/certified by engineers.
* **Electrical Failure / Arc Flash**: Isolate circuit using emergency breakers, avoid open panels, use CO2/DCP for electrical fire (no water).
* **Medical Emergency**: Raise alarm, basic first aid, medical triage, hospital transport via ambulance.
* **All-Clear & Recovery**: Issued by IC after HSE verification, final headcount, stepwise restoration, debrief session.

## App 11. Emergency Shutdown During LNG Unloading Procedure {#np-11-app-11-emergency-shutdown-during-lng-unloading}

### App 11.1 Purpose and Scope {#np-11-app-11-1-purpose-and-scope}
* Systematic guideline for executing Emergency Shutdown (ESD) during LNG unloading from ISO Tank to regasification unit via unloading skid.
* Applies to manual/remote/automatic ESD activation, operator/supervisor/HSE duties, ERT coordination, safe shutdown, post-ESD inspection, and reporting.

### App 11.2 Responsibilities {#np-11-app-11-2-responsibilities}
* **Site Manager**: Overall compliance, final decision on stopping NG supply operations.
* **Sr. Operation & Maintenance Leader**: Supervises unloading operations and gives direct instructions to ops/maint teams during ESD.
* **Operation Leader**: Field coordination, ensures proper execution of shutdown steps, reports to management.
* **Field Operator**: Operates equipment, presses local/remote ESD button, secures operational area.
* **Mechanic Engineer / Operator**: Technical readiness, mechanical equipment isolation and maintenance during shutdown.
* **E/I Technician**: Ensures electrical, instrumentation, ESD system, gas detectors, and alarms remain functional.
* **Emergency Response Team**: Evacuation, fire suppression, LNG leak handling, medical assistance.
* **HSE Officer**: Monitors HSE compliance, PPE, conducts gas testing, secures perimeter, prepares incident investigation report.

### App 11.3 Procedure {#np-11-app-11-3-procedure}
1. **Emergency Identification**: Triggered by LNG leak (cryogenic spill/gas detector), fire/ignition source, abnormal pressure (overpressure/vacuum), critical equipment failure (hose, quick close coupler, skid, valve, PBU pump), or control room / SM command.
2. **ESD Activation**: Field Operator or nearest staff presses local/remote ESD button. System automatically:
   - Closes ESDVs on ISO Tank and Unloading Skid.
   - Isolates LNG flow from source to buffer tank / regas unit.
   - Activates audio-visual alarms at unloading area and control room.
   - Cuts non-essential power supply in unloading area.
3. **Evacuation & Area Isolation**: Operators direct staff to assembly point. HSE Officer secures perimeter (no unauthorized entry). ERT mobilizes with DCP, water curtain, SCBA.
4. **Situation Control**:
   - Minor leak: Portable gas detector test, N2 purging.
   - Fire: ERT uses DCP or water spray cooling. **Direct water shall NOT be used on LNG spill.**
5. **Coordination & Communication**: Operation Leader reports to SM, coordinates via emergency radio channel.
6. **Recovery & Normalization**: Venting until gas concentration < LEL -> Mechanical & E/I Technicians inspect equipment -> Spare parts replaced if needed -> SM authorizes resumption/suspension.
7. **Reporting & Documentation**: Record in Emergency Log Sheet, HSE Officer prepares investigation report for management.

## App 13. Emergency Isolation ISO Tank in ISO Tank Yard Procedure {#np-11-app-13-emergency-isolation-iso-tank-yard}

### App 13.1 Purpose, Scope, Definitions, and References {#np-11-app-13-1-purpose-scope-definitions-references}
* Standardized procedure for emergency isolation of ISO Tanks in the ISO Tank Yard during leaks, overpressure, fire/ignition, valve malfunction, vapor release, or structural damage.
* Applies to all O&M and safety personnel. Aligns with NFPA 59A, ISO 11606, OEM manuals, and ERP.
* Definitions: Emergency Isolation, ESD, LOTO, Relief Valve / PRV.

### App 13.2 Roles & Responsibilities {#np-11-app-13-2-roles-and-responsibilities}
* **Site Manager**: Overall implementation, strategic decisions.
* **SOML / Operation Leader**: Direction to Field Operators, coordination with HSE/ERT/Maintenance.
* **Field Operator**: Direct field execution of isolation, reports to OL.
* **HSE Officer**: Safety monitoring, PPE, ERT coordination.
* **Logistics Officer**: Direct responsibility for ISO Tank Yard, first line to identify/report abnormalities.
* **Mechanic Engineering / Operator**: Technical support for valve/piping/isolation tools.
* **ERT**: Firefighting, spill containment, rescue.

### App 13.3 Emergency Cases and Isolation Actions {#np-11-app-13-3-emergency-cases-and-isolation-actions}
* **LNG Leakage (Valve / Flange / Hose)**:
  1. Detection: Shout "LEAK, LEAK", press manual alarm button, report location/tank ID/leak type to Control Room and OL via radio.
  2. Evacuation & Area Control: Evacuate non-essential staff upwind. Check wind flag. Muster point roll call.
  3. ESD Activation: Control Room triggers ESD. Field Operator verifies valve closure on panel and visually using binoculars from safe distance.
  4. Manual Isolation if ESD Fails: Supervisor authorizes entry. 2-person buddy team in full cryogenic PPE & FR coveralls approaches slowly upwind with radio link. Close inlet valve first, then outlet valve smoothly. Standby ERT ready with fire protection.
  5. Vent & Relief Check: Ensure relief valves/vent stacks unobstructed and directed to safe flare/vent. Report status every 10 min.
  6. Safety Perimeter: Establish 50-100m exclusion zone. Barricade tape + "Gas Hazard - Do Not Enter" signs. Security access log.
  7. LOTO: Apply LOTO locks and tags with valve ID, closure time, responsible person name.
  8. Continuous Monitoring: Monitor pressure, level, gas detectors every 5 min. Perimeter gas test every 15 min.
  9. Escalation / Post-Isolation: Controlled depressurization if uncontrolled. Contact external fire dept if cloud expands. Re-entry after HSE air quality clearance.
* **High Pressure / Overpressure**:
  1. Manageable (< PSV setpoint 0.84-0.88 MPa, e.g. 0.78-0.80 MPa):
     - Empty ISO Tank (residual vapor): Connect to skid, equalize pressure up to ~0.75 MPa, depressurize to vent stack down to ~0.40 MPa, tag "High Pressure - Under Monitoring".
     - Full ISO Tank (containing product): Connect to skid, equalize, designate as priority feed to Regas Unit (do NOT depressurize to vent).
  2. Approaching PSV (≈0.83 MPa, Risk of Popping): Connect high-pressure tank to standby tank in Yard using certified flexible hose, slowly open isolation valves (BV-039/BV-040) for gradual pressure equalization until both stabilize at ~0.75 MPa.
  3. PSV Popping at Setpoint (0.84-0.88 MPa): Allow PSV to release vapor, observe re-seating at ~0.70 MPa, move to skid, equalize (~0.75 MPa), depressurize to vent stack down to ~0.40 MPa.
  4. Abnormal PSV Popping (Faulty Valve): Close upstream isolation valve of PSV, depressurize tank to vent stack, Mechanical Engineer & FO replace faulty PSV with calibrated spare, leak test and re-commission.
* **Fire or Ignition Near ISO Tank**:
  1. Minor Fire Near Hose/Connection: Activate manual alarm, notify CCR/Supervisor/HSE. Evacuate non-essential staff upwind. ERT approaches upwind with DCP/CO2 extinguishers. Activate ESD. If ESD fails -> 2-person buddy manual valve closure in cryogenic/FR PPE. Apply DCP at flame base. Water mist cooling around area (avoid direct water on LNG). Gas test (LEL) after extinguishing. LOTO & barricade. Monitor 30-60 min for re-ignition.
  2. Major Fire at ISO Tank Body / Manifold: Activate site alarm, announce "Major Fire Emergency", call external fire dept. Evacuate Yard (100m exclusion zone). Remote ESD activation. ERT operates fixed/portable water spray to cool adjacent tanks. Apply large-capacity DCP or LNG-compatible foam. Continuous cooling for min 60 mins post-extinguishing. LOTO & structural inspection.
  3. Ignition Without Visible Fire (Flash Fireball): Activate site alarm, evacuate downwind area min 200m. Remote ESD activation. ERT deploys water spray for vapor dispersion & cooling. Continuous gas test (<10% LEL safe). LOTO & barricade.
  4. Fire Escalation Threatening Multiple Tanks: Declare Level 3 Emergency, full site alarm, call external fire dept/mutual aid. Total evacuation 300m radius. Remote ESD all lines. Water monitors/deluge cooling on unaffected tanks (prioritize highest fill level). DCP/foam attack under ERT command. Post-fire water cooling for min 2 hours.
  5. Post-Fire Recovery: Water cooling 60-120 min post-fire. Atmospheric monitoring. Verify tank pressures/PRV. LOTO. Engineering structural integrity check. Incident report within 24h.
* **ESD Failure or Valve Stuck Open**:
  - Notify CCR/Supervisor/HSE. Evacuate 100m radius. Attempt ESD re-activation from secondary panel.
  - If fails -> 2-person buddy team in full PPE performs manual closure upwind after gas test.
  - If manual closure fails -> skid-level block valves or upstream isolation. Apply LOTO once closed.
* **Abnormal Vapor Release**:
  - Notify CCR/Supervisor/HSE. Evacuate downwind/crosswind areas. Stop all yard vehicle traffic.
  - Activate ESD. If fails -> 2-person buddy manual valve closure upwind in full PPE.
  - Deploy portable gas detectors. If >10% LEL -> expand exclusion zone. Deploy water spray curtains to disperse vapor cloud (no direct water on liquid LNG). Apply LOTO once safe (<1% LEL).
* **Structural Damage or Tank Rollover**:
  - Activate alarm, stop all yard operations, notify CCR/Supervisor/HSE. Total evacuation, roll call.
  - Activate ESD. Establish min 100m exclusion zone. Atmospheric monitoring. Engineering assesses tank stability. Engage heavy-lift/recovery external units if unstable. Controlled pressure transfer or depressurization once stable. Water spray/foam if product released.
* **Power Failure During Operation**:
  - Notify CCR/Supervisor/HSE. Evacuate non-essential staff, stop vehicle movement.
  - Close manual isolation valves on hose and skid connection immediately.
  - 2-person buddy team in full PPE performs manual valve closure on inlet/outlet valves.
  - Deploy emergency lighting and portable radios. Apply LOTO ("Isolated - Power Failure"). Re-commission only after electrical check & SM authorization.
* **Multiple ISO Tank Abnormalities**:
  - Notify CCR/Supervisor/HSE for Level 3 escalation. Full yard alarm & evacuation. Activate ESD on all skids.
  - Prioritize: Leak/fire -> highest; Overpressure without release -> second; Abnormal vapor -> lower.
  - Split ERT/FO teams to handle multiple events simultaneously. Expand exclusion zone. Apply LOTO.

## App 14. Emergency Management LNG ISO Tank Mobilization Procedure {#np-11-app-14-emergency-management-iso-tank-mobilization}

### App 14.1 Purpose and Scope {#np-11-app-14-1-purpose-and-scope}
* Safe, effective, coordinated emergency response during ISO Tank LNG mobilization (delivery, port/regasification placement in Nias, land distribution to PLN PLTMG Nias 25 MW).
* Defines organizational structure, roles, detailed emergency procedures, internal/external comms, and training.

### App 14.2 Definitions and References {#np-11-app-14-2-definitions-and-references}
* **LNG**, **LNG ISO Tank Container**, **HSSE**, **CCR**, **ESD**, **ESDV**, **IC** (Incident Commander), **ICC** (Incident Control Center), **OSC** (On Scene Commander), **FIT** (First Intervention Team), **Popping**, **Venting**, **BLEVE**, **Danger Zone** (min 50m).
* References: NFPA 59A, ISO 20421-1, ISO 16924, ISO 45001, IMDG Code, Permenhub PM 16/2021 & PM 60/2019.

### App 14.3 Role and Responsibilities {#np-11-app-14-3-role-and-responsibilities}
* **Site Manager (SM)**: Final decisions, activate ICS, coordinate with PLN, KSOP, BPBD, Fire Brigade.
* **Sr. O&M Leader (SOML)**: Technical isolation and shutdown support.
* **Operation Leader (OL)**: Leads field ops, instructs evacuation, executes procedures.
* **Field Operator (FO)**: Valve isolation, controlled venting, gas monitoring.
* **HSE Officer (SO)**: HSE supervision, PPE, gas testing, hazardous area risk assessment.
* **Emergency Response Team (ERT)**: Rapid response, firefighting, victim evacuation, first aid.

### App 14.4 Procedure & Response Actions {#np-11-app-14-4-procedure-and-response-actions}
* **Three-Tier Command**: Level 1 (FIT on-site) -> Level 2 (Facility owner ERT in Nias) -> Level 3 (Joint support: KSOP, Fire Dept, BPBD, PLN Nias, Corporate BSGL).
* **Pre-Shipping Inspection**: Gas detector leak check, tank pressure & relief valve test, valve/venting test, IMDG Class 2.1 (UN1972) labels, MSDS & manifest check.
* **Emergency Equipment**: CO2/DCP extinguishers, CH4 gas detector, thermal camera, water mist, cryogenic PPE, remote shut-off valves, radios, evacuation maps.
* **Popping / Venting Event**: Report to IC -> Instruct OL for first aid -> Activate alarm/siren -> Evacuate personnel min 50m upwind (gas detector verified) -> Isolate hazard/ignition sources -> Close main isolation valve -> Communicate with Fire Dept/KSOP.
* **Spill / Leak Event**: Uncontrolled release marked by frost on ISO Tank panel box -> Report to IC -> Activate evacuation siren -> Assembly point headcount -> Evacuate min 50m upwind -> Close main isolation valve -> **Do NOT touch -162°C liquid** -> Full cryogenic PPE -> Water mist to dilute methane gas (do NOT extinguish LNG fires directly with water).
* **Fire Handling**: Report to IC -> Activate evacuation siren -> Evacuate staff -> Close main isolation valve -> Use DCP for small fires -> **Do NOT use direct water on LNG layer** (use water mist for cooling surrounding structures to prevent BLEVE).
* **Personnel Injury & Medical Evacuation**:
  - Evaluate victim (respiratory, cryogenic frostbite, burns).
  - Evacuate to safe assembly point (radius ≥50m).
  - First Aid: Frostbite -> gentle rewarming with warm (not hot) water; Vapor inhalation -> medical oxygen, remove PPE; Burns -> sterile compresses.
  - Transport via stretcher to referral hospital.
  - Required medical equipment at facility: Cryogenic first aid kit (salep, eye wash), portable oxygen, folding stretcher, emergency blanket, hospital contact list.
  - Required in transport vehicle: Hazmat first aid kit, hotline numbers, evacuation map.
* **Emergency Contact Numbers**: District Fire Dept, BPBD Nias, KSOP, PLN Nias Unit, BSGL Emergency Line.
* **Post-Incident & Drills**: Re-inspect tank pressure/structure, safe evacuation of remaining LNG, logbook records, SOP updates within 7 days, drills twice per year.

### NP11-19 Medical Emergency Response - Hospital Transport Checklist {#np-11-form-np11-19-medical-emergency-response-hospital-transport-checklist}

**Facility**: NIAS LNG Regasification Terminal  
**Date & Time**: [ DD / MM / YYYY HH:MM ]

**Phase 1: Immediate Response & Assessment**
1. Scene Safety Assessment: Assess for ongoing hazards (LNG leak, electrical arc, fire, pressurized gas).
2. Activate Site Emergency: Shout "Code Red Medical Emergency at [Location]", alert Security at gate.
3. Initial First Aid (DRABC Survey): Danger, Response, Airway, Breathing, Circulation.
4. Control Major Bleeding: Direct pressure, sterile dressing, tourniquet if trained/necessary.

4. Prepare Patient for Movement: Immobilize neck/spine if spinal injury suspected, log-roll onto stretcher, cover with blanket.
5. Patient Folder: Patient name, age, blood type, emergency contact, incident description, first aid given.

**Phase 3: Execution & Handover**
1. Safe Transfer to Ambulance / Vehicle: Carry stretcher carefully.
2. Accompanying Personnel: HSE Officer or Site Manager accompanies patient to hospital as company liaison.
3. Management Communication: Site Manager notifies senior management and family.
4. Hospital Handover: Hand over information package to triage nurse/doctor.

**Phase 4: Post-Incident Actions**
1. Secure Incident Area: Cordon off area with barrier tape for investigation.
2. Debrief On-Site Team: Hot-wash meeting with involved personnel.
3. Formal Incident Reporting: Complete formal report within statutory timeframes.
4. Critical Stress Debriefing: Professional counseling for traumatized staff.
