#### 1.1.1 Purpose of Legal Training {#np-02-1-1-1-purpose-legal-training}

The purpose of legal training is to promote safety and efficient service of Site by improving quality of team and satisfying requisite through completing all educations prescribed by enforced laws and regulations.

#### 1.1.2 Identification of Necessity of Legal Education {#np-02-1-1-2-identification-necessity-legal-education}

- In case that any of new education is needed to get on new kinds of Site.
- In case that any of new education is needed to join a Site occupied with a new rank or responsibility.
- In case that validity of certificate or license expired.
- In case that the validity of certificate or license is expected to be expired during joining a Site.

#### 1.1.3 Planning and Implementation of Education {#np-02-1-1-3-planning-implementation-education}

- Chief of HRGA shall prepare a draft letter of performing education to get approval of administration representative/SM when the necessity of education to be acknowledged.
- Visit designated institution to complete needed education.

#### 1.1.4 Managing Certificate and Records {#np-02-1-1-4-managing-certificate-records}

- Site staff that has completed any education shall get cert for it and submit to company (Nias Site).
- SM shall manage all certificates issued by educational institutions.
- HSE Officer shall record the training contents on the ‘(Form NP02-01 Employee Training & Competency)’.

### 1.2 In-house Training {#np-02-1-2-in-house-training}

#### 1.2.1 Recognizing Necessity of Education {#np-02-1-2-1-recognizing-necessity-education}

- Education for new employee
- Education for existing team who will join another Site
- In case that any education to promote the quality of team to be needed as a result of appraisal or evaluation.
- Drastic revision of management system
- In case that the necessity of education was acknowledged as a result of management review and the internal/external audit
- Chief of each team shall consult chief of HRGA to establish Detailed Training & Drill program when any of above reason happens.
- NP-SSHQE Familiarization training for Site Manager

#### 1.2.2 Establishing Educational Plan {#np-02-1-2-2-establishing-educational-plan}

- Site Manager should prepare “Yearly Site Training & Drill Plan (Form NP02-02)” for next year until 15th, December, and get approval of administration representative / SM.
- SM should prepare “Monthly Training & Drill Plan & Record (Form NP02-03)” and get approval of Site Manager/SM on 5days before the scheduled day in “Yearly Site Training & Drill Plan (Form NP02-02)”.
- Appointed instructor shall prepare educational data.

#### 1.2.3 Implementation Method of Education {#np-02-1-2-3-implementation-method-education}

- Self-education of company
- Entrusted education to outside institution

#### 1.2.4 Implementation, Evaluation, Record & Issuance of Certificate of Self-Education {#np-02-1-2-4-implementation-evaluation-record-self-education}

- The education shall be conducted in accordance with “Monthly Training & Drill Plan & Record” under control of SM.
- HSE Officer shall record the contents of education and results of evaluation in “(Form NP02-04a Training & Drill report)”.
- Re-education shall be conducted when the result of evaluation is indicating dissatisfaction.
- SM shall record in Form “NP02-06 Management List of Onsite Worker's Document” for Site personnel to manage all education completed by Site personnel.

#### 1.2.5 Implementation, Evaluation, Record and Issuance of Certificate of Entrusted Education {#np-02-1-2-5-implementation-evaluation-record-entrusted-education}

- Entrusted education shall be implemented under the control of HRGA.
- Certificate of completion of a course of the institution which carried out concerned education shall be substituted for evaluation.
- HRGA shall record the contents of education and fact of completion in “Training and drill report (NP02-04a)”.

#### 1.2.6 Guideline of Evaluation for In-house Education {#np-02-1-2-6-guideline-evaluation-inhouse-education}

- The estimation shall be done by written answer, oral statement, assignment, observation and shall adopt one of (observation + oral statement), (observation + written answer) and (observation + assignment).
- Estimation of education shall be classified into A (Satisfied, over 80 points), B (Ordinary 69-79 points), C (Unsatisfied, lower than 60 points), and should be reeducated unless scored B or higher.

## 2. On Site Training and Emergency Drill {#np-02-2-onsite-training-emergency-drill}

### 2.1 Emergency Drill Procedures {#np-02-2-1-emergency-drill-procedures}

#### 2.1.1 Types of Emergency Drills and Exercises {#np-02-2-1-1-types-emergency-drills}

- Tabletop Exercises (TTX)
- Functional Exercises (FE)
- Full-Scale Exercises (FSE) / Drills

#### 2.1.2 Specific Schedule and Frequency of Drills {#np-02-2-1-2-specific-schedule-frequency}

The frequency and scheduling of emergency drills at LNG regasification terminals are dictated by regulatory requirements, industry best practices, and the need to maintain a constant state of readiness. Regular drills are essential for ensuring that personnel are proficient in their roles and that emergency procedures are effective and up-to-date. Personnel in positions like Incident Commander are often required to function in their role during an operational incident, planned event, exercise, drill, or simulation at least once every three years to maintain currency.

#### 2.1.3 PIC Roles and Responsibilities {#np-02-2-1-3-pic-roles-responsibilities}

| Position | Responsibilities during Emergency Drill |
| --- | --- |
| Site Manager | As Incident Commander; overall coordination, communication with external parties (firefighters, ambulances), shutdown/restart authorization. |
| Ops & Main Team Leader | Assist Site Manager; lead technical team for equipment isolation, procedural shutdown coordination. |
| Operator | Perform unit shutdown according to procedures; monitor system parameters from the control room (DCS); assist with equipment isolation in the field. |
| Mechanical & Electrical | Energy isolation (electrical, pneumatic); assist critical valve operation; support emergency repairs. |
| SSHQE Officer | Monitor the area with gas detectors; ensure evacuation routes are clear; record all incidents and personnel; coordinate first aid. |
| Logistik Staff | Control logistics access; ensure ISO tank loading and unloading areas are secure and locked. |
| Admin HR-GA | Assist HSE Officer in recording and counting personnel at the assembly point. |
| Security | Control access in/out of the facility; direct traffic for firefighters/ambulances; maintain a security perimeter. |

### 2.2 Drill Planning and Execution Methodology {#np-02-2-2-drill-planning-execution-methodology}

#### 2.2.1 Pre-Drill Planning and Scenario Development {#np-02-2-2-1-predrill-planning-scenario-development}

- Goal and Objective Definition: Create goals by determining what capabilities are to be evaluated and why.
- Scenario Development: Create scenario based on credible potential emergency scenarios identified in ERP and Safety Case.
- Resource Identification: Identify resource gaps in personnel, facilities, equipment, and systems.
- Stakeholder Engagement: Engage with stakeholders, government authorities, and first responders.
- Documentation Review: Review operational plans, policies, and procedures.

#### 2.2.2 Drill Execution and Realism {#np-02-2-2-2-drill-execution-realism}

- Activation and Notification: Simulated detection of emergency and activation of emergency alarms and notification procedures.
- Incident Management System Activation: Incident Command System (ICS) activated.
- Tactical Response: Field Response Teams (FRT) or First Intervention Teams (FIT) execute tactical objectives.
- Personnel Actions: Follow general actions and specific checklists with building wardens ensuring muster.
- External Coordination: Procedures for notifying local emergency services and initiating unified management.
- Safety Protocols: Responders strictly observe safety protocols including PPE use and safe routes.

#### 2.2.3 Post-Drill Evaluation and Reporting {#np-02-2-2-3-postdrill-evaluation-reporting}

- Observation and Documentation: Meticulously record observations and deficiencies within 24 hours.
- Debriefing and Hot Wash: Immediate debriefings conducted to capture initial observations.
- Root Cause Analysis: Perform root cause analysis for identified deficiencies or failures.
- Action Plan Development: Time-bound action plan prepared for rectifications.
- Report Submission: Completed Standard Inspection Report or Post Inspection Memorandum (PIM) submitted to regulatory authorities within 30-60 days.
- Plan Updates: Response plans reviewed and updated at intervals not exceeding 27 months, but at least once every 2 calendar years.

## 4. Emergency Drill Scenarios (Appendices) {#np-02-4-emergency-drill-scenarios}

### 4.1 Scenario 1: DCS & ESD System Failure {#np-02-4-1-scenario-dcs-esd-failure}

- Title: Total DCS/ESD Failure During Normal Operation with Pressure Build-up
- Drill Type: Full-scale functional exercise with manual response
- Affected Systems: Distributed Control System (DCS) and Emergency Shutdown System (ESD)
- Location: Entire Facility - Control Room and Process Area
- Timeline & Steps:
  - Phase 1: Initial Failure Detection (T+0 to T+5 min) - DCS screen blank at T+0, ESD system fault at T+1:15, Pressure buffer tank rising at 85% MAWP at T+2:30, Audible alarm at T+3:45.
  - Phase 2: Manual Emergency Response Activation (T+5 to T+15 min) - Emergency declared when pressure reaches 90% MAWP at T+5:00. Stop LNG pump and isolate buffer tank manually.
  - Phase 3: Manual Process Stabilization (T+15 to T+25 min) - Buffer tank pressure down to 75% MAWP at T+15:00.
  - Phase 4: System Recovery & Restart (T+25 to T+40 min) - Restart DCS/ESD system.
  - Phase 5: Evaluation & Lessons Learned (T+40 to T+50 min) - Hot wash debrief and initial incident report.

### 4.2 Scenario 2: Total Electrical Blackout with Emergency Genset Failure {#np-02-4-2-scenario-blackout-genset-failure}

- Title: Total Grid Power Blackout with Genset Failure and Pressure Build-up on LNG System
- Drill Type: Full-scale functional exercise with manual system response
- Affected Systems: Entire electrical, control, and safety systems
- Location: Entire Facility - Main Switchroom to Process Area
- Timeline & Steps:
  - Phase 1: Blackout & Initial Detection (T+0 to T+5 min) - Total blackout at T+0, Genset failed to auto-start (battery low & fuel valve stuck) at T+0:45, Buffer tank pressure 75% MAWP at T+1:30.
  - Phase 2: Manual Response & Stabilization (T+5 to T+20 min) - Declare blackout emergency at T+5:00.
  - Phase 3: System Stabilization & Contingency (T+20 to T+40 min) - Emergency genset manually started at T+20:00, pressure stabilized at 65% MAWP at T+26:00.
  - Phase 4: System Recovery & Restart (T+40 to T+60 min) - Grid power restored from PLN at T+40:00.
  - Phase 5: Evaluation & Lessons Learned (T+60 to T+75 min) - Hot wash debrief and RCA initiation.

### 4.3 Scenario 3: Main Electrical Panel Fire with Arc Flash Victims {#np-02-4-3-scenario-electrical-fire-arc-flash}

- Title: Arc Flash in Main Switchroom Causing Electrical Fire and Multiple Burn Victims
- Drill Type: Full-scale functional exercise with electrical fire response and first aid
- Location: Main Switchroom and Surrounding Area
- Key Thresholds & Response: Arc flash explosion, remote shutdown failed, manual electrical isolation with full arc flash PPE required before firefighting with CO2/clean agent (NO water/foam). Cool burns with sterile saline for 10-15 minutes. Medical notification for victim with 20% BSA burns and potential inhalation injury.

### 4.4 Scenario 4: BOG Compressor Fire with Thermal Burn Victims {#np-02-4-4-scenario-bog-compressor-fire}

- Title: BOG Compressor Mechanical Seal Fire with Flash Fire and Multiple Thermal Burn Victims
- Drill Type: Full-scale functional exercise with hydrocarbon fire response and medical
- Location: BOG Compressor Area
- Key Thresholds & Response: Gas methane detected at 40% LEL, flash fire from static electricity. Activate ESD and fixed fire suppression. Apply foam for fire suppression. Cool thermal burns with sterile saline for 15-20 minutes, perform Rule of Nines burn assessment. Fluid resuscitation prepared for burns >15% BSA.

### 4.5 Scenario 5: Major LNG Leakage with Cryogenic Exposure Victims {#np-02-4-5-scenario-lng-leakage-cryogenic}

- Title: 4 Inch LNG Transfer Line Rupture with Major Spill and Multiple Cryogenic Exposure Victims
- Drill Type: Full-scale functional exercise with cryogenic medical response
- Location: LNG Transfer Area between Storage Tank and Buffer Tank
- Key Thresholds & Response: Gas detector reaches 60% LEL. Activate ESD and water curtain system. Rescue team with SCBA. Flush cryogenic burns with warm water (40-45°C) for 15-20 minutes. DO NOT rub or massage frozen skin. 100% oxygen for inhalation injury. Thermal blankets for hypothermia.

### 4.6 Scenario 6: Intrusion and Facility Sabotage {#np-02-4-6-scenario-intrusion-sabotage}

- Title: Unauthorized Personnel Intrusion with Attempted Sabotage on Main Control System
- Drill Type: Full-scale functional exercise with security response and crisis management
- Location: Perimeter, Control Room, Process Area
- Response: Perimeter breach east sector (3 intruders). Activate total lockdown, request SWAT and police, remote plant shutdown, roll call and personnel accountability, SWAT rescue operation for hostages.

### 4.7 Scenario 7: Pressure System Failure {#np-02-4-7-scenario-pressure-system-failure}

- Title: Cascading Pressure System Failure During BOG Compressor Trip with PSV Operation
- Drill Type: Full-scale functional exercise on pressure control and emergency isolation
- Primary Hazards: Overpressure events, equipment rupture potential, hydrocarbon release
- Key Thresholds & Response: Buffer tank pressure at 85% MAWP, PCV-101 failure, PSV-201 chattering at setpoint, high-high pressure alarm. Full plant shutdown (ESD-1) activated, rupture disk operates to flare/atmosphere. Isolate manual block valves around buffer tank.

### 4.8 Scenario 8: Rotating Equipment Failure {#np-02-4-8-scenario-rotating-equipment-failure}

- Title: BOG Compressor Bearing Failure Causing Severe Vibration and Minor Fire
- Drill Type: Full-scale functional exercise with medical response
- Location: BOG Compressor Plant Area
- Key Response: Grinding noise and severe vibration, automatic trip failed -> execute manual trip from DCS. LOTO motor compressor, cool thermal burn from hot oil spray with normal temperature running water for 10-15 minutes.

### 4.9 Scenario 9: ISO Tank Truck Transport Accident {#np-02-4-9-scenario-iso-tank-truck-failure}

- Title: ISO Tank Truck Accident on Public Access Road near Facility with Major LNG Release
- Drill Type: Full-scale functional exercise with external coordination
- Location: Facility Access Road (500 meters from main gate)
- Response: Safety perimeter established (500m / 300m resident evacuation). Notify Fire Department, Police, Hospital. Rescue driver using SCBA and spinal precaution. Set up water curtain for vapor cloud dispersion.

### 4.10 Scenario 10: ISO Tank Yard Unconscious Worker {#np-02-4-10-scenario-iso-tank-yard-unconscious}

- Title: Unconscious Worker on ISO Tank Yard
- Location: ISO Tank Yard
- Response: First aid by HSE/Paramedic, verify breathing and pulse, evacuate via ambulance to nearby health center.

### 4.11 Scenario 11: CCR Electrical Panel Fire {#np-02-4-11-scenario-ccr-electrical-panel-fire}

- Title: Fire at Electrical Panel in CCR (Electrical Room)
- Location: CCR (Electrical Room)
- Response: Extinguish with portable fire extinguisher, activate Emergency Response Procedure, ring alarm, muster at Assembly Point, POB count by Security.

### 4.12 Scenario 12: Area Process Evacuation Due to LNG Leak {#np-02-4-12-scenario-area-process-lng-leak}

- Title: Evacuation (Mustering) Due to LNG Leak in AAV Area
- Location: AAV Area / Process Area
- Response: Inform CCR, activate Emergency Response Procedure, evacuation to Assembly Point, POB counting, don Cryogenic Suits for leak handling.
